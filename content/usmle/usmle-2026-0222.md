## 문제
A 72-year-old woman is evaluated on the third day of hospitalization. She was admitted after the sudden onset of weakness of the right hand and difficulty finding words while gardening; she arrived 6 hours after symptom onset and did not receive thrombolysis. Her symptoms have mostly resolved, and she now has mild clumsiness of the right hand and occasional word-finding pauses. She has hypertension treated with lisinopril and has no history of bleeding, falls or heart disease. She does not drink alcohol. Her vital signs are shown. Neurologic examination shows mild weakness of the right hand intrinsic muscles and mildly nonfluent speech with intact comprehension. MRI of the brain shows a 1.5-cm acute infarct in the left frontal cortex without hemorrhage and two small chronic cortical infarcts in the right parietal lobe. Duplex ultrasonography shows 30% stenosis of the left internal carotid artery and 20% stenosis of the right. Transthoracic echocardiography shows a left atrial diameter of 4.6 cm, thin mobile mitral leaflets without stenosis, no prosthetic valves and an ejection fraction of 60%. Laboratory results are shown. Continuous telemetry since admission has shown the rhythm in the ECG; she has never been told of a heart rhythm problem. Which of the following is the most appropriate long-term pharmacotherapy to prevent recurrent stroke?

- A. Aspirin plus clopidogrel for 21 days
- B. Warfarin, target INR 2.0 to 3.0
- C. Apixaban 5 mg twice daily
- D. Aspirin plus extended-release dipyridamole
- E. Clopidogrel 75 mg daily

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: C

- **정답근거**: 3단계 추론. ① 심전도: P파 없음 + 불규칙하게 불규칙한 RR → **심방세동**. ② 여러 혈관 영역의 피질 경색 + 경동맥 협착 30 % → **심장 색전성 뇌졸중 → 경구 항응고제**. ③ 승모판 협착·기계 판막 없음 + 신기능 정상 → **비판막성 → 와파린보다 DOAC(아픽사반)**.
- **오답감별**:
  - (A) 21일 이중 항혈소판은 색전원이 없는 경증 비심인성 뇌졸중·고위험 TIA 의 치료다 — 심방세동 색전에는 항응고제만 못하다.
  - (B) 와파린도 색전을 막지만 비판막성 심방세동에서는 DOAC 가 두개내 출혈이 적어 우선이다 — 와파린이 필요한 판막 조건이 없다.
  - (D) 아스피린+서방형 디피리다몰은 비심인성 뇌졸중의 항혈소판 선택지다 — 심방세동에는 항응고제를 대신하지 못한다.
  - (E) 클로피도그렐 단독도 비심인성 뇌졸중의 항혈소판 선택지일 뿐 심장 색전 예방에는 부족하다.
- **임상핵심**: **심방세동 + 허혈성 뇌졸중이면 항혈소판이 아니라 항응고 — 승모판 협착·기계 판막이 없으면 와파린보다 DOAC.**
- **출처**: AHA/ASA 2021 secondary stroke prevention guideline (Stroke 2021); 2023 ACC/AHA/ACCP/HRS AF guideline (Circulation 2024).
