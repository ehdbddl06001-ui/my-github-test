## 문제
A 52-year-old woman comes to the emergency department because of a throbbing left-sided headache that began 7 hours ago and built up over about an hour. She has nausea, has vomited three times and finds light and noise unbearable. She has had similar headaches two or three times a year since her 20s; they usually resolve after she sleeps, but this one has not. She took ibuprofen 3 hours ago and vomited shortly afterward. Two years ago, she underwent placement of a drug-eluting stent in the left anterior descending artery for exertional angina; she has had no chest pain since. Her medications are aspirin, atorvastatin and metoprolol. Her vital signs are shown. She is lying still in a darkened room with her eyes closed. The neck is supple. Funduscopic examination shows sharp optic disc margins. Cranial nerves, strength, sensation, reflexes and coordination are normal, and her gait is normal. An ECG shows sinus rhythm with no ST-segment changes. Laboratory results are shown. Which of the following is the most appropriate pharmacotherapy?

- A. Subcutaneous sumatriptan
- B. Intravenous morphine
- C. Intravenous dihydroergotamine
- D. Oral butalbital-acetaminophen-caffeine
- E. Intravenous metoclopramide

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: E

- **정답근거**: 3단계 추론. ① 1시간에 걸쳐 시작 + 20대부터 같은 양상 반복 + 경부 강직 없음·정상 유두·정상 신경 진찰 → **평소의 편두통 발작**. ② 관상동맥 스텐트(관상동맥 질환) → **트립탄·맥각 금기**. ③ 구토로 경구 약 실패 + 아편유사제·butalbital 은 권고되지 않음 → **정맥 metoclopramide**(구역과 두통을 함께 줄인다).
- **오답감별**:
  - (A) sumatriptan 은 5-HT1B 작용으로 관상동맥을 수축시켜 관상동맥 질환에서 금기다.
  - (B) morphine 은 편두통에서 도파민 길항제보다 효과가 떨어지고 재방문·약물 과용 두통을 늘려 1차로 쓰지 않는다.
  - (C) dihydroergotamine 은 강한 혈관 수축 작용이 있어 관상동맥 질환에서 금기다.
  - (D) butalbital 복합제는 경구라 구토 중에 맞지 않고, 약물 과용 두통·의존 위험으로 권하지 않는다.
- **임상핵심**: **편두통 + 관상동맥·뇌혈관 질환 → 트립탄·맥각 금기 → 정맥 도파민 길항제(metoclopramide·prochlorperazine). 아편유사제는 피한다.**
- **출처**: AHS evidence assessment of parenteral migraine therapies(Headache 2016); AHS consensus statement(Headache 2021); Harrison's 21e(Migraine).
