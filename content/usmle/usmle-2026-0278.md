## 문제
A 68-year-old man comes to the emergency department because of 2 days of diffuse muscle aching and weakness and dark brown urine since this morning. Six days ago, he began a 10-day course of clarithromycin for community-acquired pneumonia; his cough has improved. He has hyperlipidemia, hypertension, and coronary artery disease. He has taken simvastatin 40 mg daily, amlodipine, and aspirin for 4 years without muscle symptoms; his serum creatine kinase activity and creatinine concentration 6 months ago were normal. He does not drink alcohol and has not exercised strenuously. His vital signs are shown. The thigh and upper arm muscles are tender to palpation, and strength is 4/5 in the proximal muscles of all four extremities. Deep tendon reflexes are normal. There is no rash. Laboratory results are shown. A urine dipstick is strongly positive for blood; microscopy shows 0–2 RBC/hpf. Which of the following is the most likely mechanism of this patient's muscle injury?

- A. Autoantibodies against HMG-CoA reductase in skeletal muscle
- B. Decreased renal excretion of the statin from kidney injury
- C. Displacement of the statin from plasma albumin binding sites
- D. Inhibition of hepatic CYP3A4-mediated metabolism of the statin
- E. Direct mitochondrial toxicity of the antibiotic in muscle fibers

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: D

- **정답근거**: 3단계. ① CK 18,400 + 적혈구 없는 잠혈 강양성 → **횡문근융해**. ② 4년 안정 simvastatin + 6일 전 clarithromycin → 새 약물 상호작용. ③ clarithromycin 의 **CYP3A4 억제** → simvastatin 농도↑ → 근독성.
- **오답감별**:
  - (A) 항-HMGCR 자가면역 근병증은 수주~수개월에 걸쳐 진행한다 — 새 약 시작 6일 만의 발병과 맞지 않는다.
  - (B) simvastatin 은 간에서 제거된다 — 크레아티닌 상승은 횡문근융해의 결과다.
  - (C) 단백 결합 치환은 유리 분율을 잠깐 바꿀 뿐 이런 독성의 주된 기전이 아니다.
  - (E) 마크롤라이드는 그 자체로 근독성 약이 아니다 — 스타틴 농도를 올려 간접적으로 해친다.
- **임상핵심**: **CYP3A4 로 대사되는 스타틴(simvastatin·lovastatin·atorvastatin) + 강력한 CYP3A4 억제제(clarithromycin·itraconazole·HIV 단백분해효소 억제제) = 횡문근융해 위험** — 병용 중엔 스타틴을 쉬거나 pravastatin·rosuvastatin 처럼 CYP3A4 비의존 약으로 바꾼다.
