## 문제
A 31-year-old woman is brought to the emergency department by her coworkers because of sweating, tremor and confusion that began during her shift as an intensive care unit nurse. Blood was drawn before intravenous dextrose was given, and her symptoms resolved within minutes of the infusion. This is her third similar episode in 2 months; each time her glucose was below 40 mg/dL. She does not have diabetes; her younger sister has type 1 diabetes and uses an insulin pump. She has had four emergency department visits in the past year for recurrent skin abscesses on her thighs that grew mixed bowel flora. She says she has never injected insulin. She asks to be discharged quickly so she will not miss her next shift; she has declined offers of medical leave and has no pending disability, insurance or legal claims. She appears calm and cooperative now. Her vital signs are shown. There are several healed puncture marks on the lower abdomen. Laboratory results are shown. Which of the following is the most likely diagnosis?

- A. Factitious disorder imposed on self
- B. Malingering
- C. Functional neurological symptom disorder
- D. Somatic symptom disorder
- E. Illness anxiety disorder

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: A

- **정답근거**: 3단계 추론. ① 저혈당 중 인슐린 98 + C-펩타이드 0.1·프로인슐린 억제 + 설폰요소제 음성 → **외인성 인슐린**. ② 사용을 부인 + 주사 자국·장내 세균 농양 반복 → 증상을 **의도적으로 만들고 숨긴다**(비의도적 장애가 아님). ③ 병가 거절·청구 없음·근무 복귀 희망 → **외적 보상 없음 → 인위성 장애**(꾀병 아님).
- **오답감별**:
  - (B) 꾀병도 의도적이지만 외적 보상이 목적이다 — 병가를 거절했고 청구가 없다.
  - (C) 기능성 신경학적 증상 장애는 비의도적 신경 증상이고 검사로 확인되는 실제 저혈당을 만들지 않는다.
  - (D) 신체 증상 장애는 증상에 대한 과도한 생각·불안이 핵심이며 의도적으로 징후를 만들지 않는다.
  - (E) 질병 불안 장애는 증상이 없거나 경미한데 심각한 병을 걱정하는 것 — 이 환자는 실제 저혈당을 만들고 부인한다.
- **임상핵심**: **의도적으로 만든 징후 + 외적 보상 없음 → 인위성 장애 · 외적 보상 있음 → 꾀병 · 비의도적 → 신체 증상 관련 장애.**
