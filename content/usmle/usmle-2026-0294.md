## 문제
A 68-year-old man is brought to the emergency department 10 hours after the sudden onset of severe dizziness while he was watching television. He describes the room spinning constantly; the sensation is worse when he moves his head but has not stopped at any time, even while lying still. He has vomited six times. He has had no headache, neck pain, hearing loss, tinnitus, ear fullness, double vision, or difficulty speaking or swallowing. He had no recent cold or flu-like illness and has never had a similar episode. He has hypertension and type 2 diabetes mellitus and has smoked one pack of cigarettes daily for 45 years. His vital signs are shown. He is alert and his speech is normal. There is horizontal nystagmus that beats to the left on left gaze and to the right on right gaze. When his head is turned rapidly to each side while he looks at the examiner's nose, his eyes stay on target with no corrective eye movement. Facial movements and sensation are symmetric. Hearing is normal to finger rub in both ears. Strength and sensation are normal in all extremities, and finger-to-nose testing is normal on both sides. He cannot sit upright on the stretcher without holding on and cannot stand. Laboratory results are shown. A noncontrast CT of the head shows no abnormalities. Which of the following is the most likely diagnosis?

- A. Benign paroxysmal positional vertigo
- B. Vestibular neuritis
- C. Cerebellar infarction
- D. Ménière disease
- E. Vestibular migraine

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: C

- **정답근거**: 3단계. ① 갑자기 시작해 **10시간 쉬지 않는** 회전성 어지럼·구토·안진, 청력 정상 → **급성 전정 증후군**. ② **머리 충동 검사 정상** + **주시 방향에 따라 바뀌는 안진** + **붙잡지 않고 앉지 못하는 체간 운동실조** → 중추성. ③ 고혈압·당뇨·흡연 + CT 정상(후두와 경색에 둔감) → **소뇌 경색** — 다음 검사는 확산강조 MRI.
- **오답감별**:
  - (A) 양성 돌발성 두위현훈은 머리 위치를 바꿀 때 수 초~1분만 생긴다 — 누워 있어도 10시간 계속된다.
  - (B) 전정신경염은 머리 충동 검사가 비정상(교정 단속운동)이고 안진이 한 방향으로만 뛴다 — 둘 다 반대다.
  - (D) 메니에르병은 청력 저하·이명·귀 먹먹함을 동반한 수 시간짜리 발작이 반복된다 — 청력 정상, 첫 발작이다.
  - (E) 전정 편두통은 편두통 병력·두통·빛 공포가 단서이고 앉지 못하는 운동실조와 맞지 않는다.
- **임상핵심**: **급성 전정 증후군에서 정상 머리 충동 검사 · 방향이 바뀌는 안진 · 사위 중 하나라도 있으면 중추(뇌졸중)** — 사지 협조 검사 정상·CT 정상은 소뇌(충부) 경색을 배제하지 못하니 확산강조 MRI 를 한다.
- **출처**: Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine, 22e. 24장 Dizziness and Vertigo; 437장 Introduction to Cerebrovascular Diseases.
