## 문제
A 31-year-old man comes to the physician because of fever, joint pain and a rash for 2 days. He was diagnosed with severe aplastic anemia 3 weeks ago. Ten days ago he began treatment with equine antithymocyte globulin, given intravenously once daily for 4 days, together with oral cyclosporine. During the first infusion he had chills and a fever that resolved within 3 hours; the remaining infusions were uneventful. He now has aching in his knees, wrists and fingers and a mildly itchy rash on his hands and feet. He has had no wheezing, lip swelling or lightheadedness. He takes no other medications. Examination shows erythematous, partly purpuric macules and wheals in a band along the sides of both hands and feet where the palmar and plantar skin meets the dorsal skin. Both wrists and knees are tender with small effusions. There are tender, mobile 1-cm lymph nodes in both axillae. The lungs are clear to auscultation. His vital signs are shown. Laboratory results are shown. Which of the following is the most likely mechanism of this patient's current condition?

- A. Antigen-specific IgE cross-linked on mast cells, triggering rapid degranulation
- B. Drug-specific CD8+ T cells killing keratinocytes and synovial lining cells
- C. Antigen–antibody complexes deposited in vessel walls, activating complement
- D. Cytokine release from lymphocytes lysed by the infused antibody preparation
- E. IgG bound to equine protein adsorbed onto platelets, causing their clearance

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: C

- **정답근거**: 2단계 추론. ① 말 단백(equine antithymocyte globulin) 시작 10일 뒤 발열·다발 관절통·손발 가장자리 띠 모양 발진·림프절 비대·단백뇨 → **혈청병**. ② 숙주 IgG 가 생기는 7–14일 무렵 혈중에 남은 항원과 항원 과잉 상태의 작은 면역 복합체를 만들어 혈관벽·관절막·사구체에 쌓이고 보체를 활성화한다 — C3·C4 저하가 그 흔적이다(**제3형 과민반응**).
- **오답감별**:
  - (A) 제1형은 감작된 사람에서 노출 수 분–수 시간 안의 두드러기·천명·저혈압이다. 첫 노출 10일 뒤이고 천명·혈관부종·어지럼이 없으며 보체 저하·단백뇨를 설명하지 못한다.
  - (B) 제4형(약물 특이 T 세포)은 피부 박리·점막 침범이나 장기 침범(간염 등)으로 나타나고 보체를 소모하지 않는다.
  - (D) 사이토카인 방출은 첫 주입 때의 오한·발열로 3시간 안에 끝났다 — 지금 증상과 시점이 다르다.
  - (E) 제2형 면역성 혈소판 파괴를 떠올릴 수 있지만 혈소판 감소는 재생불량빈혈 자체로 이미 있었고, 관절통·발진·단백뇨·보체 저하를 설명하지 못한다.
- **임상핵심**: **이종 단백·키메라 항체 7–14일 뒤 발열 + 관절통 + 발진 + 단백뇨 + C3·C4 저하 = 혈청병(제3형, 면역 복합체).**
- **출처**: Robbins & Cotran 10e(Type III hypersensitivity); Harrison's 21e(Aplastic anemia — antithymocyte globulin).
