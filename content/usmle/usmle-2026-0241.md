## 문제
A 26-year-old woman comes to the physician for a follow-up visit 4 weeks after starting fluoxetine 20 mg daily. For the past 2 years, she has had intrusive thoughts that she will contaminate her family with germs. She recognizes that these fears are excessive but feels intense anxiety until she washes her hands, which she does for about 3 hours each day; her hands are now cracked and bleeding. She left her job as a pharmacy technician 2 months ago because she could not keep up with her work. She reports no change in these symptoms since starting the medication. She has not had nausea, insomnia or sexual dysfunction and takes the medication every day. She has no history of tics, mania or psychosis, and she does not drink alcohol or use other substances. She is on a waiting list for exposure and response prevention therapy. Her vital signs are shown. Mental status examination shows an anxious affect; her thought process is linear, and she has no suicidal ideation. Which of the following is the most appropriate next step in pharmacotherapy?

- A. Add risperidone
- B. Increase the fluoxetine dose
- C. Switch to clomipramine
- D. Add clonazepam
- E. Switch to bupropion

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: B

- **정답근거**: 3단계 추론. ① 오염 침투 사고 + 하루 3시간 손 씻기 + 지나침을 앎 → **병식이 좋은 강박장애**. ② 플루옥세틴 20 mg 4주 무반응 → 강박장애에는 **아직 충분한 시도가 아니다**(고용량·8–12주). ③ 부작용 없음·매일 복용 → **플루옥세틴 증량·유지**(노출·반응방지 병행).
- **오답감별**:
  - (A) 항정신병약 증강은 최대 내약 용량 SSRI 를 충분히 쓴 뒤에도 부분 반응일 때다 — 시작 용량 4주로는 치료 저항이라 할 수 없다.
  - (C) 클로미프라민은 효과가 있지만 항콜린·심전도·발작 부작용이 커서 SSRI 를 충분히 쓴 뒤의 선택이다.
  - (D) 클로나제팜은 불안을 잠시 줄여도 강박 사고·행동을 줄이지 못하고 의존 위험이 있다.
  - (E) 부프로피온은 세로토닌 재흡수를 막지 않아 강박장애에 효과 근거가 없다.
- **임상핵심**: **강박장애의 SSRI 는 고용량·8–12주가 충분한 시도 — 시작 용량 4주 무반응·부작용 없음이면 증량이 먼저, 증강·교체는 그 뒤.**
- **출처**: APA Practice Guideline for the Treatment of Patients With Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder(2007)·Guideline Watch(2013). Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine, 22e. 463장 Psychiatric Disorders.
