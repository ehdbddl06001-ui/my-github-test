## 문제
A 2-month-old girl is brought to the emergency department because of coughing spells for 10 days. The cough began with a runny nose and has become worse; during the spells she coughs many times without catching her breath, her face turns blue, and the spells often end with vomiting. Between spells she feeds and appears comfortable. She has not had fever. She has not yet received any immunizations; her 2-month visit is scheduled for next week. Her 9-year-old brother has had a cough for 3 weeks. Her vital signs are shown; oxygen saturation is 97% on room air between spells. During the examination, she has a coughing spell with perioral cyanosis lasting 40 seconds. There are petechiae over the forehead. The lungs are clear between spells. Laboratory results are shown. A chest x-ray shows no infiltrates. The marked increase in this patient's leukocyte count is most likely caused by which of the following actions of a bacterial toxin?

- A. ADP-ribosylation of Gi proteins, blocking chemokine signals for lymphocytes to leave the blood
- B. ADP-ribosylation of Gs proteins, locking adenylate cyclase in the active state
- C. Calmodulin-activated adenylate cyclase entering phagocytes and raising their cAMP
- D. Cleavage of 28S ribosomal RNA in the 60S subunit, halting host protein synthesis
- E. Cross-linking of MHC class II molecules with T-cell receptors, driving polyclonal T-cell proliferation

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: A

- **정답근거**: 3단계. ① 예방접종 전 영아 + 발작성 기침·청색증·기침 뒤 구토 + 무열 + 형 3주 기침 → **백일해**. ② 백혈구 42,000 · 작고 성숙한 림프구 · 폐 깨끗 → **림프구 재분포**(증식·감염 반응 아님). ③ 백일해 독소 → **Gi ADP-리보실화 → 케모카인 수용체 신호 차단** → 림프구가 혈중에 머묾.
- **오답감별**:
  - (B) Gs ADP-리보실화는 콜레라·장독소성 대장균 이열성 독소다 — 백일해 독소는 Gi 를 표적으로 한다.
  - (C) 백일해의 adenylate cyclase toxin 은 실제 독력 인자로 식세포의 살균을 떨어뜨리지만, 림프구 증가를 일으키지는 않는다.
  - (D) 28S rRNA 절단은 시가 독소·시가 유사 독소의 기전이다 — 출혈성 설사·용혈성 요독 증후군과 연결된다.
  - (E) 초항원은 다클론 T 세포 증식과 독소 쇼크(고열·저혈압)를 일으킨다 — 열이 없고 림프구가 성숙해 증식과 맞지 않는다.
- **임상핵심**: **백일해 영아의 극심한 림프구 증가 = 백일해 독소의 Gi ADP-리보실화 → 림프구 귀소 차단(재분포). 콜레라 = Gs.**
- **출처**: Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine, 22e. 165장 Pertussis and Other Bordetella Infections.
