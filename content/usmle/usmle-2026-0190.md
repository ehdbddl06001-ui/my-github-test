## 문제
A 62-year-old woman develops shortness of breath and bluish discoloration of the lips 30 minutes after outpatient upper endoscopy for dyspepsia. Before the procedure, her oropharynx was anesthetized with several sprays of topical benzocaine because she had a strong gag reflex, and she received intravenous midazolam. The procedure was uneventful and lasted 10 minutes. She has hypertension treated with amlodipine and has never smoked. She is alert and fully oriented. The lips and nail beds are slate-gray to blue. The lungs are clear to auscultation with symmetric air entry. Heart sounds are regular without murmurs. There is no calf swelling or tenderness. A portable chest x-ray shows clear lungs and a normal cardiac silhouette. Pulse oximetry read 86% on room air; after 15 minutes of 100% oxygen by nonrebreather mask, it still reads 85%, and the reading is the same with a probe on the earlobe. An arterial blood sample is obtained while she breathes 100% oxygen. Her vital signs are shown. Laboratory studies are shown. Which of the following is the most appropriate next step to establish the cause of this patient's low oxygen saturation?

- A. CT angiography of the pulmonary arteries
- B. Transthoracic echocardiography with agitated saline
- C. Ventilation-perfusion lung scintigraphy
- D. Spirometry with diffusing capacity measurement
- E. Multiple-wavelength co-oximetry of the arterial sample

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: E

- **정답근거**: 3단계 추론. ① 100% 산소에도 SpO2 85% 그대로, 귀 탐침도 같음 → 실제로 산소에 반응하지 않는 저포화. ② 같은 시점 **PO2 486 mm Hg** + 맑은 폐 → 단락·V/Q 불균형·폐색전증으로 설명할 수 없다(**포화도 간극**). ③ benzocaine 분무 + 회색-청색 청색증, 혈액가스의 SaO2 100% 는 PO2 에서 계산한 값 → 이상 헤모글로빈(메트헤모글로빈) → **co-oximetry** 로 분율을 직접 측정.
- **오답감별**:
  - (A) 폐색전증이 포화도를 이만큼 떨어뜨리면 PO2 가 낮아야 한다. PO2 486 에서는 설명이 안 된다.
  - (B) 우좌 단락은 산소 불응성 저산소혈증, 즉 낮은 PO2 를 만든다. PO2 가 높으니 맞지 않는다.
  - (C) V/Q 불균형은 100% 산소에 잘 반응해 포화도가 오른다 — 85% 에 묶인 포화도와 맞지 않는다.
  - (D) 확산 장애도 100% 산소로 교정되고, 급성 시술 직후의 원인을 찾는 검사가 아니다.
- **임상핵심**: **높은 PO2 + SpO2 ≈ 85% 고정 = 포화도 간극 → 메트헤모글로빈혈증 → co-oximetry(혈액가스의 계산 SaO2 는 믿지 않는다).**
- **출처**: Harrison 21e(Hypoxia and Cyanosis; Disorders of Hemoglobin); FDA Drug Safety Communication(benzocaine, 2018).
