## 문제
A 14-month-old boy is brought to the emergency department by his parents 15 minutes after he became deeply blue and began breathing rapidly during a long crying fit on waking from a nap. A heart murmur was noted at 2 months of age; the family missed the scheduled cardiology follow-up after moving. He has had no fever, cough or wheezing, and he fed well this morning. His vital signs are shown. He is irritable and crying, with deep cyanosis of the lips and nail beds. There is no nasal flaring or grunting. The lungs are clear to auscultation. A grade 2/6 systolic ejection murmur is heard at the left upper sternal border; at the clinic visit 3 months ago it was recorded as grade 4/6. The liver edge is palpable 1 cm below the right costal margin. Capillary refill time is 2 seconds. Which of the following is the most appropriate initial step in management?

- A. Intravenous furosemide
- B. Nebulized albuterol
- C. Intravenous dobutamine
- D. Knee-to-chest positioning
- E. Intravenous adenosine

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: D

- **정답근거**: 3단계 추론. ① 영아기 심잡음 + 평소 청색증 + 좌상부 흉골연 박출 잡음 → **교정 안 된 팔로 4징**. ② 울음 뒤 SpO2 66% + 과호흡 + 잡음이 작아짐 + 폐 맑음 → **과청색증 발작(폐 혈류 감소)**. ③ 첫 처치 → **무릎-가슴 자세**로 전신 혈관 저항을 올려 우→좌 단락을 줄인다.
- **오답감별**:
  - (A) furosemide 는 폐혈류 과다·심부전의 처치 — 폐가 맑고 간 비대가 없으며 전부하를 줄이면 단락이 악화될 수 있다.
  - (B) albuterol 은 기관지 연축 처치 — 천명이 없고 폐가 맑다; β 작용이 빈맥을 더한다.
  - (C) dobutamine 등 강심제는 누두부 수축을 강화해 우심실 유출로를 더 좁힌다 — 금물이다.
  - (E) adenosine 은 회귀성 SVT 처치 — 심박 168 은 울음·저산소에 대한 동성 빈맥이다.
- **임상핵심**: **팔로 4징 + 울음 뒤 깊어진 청색증 + 잡음 감소 → 과청색증 발작 → 무릎-가슴 자세 → 산소·morphine·수액 → phenylephrine(강심제 금물).**
- **출처**: Nelson Textbook of Pediatrics, 21e(Tetralogy of Fallot). Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine, 22e. 280장 Congenital Heart Disease in the Adult.
