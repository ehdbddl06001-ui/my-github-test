## 문제
A 24-year-old woman comes to the emergency department because of swelling of her upper lip and left cheek that began 10 hours ago and has slowly worsened. Since age 13 years, she has had 5 similar episodes of swelling of the face, hands, or genitals, each lasting 2 to 4 days; two episodes were accompanied by severe crampy abdominal pain and vomiting. During one previous episode, she was treated in another emergency department with intramuscular epinephrine, intravenous diphenhydramine, and methylprednisolone without noticeable improvement. The episodes have become more frequent since she began taking a combined estrogen-progestin oral contraceptive 4 months ago. She takes no other medications. Her father had similar episodes of swelling and required an emergency tracheostomy at age 40 years. Her vital signs are shown. The upper lip and left cheek are swollen, nonpitting, and not red; there is no urticaria, and she reports no itching. The tongue and uvula are not swollen, and there is no stridor or hoarseness. The lungs are clear to auscultation. Laboratory results are shown. Which of the following best explains the mechanism of this patient's swelling?

- A. IgE-mediated degranulation of mucosal mast cells
- B. Immune complex deposition activating the classical complement pathway
- C. Impaired breakdown of bradykinin by angiotensin-converting enzyme
- D. Unregulated plasma kallikrein activity generating excess bradykinin
- E. Increased leukotriene synthesis caused by cyclooxygenase inhibition

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: D

- **정답근거**: 3단계. ① 두드러기·가려움 없음 + epinephrine·항히스타민 무반응 → **비히스타민(브래디키닌) 혈관부종**. ② 13세 발병·우성 가족력 + C4 저하·C1q 정상 → **유전성 혈관부종(C1-INH 결핍)**. ③ C1-INH 가 없으면 **plasma kallikrein 이 억제되지 않아 bradykinin 과다** → B2 수용체 → 혈관 투과성↑.
- **오답감별**:
  - (A) 비만세포 탈과립은 두드러기·가려움·epinephrine 반응·트립타제 상승을 동반한다 — 모두 없다.
  - (B) 면역복합체는 혈관염·사구체신염을 일으키지, 며칠 지속하다 저절로 가라앉는 비함요 부종 발작을 일으키지 않는다.
  - (C) 같은 bradykinin 축이지만 ACE 억제제를 먹지 않고 가족력·C4 저하가 있다 — 분해 감소가 아니라 생성 과다다.
  - (E) COX 억제에 따른 류코트리엔 과다는 NSAID 복용과 두드러기·천식을 동반한다.
- **임상핵심**: **두드러기 없는 혈관부종 + 히스타민 치료 무반응 = 브래디키닌성** — C4 로 선별하고(낮으면 C1-INH 양·기능), C1q 가 낮으면 후천성. 급성 발작 치료도 epinephrine 이 아니라 C1-INH 농축제·icatibant(B2 길항제)·ecallantide(kallikrein 억제제)다.
- **출처**: Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine, 22e. 363장 Urticaria, Angioedema, and Allergic Rhinitis.
