## 문제
A 67-year-old man is evaluated for increasing shortness of breath on the second day after an open right hemicolectomy for adenocarcinoma of the cecum. He has been reluctant to cough or breathe deeply because of incisional pain. He has no chest pain. He has never smoked and had normal pulmonary function before surgery. The hospital is at sea level. His vital signs are shown. He is alert and fully oriented. Breath sounds are absent over the right lower posterior chest, and there is dullness to percussion in the same area. The calves are not swollen or tender. Arterial blood gas analysis is performed while he breathes room air and again after he has breathed 100% oxygen for 20 minutes through a tight-fitting mask with a reservoir; laboratory results are shown. A chest x-ray shows homogeneous opacification of the right lower lobe with elevation of the right hemidiaphragm and shift of the major fissure toward the opacity; there are no air bronchograms. Which of the following is the most likely primary mechanism of this patient's hypoxemia?

- A. Reduced overall alveolar ventilation that raises alveolar carbon dioxide
- B. Impaired diffusion of oxygen across a thickened alveolar-capillary membrane
- C. Reduced partial pressure of oxygen in the inspired air
- D. Lung units whose ventilation is reduced relative to their perfusion
- E. Blood flow through alveoli that are perfused but not ventilated

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: E

- **정답근거**: 3단계. ① 해수면 PAO2 ≈ 150 − 34/0.8 ≈ 107 → **A-a 차이 약 51 mm Hg 증가**, PaCO2 34 → 환기 저하·흡입 산소 저하가 아니다. ② 100% 산소 20분 뒤에도 PaO2 **78 mm Hg** → 산소에 반응하지 않는 저산소증 = **션트**. ③ 수술 뒤 얕은 호흡 + 우하엽 음영·횡격막 상승·엽간열 이동 → **무기폐에 의한 폐내 션트**.
- **오답감별**:
  - (A) 폐포 환기 저하는 PaCO2 가 오르고 A-a 차이가 정상이다 — PaCO2 34, A-a 차이 증가.
  - (B) 확산 장애는 A-a 차이를 늘리지만 100% 산소로 쉽게 교정되고, 정상 폐기능이던 수술 이틀째 환자에게 간질 두께 변화가 생길 까닭이 없다.
  - (C) 흡입 산소 분압 저하(고지대)는 A-a 차이가 정상이다 — 해수면이고 A-a 차이가 크다.
  - (D) 낮은 V/Q 단위는 환기가 남아 있어 100% 산소로 PaO2 가 크게 오른다 — 78 에 그쳤다.
- **임상핵심**: **A-a 차이가 정상 → 환기 저하·고지대 / A-a 차이 증가 → 100% 산소에 반응하면 V/Q 불균형·확산 장애, 반응하지 않으면 션트**(무기폐·폐부종·ARDS·폐렴의 경화·심내 우→좌 단락).
- **출처**: Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine, 22e. 42장 Hypoxia and Cyanosis.
