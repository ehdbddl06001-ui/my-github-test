## 문제
A 54-year-old man is evaluated in the hospital because of tremor, sweating and anxiety that began this morning. He was admitted 2 days ago for cellulitis of the left leg and is receiving intravenous cefazolin. He has drunk 12 to 15 cans of beer daily for 20 years; his last drink was shortly before admission, about 48 hours ago. Alcohol-related cirrhosis was diagnosed 1 year ago. He has never had a withdrawal seizure. He says that he sees insects crawling on the wall and asks that the room be sprayed. His vital signs are shown. He is alert and oriented to person, place and time and can name the days of the week backward. There is a coarse tremor of the outstretched hands, and his skin is diaphoretic. There are spider angiomas on the chest, and the abdomen is distended with shifting dullness. There is no asterixis. The left leg erythema is receding within its marked borders. The Clinical Institute Withdrawal Assessment for Alcohol (CIWA-Ar) score is 21. Laboratory results are shown. Which of the following is the most appropriate pharmacotherapy at this time?

- A. Lorazepam
- B. Chlordiazepoxide
- C. Haloperidol
- D. Clonidine
- E. Naltrexone

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: A

- **정답근거**: 3단계. ① 마지막 음주 48시간 + 떨림·발한·빈맥·고혈압 + CIWA-Ar 21 → **중등도~중증 알코올 금단**. ② 지남력·주의력 보존 + 환시 → 진전 섬망이 아닌 **금단 환각증** — 항정신병약이 아니라 **벤조디아제핀**. ③ 복수·알부민 2.6·빌리루빈 3.4·INR 1.8 → **비대상성 간경변** → 글루쿠로니드 포합만 거치는 **lorazepam**.
- **오답감별**:
  - (B) chlordiazepoxide 는 간 산화 대사·긴 활성 대사물로 비대상성 간경변에서 쌓여 과진정·간성 뇌병증을 부른다 — 간 기능이 정상이면 적절하다.
  - (C) haloperidol 은 금단 환시에 끌리지만 발작 역치를 낮추고 금단을 치료하지 못한다 — 벤조디아제핀 뒤 보조로만.
  - (D) clonidine 은 빈맥·고혈압·발한만 줄이고 발작·진전 섬망을 막지 못한다.
  - (E) naltrexone 은 금단이 끝난 뒤 금주 유지 약이고 비대상성 간경변에서 피한다.
- **임상핵심**: **알코올 금단 = 벤조디아제핀. 간경변·고령이면 lorazepam·oxazepam(포합만), 간이 정상이면 chlordiazepoxide·diazepam.**
