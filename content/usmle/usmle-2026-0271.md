## 문제
A 76-year-old man is brought to the emergency department 5 hours after the sudden onset of shortness of breath at rest and lightheadedness. He has severe aortic stenosis and is scheduled for transcatheter valve replacement next month; until today, he became short of breath only after climbing one flight of stairs. He has hypertension treated with lisinopril. His vital signs are shown; oxygen saturation is 89% on room air. There is a grade 3/6 late-peaking systolic murmur at the right upper sternal border radiating to both carotid arteries; the intensity is unchanged from his last visit. There are crackles over both lower lung fields. Jugular venous pressure is 9 cm H2O. The rhythm strip is shown. Laboratory results are shown. Transthoracic echocardiography shows an aortic valve area of 0.8 cm2, unchanged from 2 months ago, concentric left ventricular hypertrophy with a wall thickness of 15 mm, a left ventricular ejection fraction of 60%, and no mitral regurgitation. Which of the following best explains the abrupt worsening of this patient's condition?

- A. Increased left ventricular afterload from progression of the valve obstruction
- B. Decreased contractility from tachycardia-induced cardiomyopathy
- C. Decreased filling of a stiff hypertrophied left ventricle after loss of atrial contraction
- D. Acute mitral regurgitation from ischemic papillary muscle dysfunction
- E. Decreased systemic vascular resistance from baroreceptor withdrawal

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: C

- **정답근거**: 3단계. ① 리듬 띠: P 파 없음 + 불규칙한 RR → **새 심방세동**. ② 판막 면적 불변·박출률 60 %·승모판 역류 없음·트로포닌 정상 → 후부하 증가·수축력 저하·허혈 아님. ③ 동심성 비대의 뻣뻣한 좌심실 + **심방 수축 소실 + 짧은 이완기** → 좌심실 충만(전부하) 급감 → 1회 박출량↓(저혈압) + 좌심방압↑(폐부종).
- **오답감별**:
  - (A) 판막 면적이 2개월 전과 같다 — 협착이 진행한 것이 아니다.
  - (B) 빈맥 유발 심근병증은 수주 이상의 빈맥 뒤 박출률이 떨어진다 — 5시간 전 시작, 박출률 60 %.
  - (D) 승모판 역류가 없고 트로포닌이 정상이다 — 허혈성 유두근 기능 이상의 근거가 없다.
  - (E) 저혈압에서 압수용체 반사는 교감 신경을 올려 혈관 저항을 높인다 — 저항 감소로 설명할 수 없다.
- **임상핵심**: **대동맥판 협착·비대성 심근병증처럼 뻣뻣한 좌심실은 「심방 수축(atrial kick)」에 기댄다 — 새 심방세동은 급격한 박출량 감소·폐부종을 일으킨다.**
- **출처**: Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine, 22e. 272장 Aortic Stenosis; Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine, 22e. 258장 Atrial Fibrillation.
