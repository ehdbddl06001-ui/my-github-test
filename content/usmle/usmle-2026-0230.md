## 문제
A 67-year-old man is brought to the emergency department 30 minutes after a generalized tonic-clonic seizure at home. For the past 2 days he has had nausea, repeated vomiting and palpitations, and his wife has noticed that his hands shake. He has severe chronic obstructive pulmonary disease treated with inhaled tiotropium, inhaled albuterol as needed and sustained-release oral theophylline, which he has taken at the same dose for 3 years. Five days ago he began a course of oral ciprofloxacin for an exacerbation with increased purulent sputum. He has smoked 1 pack of cigarettes daily for 45 years and smoked as usual this morning. He has no history of seizures or head trauma. His vital signs are shown. He is drowsy but arousable. He has a fine tremor of both hands. Scattered expiratory wheezes are heard. An ECG shows sinus tachycardia. Laboratory results are shown. Which of the following is the most likely mechanism of this patient's elevated serum drug concentration?

- A. Decreased renal tubular secretion
- B. Displacement from albumin binding sites
- C. Inhibition of intestinal P-glycoprotein
- D. Inhibition of hepatic CYP1A2
- E. Withdrawal of smoking-induced CYP1A2 activity

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: D

- **정답근거**: 3단계 추론. ① 구토·떨림·빈맥·경련 + 저칼륨·고혈당 + 혈중 34 µg/mL → **테오필린 독성**. ② 3년간 같은 용량, 5일 전 ciprofloxacin 시작 → **약물 상호작용**. ③ 흡연 지속·크레아티닌·알부민 정상 → **ciprofloxacin 의 간 CYP1A2 억제**로 테오필린 청소율이 떨어졌다.
- **오답감별**:
  - (A) 테오필린은 대부분 간에서 대사되고 크레아티닌이 정상이다 — 신 배설 감소로 설명되지 않는다.
  - (B) 테오필린의 단백 결합은 중간 정도이고 알부민도 정상이다. 결합 치환은 유리 분율을 잠깐 바꿀 뿐 총농도를 이렇게 올리지 못한다.
  - (C) P-glycoprotein 억제로 흡수가 느는 것은 digoxin 같은 기질의 기전이다 — 테오필린은 원래 거의 다 흡수된다.
  - (E) 금연은 CYP1A2 유도를 없애 같은 결과를 내지만, 이 환자는 오늘 아침까지 피웠다.
- **임상핵심**: **테오필린은 간 CYP1A2 로 대사되는 좁은 치료역 약 — ciprofloxacin·fluvoxamine 같은 CYP1A2 억제제나 금연이 독성을 부른다.**
