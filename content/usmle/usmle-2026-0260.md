## 문제
A 34-year-old woman comes to the physician because her calcium level was high on laboratory studies obtained for a life insurance application; a measurement 1 year ago was 10.8 mg/dL. She feels well. She has had no kidney stones, fractures, abdominal pain, constipation, polyuria or mood changes. She takes no medications or supplements, including thiazides, lithium, or calcium or vitamin D tablets. Her father has had a "high calcium level" for as long as he can remember; he underwent neck surgery at age 45 years for a presumed parathyroid tumor, and his calcium level did not change afterward. Her vital signs are shown. There is no neck mass. Physical examination shows no abnormalities. Laboratory results are shown. Which of the following is the most appropriate next step in evaluation?

- A. Technetium-99m sestamibi scan of the neck
- B. Dual-energy x-ray absorptiometry of the spine and hip
- C. Serum parathyroid hormone–related protein measurement
- D. Referral to an endocrine surgeon for parathyroidectomy
- E. Measurement of 24-hour urine calcium and creatinine

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: E

- **정답근거**: 3단계 추론. ① 칼슘 10.9(알부민 정상) + PTH 58 → **PTH 의존성 고칼슘혈증**. ② 젊고 무증상·수년간 같은 값 + 아버지의 평생 고칼슘·수술 무효 + 마그네슘 약간 높음 → **FHH 가능성**. ③ 비타민 D 충분·thiazide·리튬 없음 → **24시간 소변 칼슘/크레아티닌**으로 청소율 비를 구해 가른다(< 0.01 FHH) — 영상·수술은 그 뒤.
- **오답감별**:
  - (A) sestamibi 는 원발성 부갑상선기능항진증으로 수술을 정한 뒤 위치를 찾는 검사다 — 진단 검사가 아니다.
  - (B) DXA 는 원발성 부갑상선기능항진증의 수술 적응을 정할 때 쓴다 — FHH 를 가르지 못한다.
  - (C) PTHrP 는 PTH 가 억제된 고칼슘혈증(악성 종양)에서 잰다 — 이 환자는 PTH 가 억제되지 않았다.
  - (D) FHH 라면 수술이 칼슘을 낮추지 못한다(아버지의 경과) — 진단 확정 전 수술은 이르다.
- **임상핵심**: **고칼슘 + PTH 억제 안 됨 + 가족력·무증상 → 영상·수술 전에 24시간 소변 Ca/Cr 청소율 비(< 0.01 = FHH).**
