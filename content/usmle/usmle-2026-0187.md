## 문제
A 26-year-old man is evaluated on an inpatient psychiatric unit 6 weeks after he was admitted when police found him standing on the edge of a highway overpass because voices had told him to jump. Schizophrenia was diagnosed 4 years ago. Two years ago he took risperidone, 6 mg daily, for 8 weeks with little improvement in his hallucinations. Since admission he has received olanzapine, which was increased to 20 mg daily 6 weeks ago; nurses watch him swallow each dose. He continues to hear voices telling him that he deserves to die and believes that his neighbors are poisoning his food. He has made no attempt to harm himself in the hospital. He does not drink alcohol or use illicit drugs, and urine toxicology screening on admission was negative. He has no history of seizures, neutropenia or heart disease. His vital signs are shown. He is disheveled and guarded, with a flat affect. He says the voices are present right now and that he would "do what they say" if he were discharged. There is no insight into his illness. Laboratory studies are shown. An ECG shows normal sinus rhythm with a QTc interval of 418 msec. Which of the following is the most appropriate pharmacotherapy?

- A. Switch to aripiprazole
- B. Switch to clozapine
- C. Add haloperidol to olanzapine
- D. Switch to long-acting injectable paliperidone palmitate
- E. Add lithium to olanzapine

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: B

- **정답근거**: 3단계 추론. ① risperidone 6 mg 8주, olanzapine 20 mg 6주 — 서로 다른 두 항정신병약의 충분한 용량·기간 시도에도 환청·망상이 지속 → **치료저항성 조현병** 후보. ② 간호사가 복용을 확인했고 혈중 olanzapine 이 치료 범위 → 복약 불이행·낮은 농도에 의한 **가성 저항이 아니다**. ③ 치료저항성 + 명령 환청에 따른 **지속적 자살 위험**, 경련·호중구 감소 병력 없음·ANC 정상 → **clozapine 으로 전환**(치료저항성에 효과가 입증된 유일한 약이고, 자살 행동도 줄인다).
- **오답감별**:
  - (A) 기전이 다른 세 번째 약을 시도하고 싶지만, 충분한 두 번의 시도가 실패하면 일반 항정신병약에 반응할 가능성은 낮고 clozapine 도입만 늦어진다.
  - (C) 두 항정신병약 병용은 치료저항성의 표준이 아니며 부작용(추체외로 증상·고프로락틴혈증)만 늘린다 — clozapine 을 먼저 쓴다.
  - (D) 장기 지속형 주사제는 복약 불이행을 해결하는 선택인데, 복용이 확인되고 혈중 농도도 치료 범위라 이 환자의 문제가 아니다.
  - (E) 리튬은 기분장애에서 자살을 줄이는 근거가 있지만 조현병의 증강 효과는 약하고, 자살 행동 감소 근거가 있는 약은 clozapine 이다.
- **임상핵심**: **충분한 두 항정신병약 실패(복약·농도 확인) 또는 지속적 자살 위험이 있는 조현병 → clozapine. 복약 불이행이 문제면 장기 지속형 주사제.**
- **출처**: APA Practice Guideline for Schizophrenia 3rd ed. (2020); TRRIP consensus (2017); InterSePT (2003).
