## 문제
A 29-year-old woman is brought to the emergency department 25 days after an uncomplicated vaginal delivery because of a seizure. Her husband reports that she has had a headache for 5 days that has steadily worsened, is worse in the morning and when she lies down, and is not relieved by acetaminophen; she vomited twice yesterday. This morning, her right arm jerked rhythmically for 2 minutes before she lost consciousness and had generalized shaking. Her blood pressure was normal throughout pregnancy, and she had no proteinuria. She takes no medications and is breastfeeding. Her vital signs are shown. She is drowsy but follows commands. Funduscopic examination shows bilateral optic disc swelling. Strength is 4/5 in the right arm, and there is an extensor plantar response on the right. The neck is supple. Laboratory results are shown. A noncontrast CT of the head shows a 2-cm hemorrhage at the gray-white junction of the left parietal lobe with surrounding edema and a hyperdense superior sagittal sinus. Which of the following is the most likely diagnosis?

- A. Cerebral venous sinus thrombosis
- B. Postpartum preeclampsia with reversible encephalopathy
- C. Reversible cerebral vasoconstriction syndrome
- D. Ruptured arteriovenous malformation
- E. Hypertensive intracerebral hemorrhage

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: A

- **정답근거**: 2단계. ① 산욕기 + 5일간 진행하고 누우면 악화되는 두통 + 유두부종 → **아급성 두개내압 상승**. ② 피질–백질 경계 출혈 + **고밀도 상시상정맥동** + 정상 혈압 → **뇌정맥동 혈전증**(확진: CT·MR 정맥조영술).
- **오답감별**:
  - (B) 혈압 124/78, 단백뇨 음성 — 자간전증·PRES 의 근거가 없다.
  - (C) RCVS 는 반복 벼락두통·볼록면 거미막밑 출혈 — 유두부종·고밀도 정맥동과 맞지 않는다.
  - (D) AVM 파열은 갑작스럽다 — 수일간 진행하는 두개내압 상승과 고밀도 정맥동은 정맥 혈전을 가리킨다.
  - (E) 고혈압성 출혈은 혈압이 높고 깊은 부위(기저핵·시상)에 생긴다.
- **임상핵심**: **산욕기·피임약·혈전성향 + 수일간 진행하는 두통·유두부종·발작 + 동맥 영역이 아닌(흔히 양측·피질하) 출혈성 경색 → 뇌정맥동 혈전증** — 출혈이 있어도 항응고(헤파린)가 치료다.
- **출처**: Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine, 22e. 437장 Introduction to Cerebrovascular Diseases; Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine, 22e. 439장 Intracerebral Hemorrhage.
