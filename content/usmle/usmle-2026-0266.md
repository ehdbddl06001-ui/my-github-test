## 문제
A 74-year-old woman comes to the physician because of nausea, loss of appetite and fatigue for 5 days. She says that over the past 2 days, white objects have looked yellow to her. She has heart failure with reduced ejection fraction and permanent atrial fibrillation. Her medications for 3 years have been carvedilol, furosemide, apixaban and digoxin 0.125 mg daily, and she has taken them as prescribed. Two weeks ago, amiodarone was added because of episodes of rapid ventricular rate. Her vital signs are shown. She is alert and oriented. There is no jugular venous distention, the lungs are clear to auscultation and there is trace ankle edema. An ECG shows atrial fibrillation with a slow ventricular response and scooped ST-segment depression in the lateral leads. Laboratory results are shown. Which of the following best explains the change in this patient's serum drug concentration?

- A. Induction of CYP3A4 that converts the drug to an active metabolite
- B. Displacement of the drug from its binding sites on plasma albumin
- C. Low extracellular potassium that enhances binding to Na+/K+-ATPase
- D. Inhibition of P-glycoprotein–mediated renal and intestinal efflux
- E. Inhibition of CYP2C9 that slows hepatic oxidative metabolism

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: D

- **정답근거**: 3단계. ① 황시증·위장 증상·느린 심실 반응 + digoxin 3.1 → **digoxin 독성**. ② 용량·복약·신기능(크레아티닌 1.0, 변화 없음)이 그대로이고 2주 전 amiodarone 추가 → **상호작용으로 농도 상승**. ③ digoxin 은 대사보다 **P-glycoprotein 으로 신세뇨관 분비·장 배출** → amiodarone 의 **P-gp 억제**가 청소율을 낮춘다.
- **오답감별**:
  - (A) CYP3A4 유도는 농도를 낮추는 쪽이고, digoxin 에는 의미 있는 활성 대사물이 없다. amiodarone 은 유도제가 아니라 억제제다.
  - (B) digoxin 은 단백 결합률이 낮고(약 25 %) 알부민도 정상이다 — 결합 치환으로 총 농도가 오르지는 않는다.
  - (C) 저칼륨은 독성을 키우는 요인이지만 농도를 올리지 않는다. 이 환자는 칼륨 5.4 로 높다(Na+/K+-ATPase 억제의 결과).
  - (E) amiodarone 의 CYP2C9 억제는 warfarin 상호작용의 기전이다 — digoxin 은 거의 대사되지 않는다.
- **임상핵심**: **digoxin + amiodarone·verapamil·quinidine·clarithromycin → P-gp 억제 → digoxin 농도 상승 — 시작할 때 digoxin 을 줄이고 농도를 본다.**
