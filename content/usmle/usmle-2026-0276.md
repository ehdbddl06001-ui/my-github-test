## 문제
A 32-year-old woman, gravida 2, para 2, has heavy vaginal bleeding 30 minutes after a spontaneous vaginal delivery of a 4.3-kg (9.5-lb) boy at 40 weeks' gestation. Labor lasted 19 hours and was augmented with oxytocin. The placenta delivered spontaneously and was inspected and found to be complete. Estimated blood loss is 1,200 mL. She has moderate persistent asthma treated with inhaled fluticasone-salmeterol and was hospitalized for an asthma exacerbation 1 year ago; she has no wheezing today. Her blood pressure throughout pregnancy and labor ranged from 108/66 to 122/78 mm Hg. She has no history of liver disease. Her vital signs are shown. The uterine fundus is soft and boggy and is palpated 3 cm above the umbilicus; it firms briefly with bimanual massage. Inspection of the cervix and vagina shows no lacerations or hematoma. An oxytocin infusion is running, and two large-bore intravenous catheters are in place. Laboratory results are shown. Further pharmacologic treatment is planned. Administration of which of the following agents should be avoided in this patient?

- A. Methylergonovine
- B. Carboprost tromethamine
- C. Misoprostol
- D. Tranexamic acid
- E. Additional oxytocin

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: B

- **정답근거**: 3단계. ① 태반 완전·열상 없음·응고 정상 + 물렁하고 큰 자궁 → **자궁 이완성 산후 출혈**. ② 임신·진통 중 혈압 정상 → methylergonovine 사용 가능. ③ 중등도 지속성 천식 → **carboprost(PGF2α)는 기관지 경련 위험으로 피한다**.
- **오답감별**:
  - (A) methylergonovine 은 고혈압·전자간증에서 금기다 — 이 환자는 혈압이 정상이라 쓸 수 있다.
  - (C) misoprostol 은 PGE1 이라 기관지 수축이 없어 천식에서도 쓸 수 있다.
  - (D) tranexamic acid 는 산후 출혈 진단 뒤 3시간 안에 권고된다 — 천식과 무관하다.
  - (E) 옥시토신은 1차 약으로 계속한다 — 천식과 무관하다.
- **임상핵심**: **자궁 이완 2차 약 — 천식이면 carboprost(PGF2α) 피하고, 고혈압·전자간증이면 methylergonovine 피한다.**
- **출처**: Williams Obstetrics, 26e(Obstetrical hemorrhage); Gabbe's Obstetrics, 8e(Postpartum hemorrhage).
