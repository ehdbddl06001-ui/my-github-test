## 문제
A 34-year-old man is brought to the emergency department 25 minutes after he was thrown from his motorcycle at high speed. He is alert and speaking in full sentences. His vital signs are shown. Breath sounds are equal bilaterally, and the trachea is midline. Two large-bore intravenous catheters are placed; 1 L of warmed crystalloid is infused, the massive transfusion protocol is activated, and transfusion of uncrossmatched type O red blood cells is started. A chest x-ray shows no pneumothorax, hemothorax or mediastinal widening. Focused assessment with sonography for trauma (FAST) shows no fluid in the pericardium, the hepatorenal or splenorenal spaces, or the pelvis. The abdomen is mildly tender without distention or guarding. There is marked tenderness over the pubic symphysis, and there is bruising of the perineum. Neurologic examination shows no deficits. An anteroposterior radiograph of the pelvis shows 4 cm of widening of the pubic symphysis and widening of both sacroiliac joints. After the crystalloid and the first unit of blood, his blood pressure is 86/54 mm Hg and his pulse is 126/min. Laboratory results are shown. Which of the following is the most appropriate next step in management?

- A. Emergency exploratory laparotomy
- B. CT of the abdomen and pelvis with contrast
- C. Pelvic angiography with embolization
- D. Additional 2-L crystalloid bolus
- E. Circumferential pelvic binder

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: E

- **정답근거**: 3단계 추론. ① 수액 1 L + 적혈구 1단위 뒤에도 혈압 86/54·맥박 126, 젖산 5.2 → **소생에 반응하지 않는 출혈성 쇼크**. ② 흉부 X선 정상·FAST 음성 + 치골결합 4 cm·양쪽 천장관절 벌어짐 → 출혈원은 **open-book 골반 골절**. ③ 불안정해 CT 불가 → 소생실에서 바로 **원형 골반 고정대**로 골반 용적을 줄이고, 그래도 불안정하면 패킹·색전술.
- **오답감별**:
  - (A) FAST 가 음성이라 복강 출혈 근거가 없고, 후복막 골반 혈종을 열면 압박 효과가 사라진다.
  - (B) CT 는 안정된 환자에게만 한다 — 소생 뒤에도 불안정하다.
  - (C) 색전술은 동맥 출혈의 확정 치료지만 시간이 걸리고 정맥총 출혈은 막지 못한다 — 고정대를 먼저 대고, 그 뒤에도 불안정하면 시행한다.
  - (D) 결정질 수액을 더 주면 희석성 응고장애·저체온·산증을 악화시킨다 — ATLS 는 1 L 뒤 혈액 제제로 넘어간다.
- **임상핵심**: **불안정 + 흉부·FAST 음성 + open-book 골반 골절 → 먼저 원형 골반 고정대(대전자 높이) + 수혈, 이후 패킹·색전술. CT 는 안정된 뒤.**
- **출처**: ATLS Student Course Manual 10th ed (ACS 2018) — shock, abdominal and pelvic trauma; WSES pelvic trauma guidelines (World J Emerg Surg 2017).
