## 문제
A 7-month-old girl is brought to the physician because of vomiting, poor feeding, and increasing sleepiness for 3 weeks. She was exclusively breastfed until age 5 months and grew along the 50th percentile for weight. Rice cereal was introduced at 5 months of age, and 4 weeks ago her parents began giving her pureed pears and apples and diluted apple juice. She often vomits and becomes sweaty and pale within 30 minutes of these feedings; she is well between feedings and sleeps through the night without feeding. She has had no fever or diarrhea. Her weight has fallen from the 50th to the 15th percentile. Her vital signs are shown. She is lethargic but arousable. The sclerae are mildly icteric. The liver edge is palpable 4 cm below the right costal margin; the spleen is not palpable. Laboratory studies obtained 40 minutes after a feeding of apple juice are shown. A deficiency of which of the following enzymes is the most likely cause of this patient's condition?

- A. Fructose-1-phosphate aldolase
- B. Hepatic fructokinase (ketohexokinase)
- C. Galactose-1-phosphate uridyltransferase
- D. Hepatic glucose-6-phosphatase
- E. Medium-chain acyl-CoA dehydrogenase

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: A

- **정답근거**: 3단계. ① 과일·주스 시작 뒤 **식후 30분 내** 구토·저혈당 + 간비대·황달 → 과당이 유발. ② 모유 5개월 정상 + 밤새 공복을 견딤 → 갈락토스혈증·G6Pase·MCAD 가능성 낮음. ③ 저혈당·**저인산혈증**·간손상 → F1P 가 쌓이는 **aldolase B 결핍**(무증상 fructokinase 결핍이 아님).
- **오답감별**:
  - (B) fructokinase 결핍(본태성 과당뇨)은 F1P 가 생기지 않아 무증상이다 — 저혈당·간손상이 없다.
  - (C) 갈락토스혈증은 유당 노출 직후(생후 첫 주) 시작한다 — 모유 5개월 동안 정상이었다.
  - (D) G6Pase 결핍은 짧은 공복에도 저혈당·젖산산증이 오고 생후 초기부터 간비대가 있다 — 이 아이는 밤새 굶어도 괜찮고 식후에 저혈당이 온다.
  - (E) MCAD 결핍은 공복·감염 때 저케톤성 저혈당이 온다 — 섭취가 아니라 굶음이 유발한다.
- **임상핵심**: **영아 저혈당은 「언제」로 가른다 — 공복 뒤면 G6Pase·지방산 산화 결함, 특정 음식 뒤면 그 당의 대사 결함.** 과당 뒤 저혈당 + 저인산혈증 + 간손상 = aldolase B 결핍 → 과당·자당·sorbitol 을 끊는다.
- **출처**: Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine, 22e. 430장 Glycogen Storage Diseases and Other Inherited Disorders of Carbohydrate Metabolism.
