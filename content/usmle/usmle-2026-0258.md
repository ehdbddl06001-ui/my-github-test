## 문제
A 54-year-old man comes to the physician because of diarrhea for 8 months. He passes 4 to 6 bulky, greasy, foul-smelling stools daily and has lost 9 kg (20 lb). For 4 years before the diarrhea began, he had episodes of pain and swelling that moved between his knees, ankles and wrists and resolved within days. His wife says he has become forgetful over the past 3 months. He has worked on a dairy farm all his life. His vital signs are shown. The skin over the face and forearms is diffusely darker than in photographs from 2 years ago. There is nontender lymphadenopathy in the cervical, axillary and inguinal regions. The abdomen is mildly distended and nontender. There is no joint swelling at this visit. Laboratory results are shown. Upper endoscopy shows pale yellow, shaggy mucosa with scattered white plaques in the distal duodenum. A biopsy specimen of the duodenum is most likely to show which of the following?

- A. Villous atrophy with crypt hyperplasia and intraepithelial lymphocytes
- B. Foamy macrophages filled with PAS-positive granules in the lamina propria
- C. Noncaseating granulomas extending through all layers of the bowel wall
- D. Pear-shaped binucleate trophozoites lying on the villous surface
- E. Markedly dilated lacteals in an otherwise uninflamed lamina propria

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: B

- **정답근거**: 2단계 추론. ① 지방변·체중 감소·저알부민 + 총 IgA 정상인데 tTG IgA 음성·Giardia 항원 음성 → 셀리악·람블편모충보다 다른 흡수 장애. ② 수년 선행한 이동성 관절염 + 미열·전신 림프절 비대·색소 침착·인지 저하 → **Whipple 병** → 생검에 **고유판의 PAS 양성 거품 대식세포**.
- **오답감별**:
  - (A) 융모 위축·움 증식·상피내 림프구는 셀리악병 — tTG IgA 음성(총 IgA 정상)이고 전신 증상이 맞지 않는다.
  - (C) 전층의 비건락 육아종은 Crohn 병 — 누공·회장 말단 염증·복통이 주이고 이동성 관절염 뒤 신경 증상이 오는 경과가 아니다.
  - (D) 서양배 모양 2핵 영양형은 람블편모충 — 대변 Giardia 항원이 음성이다.
  - (E) 확장된 유미관만 있는 고유판은 장 림프관 확장증 — 저알부민은 맞지만 발열·림프절 비대·관절염·인지 저하를 설명하지 못한다.
- **임상핵심**: **관절염 선행 + 흡수 장애 + 발열·림프절·신경 증상 → Whipple 병 → 고유판 PAS 양성 거품 대식세포.**
