## 문제
A 38-year-old woman comes to the physician because of fatigue, increased thirst and urination, and constipation for 3 months. She has also had a dry cough for 2 months. She has never smoked and does not drink alcohol. She takes a multivitamin containing 600 IU of vitamin D daily and no other medications or supplements. She works as an elementary school teacher. Her vital signs are shown. There are several 1-cm firm, nontender, red-brown papules around the nostrils. The lungs are clear to auscultation. There is no lymphadenopathy in the neck, axillae or groin, and there is no hepatosplenomegaly. Laboratory results are shown. A chest x-ray shows bilateral, symmetric hilar and right paratracheal lymphadenopathy with clear lung fields. Which of the following is the most likely mechanism of this patient's hypercalcemia?

- A. Secretion of parathyroid hormone–related peptide by tumor cells
- B. Excess intake of cholecalciferol with saturation of storage capacity
- C. Autonomous parathyroid hormone secretion by a parathyroid adenoma
- D. Extrarenal 1α-hydroxylation of vitamin D by activated macrophages
- E. Osteoclast activation by cytokines from osteolytic bone lesions

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: D

- **정답근거**: 3단계. ① 칼슘 12.1 + PTH 7(억제) → **PTH 비의존성**. ② PTHrP 음성 + 25-OH D 정상 + **1,25-(OH)2 D 증가** → 칼시트리올 매개(비타민 D 과다·악성 체액성 아님). ③ 비흡연자의 대칭성 양측 폐문 림프절 + 콧방울 구진 + 다클론 감마글로불린 → **사르코이드증 육아종 대식세포의 신장 외 1α-hydroxylation**.
- **오답감별**:
  - (A) PTHrP 가 검출되지 않고 칼시트리올이 높다 — 악성 체액성 고칼슘혈증은 칼시트리올이 낮은 것이 보통이다. 비흡연자다.
  - (B) 비타민 D 중독은 25-OH D 가 크게 오른다 — 26 으로 정상이고 600 IU 는 통상 용량이다.
  - (C) 부갑상선 선종이면 PTH 가 높거나 부적절하게 정상이다 — 여기는 7 로 억제됐다.
  - (E) 골용해 병변(골수종·전이)의 고칼슘혈증은 칼시트리올이 낮거나 정상이다. 단클론 띠가 없고 ALP 도 정상이다.
- **임상핵심**: **PTH 억제 + PTHrP 음성 + 25-OH D 정상 + 1,25-(OH)2 D 증가 → 육아종(사르코이드증)의 신장 외 1α-hydroxylase → 글루코코르티코이드.**
