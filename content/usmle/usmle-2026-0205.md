## 문제
A 74-year-old man is brought to the physician by his wife because of worsening memory and confusion over the past 14 months. On some days he is alert and follows conversations; on others he stares into space for long periods and cannot follow a television program. Several times a week he sees small children and a dog in the living room who are not there; he describes them in detail and is not frightened by them. For about 6 years his wife has noticed that he shouts and punches in his sleep as if fighting someone. He has fallen twice in the past 3 months. He takes no medications. He drinks one glass of wine weekly. His vital signs are shown. He is oriented to person and place but not to date. He scores 19/30 on the Montreal Cognitive Assessment, with the greatest difficulty copying a cube, drawing a clock and keeping attention on serial subtractions; delayed recall is relatively preserved. His face is expressionless. There is mild cogwheel rigidity of both arms and slowness of finger tapping without tremor. His gait is slow and shuffling. MRI of the brain shows mild generalized atrophy without infarcts or disproportionate hippocampal loss. Laboratory results are shown. His wife asks whether a medication could help his thinking and the visions. Which of the following is the most appropriate pharmacotherapy?

- A. Haloperidol
- B. Rivastigmine
- C. Carbidopa-levodopa
- D. Risperidone
- E. Clonazepam

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: B

- **정답근거**: 3단계 추론. ① 인지·각성 변동 + 반복되는 구체적 환시 + 6년 전부터의 꿈 행동화(REM 수면 행동장애) + 떨림 없는 파킨슨증 → **루이소체 치매**. ② 루이소체 치매는 D2 차단제에 심하게 과민하고, 도파민 약은 환시를 악화시킨다 → 항정신병약·levodopa 는 인지·환시 치료로 부적절. ③ 피질 콜린 결손이 심해 **콜린에스테라아제 억제제(rivastigmine)** 가 인지와 환시를 함께 개선한다.
- **오답감별**:
  - (A) haloperidol 은 강한 D2 차단제라 루이소체 치매에서 심한 경직·의식 저하·신경이완제 악성 증후군 유사 반응을 일으킬 수 있다.
  - (C) levodopa 는 파킨슨증을 겨냥하지만 환시·혼동을 악화시킬 수 있고, 지금 원하는 것은 인지·환시 조절이다.
  - (D) 비정형 항정신병약도 D2 차단에 따른 과민 반응 위험이 있고, 괴롭지 않은 환시에는 필요하지 않다.
  - (E) clonazepam 은 REM 수면 행동장애에 쓰이기도 하지만 인지·환시를 개선하지 못하고 낙상(이미 2회)·혼동을 늘린다.
- **임상핵심**: **변동 + 환시 + REM 수면 행동장애 + 파킨슨증 = 루이소체 치매 → 콜린에스테라아제 억제제 1차, D2 차단제는 피한다.**
- **출처**: DLB Consortium 4th consensus report(Neurology 2017); Harrison's 21e(Dementia with Lewy bodies).
