## 문제
A 49-year-old man comes to the physician for follow-up of hypertension. His blood pressure has remained elevated for 8 months despite amlodipine 10 mg, lisinopril 40 mg and chlorthalidone 25 mg daily; pharmacy refill records and pill counts confirm that he takes all three. Home blood pressure readings average 154/96 mm Hg. Over the past 3 months he has had fatigue, nocturia twice nightly and cramps in his calves at night. Potassium chloride 20 mEq daily was added 6 weeks ago for a low potassium level. He has no headaches, sweating spells or palpitations. He does not snore, and his wife has not noticed pauses in his breathing. He does not use NSAIDs, decongestants or licorice, drinks 2 beers per week and does not smoke. His father had a stroke at the age of 52 years. His vital signs are shown. BMI is 27 kg/m2. There are no purple striae, supraclavicular fat pads or proximal muscle weakness. Funduscopic examination shows arteriolar narrowing. No abdominal or flank bruits are heard. Laboratory results are shown. An ECG shows sinus rhythm with prominent U waves and no other abnormalities. Which of the following is the most appropriate next step in diagnosis?

- A. Plasma aldosterone-to-renin activity ratio
- B. CT scan of the adrenal glands
- C. Plasma free metanephrines
- D. Duplex ultrasonography of the renal arteries
- E. 24-hour urine free cortisol

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: A

- **정답근거**: 3단계 추론. ① 복용이 확인된 이뇨제 포함 3제 최대 용량 + 가정 혈압 154/96 → **저항성 고혈압 → 이차 원인 탐색**. ② 칼륨 보충에도 K 3.1·HCO₃ 31·U파, 발작 증상·쿠싱 체형·신동맥 잡음 없음 → **무기질코르티코이드 과잉, 원발성 알도스테론증 우선**. ③ 국소화보다 생화학 선별이 먼저 → **혈장 알도스테론 농도 + 레닌 활성**.
- **오답감별**:
  - (B) 부신 CT 는 원발성 알도스테론증을 생화학적으로 확진한 뒤의 국소화 검사다 — 먼저 찍으면 비기능성 우연종에 끌려간다.
  - (C) 혈장 유리 메타네프린은 갈색세포종 검사다 — 두통·발한·두근거림 발작이 없고 저칼륨·알칼리증이 더 잘 맞는 원인이 따로 있다.
  - (D) 신동맥 초음파는 신혈관성 고혈압을 찾지만 잡음이 없고 ACE 억제제에도 크레아티닌이 정상이며, 레닌 측정이 두 원인을 먼저 가른다.
  - (E) 24시간 소변 유리 코르티솔은 쿠싱 증후군 검사다 — 자색 선조·쇄골 위 지방·근위부 근력 저하가 없다.
- **임상핵심**: **저항성 고혈압·저칼륨(이뇨제 유발 포함)이면 원발성 알도스테론증 — 「알도스테론/레닌 선별 → 확진 → 부신 CT」 순서를 거꾸로 하지 않는다.**
- **출처**: Endocrine Society primary aldosteronism guideline (J Clin Endocrinol Metab 2016); AHA scientific statement on resistant hypertension (Hypertension 2018).
