## 문제
A 36-year-old woman is brought to the emergency department 8 hours after she became restless and confused. She has major depressive disorder that has been treated with phenelzine for 1 year. Yesterday evening, she began taking an over-the-counter cough syrup containing dextromethorphan for an upper respiratory tract infection. She takes no other medications and does not use alcohol or illicit drugs. Her vital signs are shown. She is agitated, diaphoretic, and oriented only to person. The pupils are 7 mm and reactive to light. The mucous membranes are moist. Bowel sounds are hyperactive, and she has had three loose stools since arrival. Patellar reflexes are 4+, with sustained clonus at both ankles; muscle tone is increased more in the legs than in the arms, and there is no lead-pipe rigidity. Laboratory results are shown. Phenelzine and the cough syrup are held, and she is given intravenous fluids, intravenous lorazepam, and external cooling. One hour later, she remains agitated with sustained ankle clonus, and her temperature is 38.9 °C (102 °F). Which of the following is the most appropriate pharmacotherapy at this time?

- A. Bromocriptine
- B. Cyproheptadine
- C. Dantrolene
- D. Haloperidol
- E. Physostigmine

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: B

- **정답근거**: 3단계. ① **phenelzine(MAOI) + dextromethorphan** → 세로토닌 과다 조합, 하룻밤 만에 발병. ② **지속 발목 클로누스·과반사(하지 우세)**·설사·산동·발한, 납관 강직 없음 → **세로토닌 증후군**. ③ 원인약 중단·lorazepam·냉각 뒤에도 지속, 39 °C → **cyproheptadine**(5-HT2A 길항).
- **오답감별**:
  - (A) bromocriptine 은 NMS(도파민 길항제 노출·납관 강직·반사 저하)용 — 세로토닌 증후군을 악화시킬 수 있다.
  - (C) dantrolene 은 악성고열(마취 노출)용 — 세로토닌 증후군에 효과가 입증되지 않았다.
  - (D) haloperidol 은 항콜린·체온조절 장애로 고열을 악화시키고 NMS 를 겹칠 수 있다.
  - (E) physostigmine 은 항콜린 독성(마른 피부·점막, 장음 감소, 요저류)용 — 이 환자는 발한·촉촉한 점막·설사가 있다.
- **임상핵심**: **클로누스·과반사 = 세로토닌 증후군, 납관 강직·반사 저하 = NMS** — 세로토닌 증후군은 원인약 중단·benzodiazepine·냉각, 지속하면 cyproheptadine, 체온 > 41 °C 면 진정·비탈분극 근이완·삽관(해열제는 효과 없음).
- **출처**: Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine, 22e. 470장 Poisoning and Drug Overdose.
