## 문제
A 3-day-old boy is brought to the emergency department because of poor feeding, vomiting and increasing sleepiness since yesterday. He was born at term after an uncomplicated pregnancy and vaginal delivery and breastfed well for the first day. His mother's only brother died in the first week of life of an illness that was never diagnosed. His vital signs are shown. He is lethargic and responds only to vigorous stimulation. Breathing is rapid and unlabored. The anterior fontanelle is full. Muscle tone is decreased. The liver is palpated 2 cm below the right costal margin. There is no rash or jaundice. Laboratory results are shown. A deficiency of which of the following enzymes is the most likely cause of this patient's condition?

- A. Carbamoyl phosphate synthetase I
- B. Argininosuccinate synthetase
- C. Propionyl-CoA carboxylase
- D. Uridine monophosphate synthase
- E. Ornithine transcarbamylase

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: E

- **정답근거**: 3단계. ① 신생아 뇌병증 + 암모니아 410 + 호흡성 알칼리증, 음이온 차·케톤·혈당 정상 → 유기산혈증이 아닌 **요소 회로 이상**. ② 시트룰린 매우 낮음 → **CPS I 또는 OTC**(ASS 아님). ③ orotic acid 크게 증가 + 외삼촌의 신생아 사망(X 연관) → **OTC 결손**.
- **오답감별**:
  - (A) CPS I 결손도 시트룰린이 낮지만 carbamoyl phosphate 가 만들어지지 않아 orotic acid 가 오르지 않는다. 상염색체 열성이다.
  - (B) argininosuccinate synthetase 결손(시트룰린혈증)은 시트룰린이 크게 오른다 — 여기는 매우 낮다.
  - (C) propionyl-CoA carboxylase 결손(propionic acidemia)도 신생아 고암모니아혈증을 일으키지만 음이온 차 대사성 산증·케톤증이 동반된다 — 여기는 호흡성 알칼리증이다.
  - (D) UMP synthase 결손은 orotic acid 가 오르지만 암모니아는 정상이고 거대적아구성 빈혈이 주 소견이다.
- **임상핵심**: **고암모니아혈증 + 산증 없음 → 요소 회로. 시트룰린 낮음 + orotic acid 높음 = OTC, orotic acid 정상 = CPS I.**
- **출처**: Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine, 22e. 431장 Inherited Disorders of Amino Acid Metabolism in Adults; Nelson Textbook of Pediatrics, 22e(Urea cycle disorders).
