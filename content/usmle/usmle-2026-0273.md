## 문제
A 29-year-old man is brought to the emergency department 30 minutes after he lost control of his motorcycle and struck a guardrail. He was wearing a helmet. On arrival, his airway is patent and he is speaking; Glasgow Coma Scale score is 14. Breath sounds are equal bilaterally. Two large-bore intravenous catheters are placed; 2 L of crystalloid and 2 units of uncrossmatched packed red blood cells are given, and the massive transfusion protocol is activated. Fifteen minutes later, his vital signs are those shown; oxygen saturation is 97% on 4 L/min of oxygen by nasal cannula. His skin is cool and mottled. There is an abrasion over the left lower ribs. The abdomen is distended and diffusely tender with guarding. The pelvis is stable to compression. There is no external bleeding, and the extremities show no deformities. Portable chest and pelvic x-rays show no fractures, pneumothorax or hemothorax. Focused assessment with sonography for trauma (FAST) shows free fluid in the hepatorenal and splenorenal spaces; the pericardial view shows no effusion. Laboratory results are shown. Which of the following is the most appropriate next step in management?

- A. Exploratory laparotomy in the operating room
- B. Contrast-enhanced CT scan of the abdomen and pelvis
- C. Diagnostic peritoneal lavage at the bedside
- D. Angiography with splenic artery embolization
- E. Needle pericardiocentesis at the bedside

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: A

- **정답근거**: 3단계. ① 수액 2 L + 적혈구 2단위 뒤에도 78/50·맥박 138·젖산 6.2 → **소생술 무반응 출혈성 쇼크**. ② 흉부 X선·골반·심낭 창·외부 출혈 모두 음성 → **출혈원은 복강**(FAST 양성). ③ 불안정 + FAST 양성 → **즉시 개복술**(CT·색전술은 안정 환자의 선택지).
- **오답감별**:
  - (B) 조영 CT 는 혈역학적으로 안정된 환자에게 손상 장기·등급을 보기 위한 검사다 — 소생술 무반응 환자는 CT 실로 보내지 않는다.
  - (C) 진단적 복강 세척은 FAST 가 없거나 애매할 때 쓰는 대안이다 — 이미 FAST 가 복강 출혈을 보였다.
  - (D) 비장 색전술은 안정적이거나 일시 반응하는 환자의 비수술 치료다 — 이 환자는 소생술에 반응하지 않는다.
  - (E) FAST 심낭 창에 삼출이 없다 — 심장 눌림증의 근거가 없다.
- **임상핵심**: **둔상 + 소생술 무반응 쇼크 + FAST 양성 → 즉시 개복술. CT·색전술은 안정(또는 반응) 환자에게만.**
- **출처**: ATLS Student Course Manual, 10e(Abdominal and pelvic trauma); Sabiston Textbook of Surgery, 21e.
