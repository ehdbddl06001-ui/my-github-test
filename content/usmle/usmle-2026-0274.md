## 문제
A 61-year-old man comes to the physician because of difficulty climbing stairs and getting up from low chairs for 4 months. He also has a dry mouth, constipation and erectile dysfunction that began over the same period. In the past 3 weeks, his eyelids have occasionally felt heavy late in the day. He has not had double vision, difficulty swallowing, muscle pain or numbness. He has smoked 1.5 packs of cigarettes daily for 40 years. He has lost 5 kg over 4 months. His vital signs are shown. There is mild bilateral ptosis. Strength is 4/5 in the hip flexors and shoulder abductors and 5/5 distally; handgrip is weak at first and becomes stronger over several seconds of sustained effort. Knee and ankle reflexes are absent at rest; after 10 seconds of maximal quadriceps contraction, the knee reflexes can be elicited. There are no fasciculations, and muscle bulk is normal. Plantar responses are flexor. Sensation to pinprick, vibration and joint position is normal. Laboratory results are shown. Which of the following is the most likely diagnosis?

- A. Generalized myasthenia gravis
- B. Inflammatory myopathy (polymyositis)
- C. Amyotrophic lateral sclerosis
- D. Lambert-Eaton myasthenic syndrome
- E. Chronic inflammatory demyelinating neuropathy

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: D

- **정답근거**: 2단계. ① 근위부 근력 저하 + 입 마름·변비·발기부전 + 사라진 반사가 최대 수축 뒤 나타남 + 지속 노력에 세지는 악력 → **시냅스 앞 신경근 접합부 장애(LEMS)**. ② 감각 정상·CK 정상·근섬유다발수축 없음 → CIDP·다발근염·ALS 가능성 낮음. 흡연력 → 소세포 폐암 탐색(흉부 CT).
- **오답감별**:
  - (A) 중증 근무력증은 반복할수록 약해지고 반사·자율신경이 정상이다 — 이 환자는 지속 수축 뒤 반사가 나타나고 자율신경 증상이 있다.
  - (B) 다발근염은 근위부 근력 저하에 CK 가 오르고 반사는 보존된다 — CK 정상, 반사 소실.
  - (C) ALS 는 상·하위 운동신경원 징후(근섬유다발수축·위축·과반사)가 섞이고 자율신경은 보존된다.
  - (E) CIDP 는 반사가 없지만 감각 이상이 동반되고 수축 뒤 반사가 돌아오지 않는다.
- **임상핵심**: **근위부 근력 저하 + 자율신경 증상 + 운동 후 촉진(반사·근력) = LEMS → 소세포 폐암을 찾는다.**
