## 문제
A 56-year-old man comes to the emergency department because of severe midback pain for 10 days and numbness of both legs and difficulty starting urination since yesterday. The back pain is constant, worse when he lies flat and wakes him at night; he has had fevers at home. Three weeks ago, an abscess on his forearm was incised and drained at an urgent care clinic. He has type 2 diabetes mellitus treated with metformin. He does not use illicit drugs. His vital signs are shown. There is marked tenderness to percussion over the spinous processes of T7 and T8. Strength is 4/5 in both lower extremities and 5/5 in the upper extremities. Patellar and ankle reflexes are 3+ bilaterally, and plantar responses are extensor. Sensation to pinprick is decreased below the level of the umbilicus. Rectal tone is decreased. Laboratory results are shown. Blood cultures are drawn. Which of the following is the most appropriate next step in diagnosis?

- A. Emergency MRI of the whole spine with gadolinium
- B. Lumbar puncture for cerebrospinal fluid culture
- C. Plain x-rays of the thoracic spine
- D. Nerve conduction studies of the lower extremities
- E. Technetium-99m bone scan of the spine

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: A

- **정답근거**: 3단계 추론. ① 발열 + 밤에 깨는 등 통증·극돌기 압통 + 최근 피부 농양·당뇨 + ESR·CRP 상승 → **척추 경막외 농양 의심**. ② 감각 수준 + 위운동신경세포 징후 + 요저류 → **진행하는 흉수 압박**(응급). ③ → **응급 가돌리늄 조영 척추 전체 MRI**(건너뛴 병변), 확인되면 감압·배액 + 정맥 항생제.
- **오답감별**:
  - (B) 요추 천자는 경막외 농양을 보여 주지 못하고 감염을 옮기거나 압박을 악화시킬 수 있다 — 척수 압박에서는 영상이 먼저다.
  - (C) 단순 X선은 뼈가 무너진 뒤에야 이상이 보이고 경막외 농양을 보지 못한다 — 진행하는 척수병증에서 시간을 버린다.
  - (D) 신경전도 검사는 말초신경병(길랭-바레 등)을 본다 — 감각 수준·반사 항진·바빈스키는 척수 병변이다.
  - (E) 골 스캔은 골수염에 민감하지만 비특이적이고 경막외 농양·척수 압박을 보여 주지 못하며 시간이 걸린다.
- **임상핵심**: **등 통증 + 발열 + 위험 인자 + 척수 징후 → 응급 가돌리늄 조영 척추 전체 MRI → 감압 + 항생제(요추 천자 아님).**
- **출처**: Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine, 22e. 453장 Diseases of the Spinal Cord · 134장 Infections of the Skin, Muscles, and Soft Tissues.
