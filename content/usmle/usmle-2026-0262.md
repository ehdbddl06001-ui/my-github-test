## 문제
A 23-year-old man comes to the physician because of tingling and numbness that began in his feet 6 weeks ago and has spread to his fingers. He has become unsteady when walking, especially in the dark, and fell twice while washing his hair in the shower. For the past year he has inhaled nitrous oxide from whipped-cream chargers at parties on most weekends, about 50 cartridges each time. He eats meat and dairy daily and drinks alcohol occasionally. He takes no medications. His vital signs are shown. Mental status is normal. Muscle strength is 5/5 throughout. Vibration and joint position sense are markedly decreased in the toes and mildly decreased in the fingers. Knee reflexes are 3+, ankle reflexes are absent, and the plantar responses are extensor. He sways and nearly falls when he stands with his feet together and closes his eyes. Laboratory results are shown. T2-weighted MRI of the cervical spine shows symmetric hyperintensity in the posterior columns from C2 to C6. Which of the following is the most appropriate next step to confirm the cause of his symptoms?

- A. Nerve conduction studies and electromyography
- B. Serum copper and ceruloplasmin measurement
- C. Serum methylmalonic acid and homocysteine measurement
- D. Serum anti–intrinsic factor antibody measurement
- E. Cerebrospinal fluid analysis with oligoclonal bands

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: C

- **정답근거**: 3단계 추론. ① 후삭(진동·위치 감각 저하·Romberg) + 측삭(반사 항진·발바닥 신전) + MRI 후삭 고신호 → **아급성 연합 변성 양상**. ② 매주 대량 아산화질소 → **코발라민 비활성화(기능적 B12 결핍)**. ③ 혈청 B12 312 는 결핍을 배제하지 못함 → **메틸말론산·호모시스테인**으로 확인.
- **오답감별**:
  - (A) 신경전도 검사는 동반 말초신경병을 보여 줄 수 있지만 원인(B12 기능 결핍)을 확인하지 못한다.
  - (B) 구리 결핍도 같은 척수병증을 만들지만 원인(위 수술·아연 과다)이 없고 N2O 노출이 뚜렷하다 — 대사물 검사가 정상일 때 다음.
  - (D) 내인자 항체는 악성 빈혈의 흡수 장애를 찾는다 — N2O 는 흡수된 B12 를 비활성화한다.
  - (E) 올리고클론띠는 다발경화증을 찾는다 — 대칭적·길게 이어진 후삭 병변과 N2O 노출은 대사성 척수병증을 가리킨다.
- **임상핵심**: **N2O 남용 + 후삭·측삭 척수병증 → 혈청 B12 가 정상이어도 메틸말론산·호모시스테인으로 기능적 결핍 확인 → N2O 중단 + B12.**
- **출처**: Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine, 22e. 104장 Megaloblastic Anemias; 453장 Diseases of the Spinal Cord.
