## 문제
A 3-year-old boy is brought to the physician because of irritability, poor appetite and intermittent abdominal pain for 6 weeks. He has had hard stools every 3 to 4 days during this period. His parents have noticed that he has stopped using several words he had been saying. The family lives in a house built in 1925; the parents have been sanding and repainting the window frames for the past 3 months, and he has been seen putting paint chips from the windowsill into his mouth. He drinks 16 oz of milk daily and eats a varied diet. He appears pale. The abdomen is soft and mildly tender without masses or organomegaly. Neurologic examination shows no focal findings. A peripheral blood smear shows hypochromic, microcytic red cells with coarse basophilic stippling. His vital signs are shown. Laboratory studies are shown. Inhibition of which of the following enzymes best explains the accumulation of protoporphyrin in this patient's red blood cells?

- A. Aminolevulinate synthase
- B. Ferrochelatase
- C. Porphobilinogen deaminase
- D. Uroporphyrinogen decarboxylase
- E. Aminolevulinate dehydratase

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: B

- **정답근거**: 3단계 추론. ① 소구성 빈혈이지만 페리틴 정상·HbA2 정상 → 철결핍·지중해빈혈 아님. ② 오래된 집 페인트 샌딩 + 페인트 조각 섭취 + 복통·변비·말 퇴행 + 거친 호염기성 반점 → **납 중독**. ③ protoporphyrin 축적은 철을 끼우는 마지막 단계가 막힌 것 → **ferrochelatase 억제**(아연이 대신 들어가 zinc protoporphyrin ↑).
- **오답감별**:
  - (A) ALA 합성효소가 막히면 하류 산물이 모두 줄어 protoporphyrin 이 쌓이지 않는다(B6 결핍성 사철적혈모구 빈혈).
  - (C) 급성 간헐성 포르피린증 — 복통·신경 증상은 겹치지만 빈혈·호염기성 반점이 없고 ALA·PBG 가 쌓인다.
  - (D) 만발성 피부 포르피린증 — 성인의 광과민 물집, uroporphyrin 축적.
  - (E) 납이 억제하는 것은 맞지만 그 결과는 δ-ALA 축적이다. protoporphyrin 축적은 설명하지 못한다.
- **임상핵심**: **납 = ALA 탈수효소(δ-ALA ↑) + ferrochelatase(protoporphyrin·zinc protoporphyrin ↑) 억제. 소구성 빈혈에서 페리틴이 정상이면 납·지중해빈혈·사철적혈모구를 가른다.**
- **출처**: Harrison 21e(Heavy Metal Poisoning; The Porphyrias); CDC Childhood Lead Poisoning Prevention(2021 참고치).
