## 문제
A 3-year-old boy is brought to the emergency department because of fever and right upper abdominal pain for 6 days. At 14 months of age, he had cervical lymphadenitis that required surgical drainage and grew Serratia marcescens; at 2 years of age, a second neck node and a perianal abscess were drained. His umbilical cord separated at 9 days of age. He has had no eczema. His immunizations are up to date. His mother's brother died at age 19 of a pneumonia caused by a mold. His vital signs are shown. Height and weight are at the 15th percentile. There is a healed scar on each side of the neck. The liver edge is tender and is palpated 3 cm below the right costal margin. Abdominal CT shows a 4-cm multiloculated hepatic abscess. Percutaneous drainage yields thick yellow pus. Laboratory results are shown. Which of the following is the most appropriate test to confirm the underlying disorder?

- A. Flow cytometry for CD18 expression on neutrophils
- B. Dihydrorhodamine 123 flow cytometry of stimulated neutrophils
- C. Serum IgG subclass concentrations
- D. Flow cytometry for CD19-positive B cells
- E. Serum IgE concentration

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: B

- **정답근거**: 3단계. ① S. aureus 간농양·Serratia 림프절염·항문 주위 농양 반복 + 외삼촌 곰팡이 폐렴 → **카탈라아제 양성균에 취약한 식세포 살균 결손(X 연관)**. ② 탯줄 9일 분리·고름 형성·습진 없음·면역글로불린 정상 → LAD·고-IgE·체액 결손 가능성 낮음 → **CGD**. ③ NADPH oxidase 결손 → **DHR 123 유세포 검사**로 산화 폭발이 없음을 확인.
- **오답감별**:
  - (A) CD18 유세포는 LAD-1 검사다 — 탯줄이 9일에 떨어졌고 고름이 형성돼 호중구 이동은 정상이다.
  - (C) IgG 아형 결핍은 피막 세균의 부비동·폐 감염을 일으키고 간농양·Serratia 는 설명하지 못한다. IgG 는 오히려 높다.
  - (D) B 세포 수(CD19)는 X 연관 무감마글로불린혈증 검사다 — 면역글로불린이 정상~높아 맞지 않는다.
  - (E) 혈청 IgE 는 고-IgE 증후군 검사다 — 습진이 없고, 원인균·가족력은 CGD 쪽이다.
- **임상핵심**: **카탈라아제 양성균의 반복 심부 농양 + 고름은 잘 생김 → CGD → DHR 123 유세포(산화 폭발 없음).**
