## 문제
A 15-year-old boy is brought to the physician because of episodes of burning pain in his hands and feet for 3 years. The pain begins during soccer games, on hot days, or when he has a fever and lasts several hours. He says he barely sweats and feels overheated sooner than his teammates. His maternal grandfather began dialysis for kidney failure at the age of 45 years and died of a stroke at 51 years. His mother has occasional burning in her feet. The patient's vital signs are shown. There are clusters of small, dark red, nonblanching papules over the lower abdomen, buttocks, and scrotum. Slit-lamp examination shows faint whorl-shaped opacities in both corneas. The facial features are normal. The liver and spleen are not palpable. Joint mobility is normal. Neurologic examination shows normal strength, reflexes, and sensation to light touch. Laboratory results are shown. Accumulation of which of the following substances is most likely responsible for this patient's condition?

- A. Glucocerebroside
- B. Globotriaosylceramide
- C. Sphingomyelin
- D. GM2 ganglioside
- E. Dermatan sulfate and heparan sulfate

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: B

- **정답근거**: 3단계. ① **유발되는 손발 작열통 + 땀 감소 + 수영복 부위 비퇴색 적색 구진 + 소용돌이 각막 + 단백뇨** → Fabry 병. ② 외조부의 이른 신부전·뇌졸중, 어머니의 가벼운 증상 → **X 연관**(Hunter 는 얼굴·관절·간비 소견이 없어 가능성 낮음). ③ **α-galactosidase A 결핍 → globotriaosylceramide(Gb3) 축적**.
- **오답감별**:
  - (A) Glucocerebroside — Gaucher 병(β-glucocerebrosidase). 간비종대·혈구감소·뼈 통증이 핵심인데 모두 없다.
  - (C) Sphingomyelin — Niemann-Pick 병. 영아기 간비종대·신경 퇴행·체리색 반점이다.
  - (D) GM2 ganglioside — Tay-Sachs 병(hexosaminidase A). 영아기 신경 퇴행·체리색 반점, 간비종대 없음 — 10대의 작열통·단백뇨와 맞지 않는다.
  - (E) Dermatan·heparan sulfate — Hunter 증후군(iduronate-2-sulfatase), 같은 X 연관이지만 거친 얼굴·관절 강직·간비종대가 없다.
- **임상핵심**: **X 연관 리소좀 질환은 Fabry 와 Hunter 둘** — 작열통·무한증·혈관각화종·소용돌이 각막·단백뇨(뒤에 신부전·뇌졸중·심근비대)는 **Fabry = α-galactosidase A ↓ → Gb3 축적**.
- **출처**: Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine, 22e. 429장 Lysosomal Storage Diseases.
