## 문제
A 26-year-old woman comes to the emergency department 5 hours after the sudden onset of severe right lower abdominal pain that began while she was lifting weights at the gym. The pain was intermittent at first and has become constant; she has vomited three times. Her last menstrual period was 2 weeks ago, and her menses are regular. She is sexually active with one male partner, and they use condoms consistently. She has no vaginal discharge or dysuria. Her vital signs are shown. The abdomen is tender in the right lower quadrant with voluntary guarding and no rebound. Pelvic examination shows a tender, mobile right adnexal mass; there is no cervical motion tenderness or cervical discharge. Laboratory results are shown. Transvaginal ultrasonography shows a 7-cm echogenic right ovarian cyst with an enlarged right ovary; color Doppler shows arterial and venous flow in the right ovary. The appendix is not visualized, and there is a small amount of free fluid in the pelvis. Which of the following is the most appropriate next step in management?

- A. CT scan of the abdomen and pelvis with contrast
- B. Diagnostic laparoscopy
- C. Intravenous cefoxitin and oral doxycycline
- D. Repeat transvaginal ultrasonography in 6 weeks
- E. Oral combined hormonal contraceptive therapy

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: B

- **정답근거**: 3단계 추론. ① 운동 중 갑작스러운 한쪽 통증(간헐 → 지속) + 구토 + 7 cm 난소 낭종 → **부속기 염전 의심**. ② hCG 음성 + 발열·자궁경부 운동 압통·분비물 없음 → 자궁외 임신·골반염 가능성 낮음. ③ 도플러 혈류 정상은 염전을 배제하지 못함 → **진단적 복강경**(해염·난소 보존).
- **오답감별**:
  - (A) CT 는 난소 염전을 정확히 가르지 못하고 수술을 늦춘다 — 복강경이 염전과 충수를 함께 확인한다.
  - (C) cefoxitin + doxycycline 은 골반염 처치 — 발열·자궁경부 운동 압통·분비물이 없다.
  - (D) 6주 뒤 재검은 증상이 가벼운 단순 기능성 낭종의 처치 — 갑작스러운 통증·구토·7 cm 낭종은 염전 소견이고 혈류는 배제 근거가 아니다.
  - (E) 경구 피임제는 새 기능성 낭종을 예방할 뿐 기존 낭종·염전을 치료하지 못한다.
- **임상핵심**: **갑작스러운 한쪽 골반 통증 + 구토 + ≥ 5 cm 부속기 종괴 → 도플러 혈류와 무관하게 진단적 복강경(해염·난소 보존).**
- **출처**: ACOG Committee Opinion No. 783: Adnexal Torsion in Adolescents(2019). Williams Gynecology, 4e(Adnexal torsion).
