## 문제
A 3-year-old boy is brought to the emergency department 4 hours after his mother found him with an open bottle of her prenatal vitamins with iron; she estimates that up to 30 tablets are missing, each containing 65 mg of elemental iron. He weighs 14 kg. Since then, he has vomited five times, most recently with streaks of blood, and has had two episodes of bloody diarrhea. He is now lethargic. His vital signs are shown. Capillary refill time is 4 seconds. The abdomen is diffusely tender without guarding. Laboratory results are shown. An x-ray of the abdomen shows no radiopaque tablets in the stomach or intestines. After a 20-mL/kg bolus of 0.9% saline, his blood pressure is 80/46 mm Hg. Which of the following is the most appropriate next step in management?

- A. Activated charcoal by nasogastric tube
- B. Intravenous deferoxamine infusion
- C. Whole-bowel irrigation with polyethylene glycol
- D. Gastric lavage with sodium bicarbonate solution
- E. Hemodialysis to remove circulating iron

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: B

- **정답근거**: 3단계. ① 원소 철 약 139 mg/kg + 출혈성 구토·혈변 → **철 중독**. ② 수액 뒤에도 쇼크 + HCO3 12 + 혈청 철 640 + 기면 → **전신 중증 중독 → 킬레이션 적응**. ③ X선에 정제 없음, 활성탄·투석 무효 → **IV deferoxamine**.
- **오답감별**:
  - (A) 활성탄은 철에 붙지 않는다.
  - (C) 장세척은 X선에 정제가 보일 때의 보조 처치 — 정제가 없고 이미 전신 중독이다.
  - (D) 위세척은 이득이 없고 흡인·천공 위험이 있다.
  - (E) 철은 투석으로 제거되지 않는다.
- **임상핵심**: **철 중독: 쇼크·대사산증·의식 변화 또는 혈청 철 > 500 μg/dL → IV deferoxamine** · 활성탄 무효 · X선에 정제가 보이면 장세척을 더한다.
- **출처**: Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine, 22e. 470장 Poisoning and Drug Overdose.
