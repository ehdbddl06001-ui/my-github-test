## 문제
A 9-year-old boy is brought to the physician because of another skin abscess on his neck. Since infancy he has had more than 10 abscesses on the scalp, neck and axillae; his parents say they were swollen and full of pus but barely warm and not very painful. Incision and drainage has repeatedly yielded pus that grew Staphylococcus aureus. He has had an itchy, scaly rash on his face and trunk since the age of 2 weeks. He has had pneumonia twice; after the second episode, CT of the chest showed thin-walled, air-filled cysts in the right lung that persist 6 months later. There was no delay in separation of the umbilical cord. Examination shows a 2-cm fluctuant, nontender mass on the posterior neck with little surrounding redness, lichenified eczematous plaques, and a broad nasal bridge. Two primary incisors are still in place, with the permanent incisors erupting behind them. There are no petechiae, and his hair and skin are normally pigmented. Laboratory results are shown. A defect in which of the following is most likely responsible for this patient's susceptibility to infection?

- A. STAT3-dependent differentiation of IL-17–producing helper T cells
- B. NADPH oxidase–dependent oxidative burst in phagocytes
- C. CD18-dependent adhesion of neutrophils to vascular endothelium
- D. WASP-dependent actin reorganization in lymphocytes and platelets
- E. LYST-dependent trafficking of lysosomes in phagocytes

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: A

- **정답근거**: 3단계 추론. ① 습진 + 반복 감염이지만 혈소판 수·크기 정상 → Wiskott-Aldrich 증후군 가능성 낮음, DHR 정상 → 만성 육아종병 가능성 낮음. ② 차가운 황색포도알균 농양 + 폐렴 뒤 공기 낭 + 젖니 잔존 + IgE 5,600·호산구 증가 → **상염색체 우성 고IgE 증후군**. ③ STAT3 기능 소실 → **Th17 분화 결손** → IL-17·IL-22 부족 → 피부·기도 상피의 호중구 동원·항균 펩타이드 감소.
- **오답감별**:
  - (B) 만성 육아종병도 황색포도알균 농양을 반복하지만 DHR 검사가 비정상이고 농양이 뜨겁고 육아종을 만든다 — DHR 이 정상이다.
  - (C) 백혈구 부착 결손증은 호중구가 혈관 밖으로 못 나가 고름이 없고 탯줄 탈락이 늦다 — 이 환자의 농양에는 고름이 차 있고 탯줄 탈락은 정상이었다.
  - (D) Wiskott-Aldrich 증후군은 습진 + 반복 감염에 작은 혈소판을 동반한 혈소판 감소가 있다 — 혈소판 수·평균 혈소판 용적이 정상이다.
  - (E) Chediak-Higashi 증후군은 부분 백색증·거대 과립·신경병증을 동반한다 — 색소가 정상이다.
- **임상핵심**: **차가운 포도알균 농양 + 폐 공기 낭 + 젖니 잔존 + IgE 극상승 = STAT3 결손 → Th17(IL-17) 결핍 → 호중구 동원 실패.**
- **출처**: Robbins & Cotran 10e — primary immune deficiencies; Janeway's Immunobiology 9e — inherited immunodeficiency diseases; Harrison's 21e — primary immune deficiency diseases.
