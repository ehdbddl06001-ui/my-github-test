## 문제
A 5-month-old girl is brought to the physician because of 2 episodes of sweating, irritability and jerking of the arms over the past week, each occurring about 3 to 4 hours after a feeding. She is breastfed every 2 to 3 hours during the day, and the episodes began after she started sleeping through the night. She was born at term after an uncomplicated pregnancy. Her vital signs are shown. She has a round face with full cheeks and is at the 15th percentile for length. The liver edge is firm and palpable 6 cm below the right costal margin; the spleen is not palpable. Both kidneys are palpable. Muscle tone and reflexes are normal. Laboratory studies obtained 4 hours after her last feeding are shown. On another day, intramuscular glucagon is given 2 hours after a feeding; serum glucose does not rise over the next 30 minutes, while serum lactate increases further. A deficiency of the enzyme that catalyzes which of the following reactions is the most likely cause of this patient's condition?

- A. Glucose 6-phosphate → glucose
- B. Glycogen α-1,6 branch → free glucose
- C. Glycogen → glucose 1-phosphate
- D. Fructose 1,6-bisphosphate → fructose 6-phosphate
- E. Pyruvate → oxaloacetate

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: A

- **정답근거**: 3단계 추론. ① 수유 3~4시간 뒤 저혈당 + 젖산산증 → **글리코겐 분해와 포도당신생합성이 모두 포도당을 내지 못함**. ② 고요산·고중성지방 + 간비대·만져지는 콩팥 + CK 정상 → **간·신장 글리코겐 축적(GSD I)**. ③ 식후 글루카곤에 포도당 무반응·젖산 상승 → 글리코겐이 G6P 에서 멈춘다 → **glucose-6-phosphatase(G6P → 포도당) 결핍**.
- **오답감별**:
  - (B) 탈분지효소 결핍(Cori)은 간비대·저혈당·고중성지방이 겹치지만 젖산이 정상이고 식후 글루카곤에 포도당이 오른다.
  - (C) 간 포스포릴레이스 결핍(Hers)은 가벼운 저혈당·간비대뿐이고 젖산산증·고요산혈증이 없다.
  - (D) FBPase 결핍은 젖산산증·공복 저혈당을 주지만 글리코겐 분해는 정상이라 식후 글루카곤에 반응하고 간·신장 글리코겐 축적이 없다.
  - (E) 피루브산 카복실레이스 결핍은 젖산산증이 있지만 신경 발달 이상이 두드러지고, 글리코겐 분해가 정상이라 글루카곤에 반응한다.
- **임상핵심**: **짧은 공복 저혈당 + 젖산·요산·중성지방 상승 + 간·신장비대 + 글루카곤 무반응 → glucose-6-phosphatase(GSD I) — 글리코겐 분해와 신생합성의 공통 출구가 막혔다.**
- **출처**: Kishnani PS, et al. ACMG practice guideline for GSD type I (Genet Med 2014;16(11):e1); Lehninger Principles of Biochemistry — glycogen metabolism.
