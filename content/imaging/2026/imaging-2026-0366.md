## 문제
A 24-year-old man comes to the physician because of shortness of breath and chest tightness when climbing stairs or running for 6 months. He has never fainted, and no family member has died suddenly. Pulse is 68/min, and blood pressure is 122/72 mm Hg. A grade 3/6 systolic ejection murmur is heard at the left lower sternal border; it becomes louder with the Valsalva maneuver and on standing and softer with squatting. Jugular venous pressure is normal, and the lungs are clear. An ECG shows left ventricular hypertrophy. Echocardiography shows an interventricular septal thickness of 19 mm, a posterior wall thickness of 10 mm, systolic anterior motion of the anterior mitral leaflet, a resting left ventricular outflow tract gradient of 55 mm Hg, and an ejection fraction of 70%. Which of the following is the most appropriate initial pharmacotherapy for his symptoms?

- A. Digoxin
- B. Metoprolol
- C. Nitroglycerin
- D. Furosemide
- E. Amlodipine

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: B

- **정답 핵심**: Exertional dyspnea and chest tightness in a young man, a systolic murmur that increases with Valsalva and standing and decreases with squatting, asymmetric septal hypertrophy (19 vs 10 mm), systolic anterior motion of the mitral valve, and an outflow gradient of 55 mm Hg establish obstructive hypertrophic cardiomyopathy. First-line therapy is a beta-blocker, which slows the heart rate to lengthen diastolic filling and reduces contractility, relieving outflow obstruction.
- **원리**: <b>Why the murmur changes with maneuvers</b>: obstruction in hypertrophic cardiomyopathy occurs between the thick septum and the anterior mitral leaflet that is pulled forward in systole (systolic anterior motion). The <b>smaller the left ventricular cavity</b>, the closer they come and the worse the obstruction. Valsalva and standing reduce venous return and shrink the cavity, so the murmur gets louder; squatting increases venous return and afterload, enlarging the cavity and softening the murmur.<br> <b>Why a beta-blocker first</b>: slowing the heart lengthens diastole so the ventricle fills more (larger cavity), and reduced contractility lowers outflow velocity and the drag on the mitral leaflet, decreasing the exercise gradient and symptoms. If insufficient, verapamil, disopyramide, or the cardiac myosin inhibitor mavacamten is added.<br> <b>Drugs to avoid</b>: vasodilators, nitrates, diuretics, and digoxin reduce preload or afterload or increase contractility and worsen obstruction.
- **비교**: <table><thead><tr><th style="width:22%">Feature</th><th style="width:39%">Metoprolol (answer)</th><th>Nitroglycerin (closest distractor)</th></tr></thead><tbody> <tr><td>Left ventricular cavity</td><td><b>Enlarges</b> with longer diastolic filling</td><td>Shrinks as preload falls</td></tr> <tr><td>Outflow gradient</td><td>Decreases</td><td>Increases — risk of syncope</td></tr> <tr><td>Chest pain it treats</td><td>Obstructive hypertrophic cardiomyopathy</td><td>Angina from coronary stenosis</td></tr> </tbody></table> For exertional chest pain with a dynamic murmur and systolic anterior motion, nitrates are contraindicated; for chest pain from coronary stenosis, nitroglycerin would be correct.
- **오답 이유**:
  - (A) Digoxin would be correct for rate control of atrial fibrillation in heart failure with reduced ejection fraction; it increases contractility and worsens obstruction.
  - (C) Nitroglycerin would be correct for stable angina from coronary stenosis; here it lowers preload and worsens the outflow obstruction.
  - (D) Furosemide would be correct for heart failure with reduced ejection fraction and pulmonary congestion; in this disease it shrinks the cavity and worsens obstruction.
  - (E) Amlodipine would be correct for hypertension or vasospastic angina; as a peripheral vasodilator it lowers afterload and increases the outflow gradient.
- **함정**: Treating exertional chest pain with antianginal vasodilators — a murmur that changes with maneuvers and systolic anterior motion point to outflow obstruction.
- **학습목표**: 발살바 수기·선 자세에서 커지고 쪼그려 앉으면 작아지는 수축기 잡음과 비대칭 중격 비대·승모판 수축기 전방운동으로 폐쇄성 비후성 심근병증을 진단하고, 증상 조절의 1차 약으로 베타차단제를 고른다
