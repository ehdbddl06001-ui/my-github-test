## 문제
A 54-year-old man with alcohol-associated cirrhosis is brought to the emergency department 2 hours after vomiting a large amount of bright red blood. His blood pressure is 92/58 mm Hg, and pulse is 118/min. He is alert. Abdominal examination shows shifting dullness. Hemoglobin concentration is 7.1 g/dL, platelet count is 78,000/mm3, and INR is 1.8. Two large-bore intravenous catheters are placed, packed red blood cells are transfused to a target hemoglobin of 7 to 8 g/dL, and an intravenous octreotide infusion is started. Endoscopy is planned within 12 hours. Which of the following should also be administered now?

- A. Oral propranolol
- B. Intravenous vitamin K
- C. Intravenous ceftriaxone
- D. Intravenous tranexamic acid
- E. Fresh frozen plasma to correct the INR

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: C

- **정답 핵심**: A patient with cirrhosis and ascites who has upper gastrointestinal bleeding most likely has variceal hemorrhage. He has received restrictive transfusion and a vasoactive drug, and endoscopy is planned. The remaining evidence-based step is short-term antibiotic prophylaxis, preferably intravenous ceftriaxone 1 g daily for up to 7 days in advanced cirrhosis. Bacterial infection develops in many cirrhotic patients with gastrointestinal bleeding and is linked to rebleeding and death; prophylaxis lowers infection, rebleeding, and mortality.
- **원리**: <b>Why bleeding cirrhotic patients become infected</b>: Portal hypertension makes the intestinal wall more permeable, and gut bacteria translocate to mesenteric lymph nodes and ascites. Bleeding and hypotension worsen intestinal ischemia, and cirrhosis impairs neutrophil and complement function. Infection then releases cytokines and endotoxin that raise portal pressure and impair hemostasis, which explains its link with early rebleeding.<br> <b>Why ceftriaxone</b>: The usual pathogens are gut gram-negative bacilli. In advanced cirrhosis and in areas with quinolone resistance, intravenous ceftriaxone was more effective than oral norfloxacin.<br> <b>Why the other treatments do not help</b>: The INR in cirrhosis does not reflect bleeding risk because both procoagulant and anticoagulant factors are reduced; plasma adds volume and raises portal pressure. Tranexamic acid did not reduce death in gastrointestinal bleeding and increased venous thrombosis. Beta-blockers lower blood pressure in acute bleeding and are started only after the bleeding is controlled.
- **비교**: <table><thead><tr><th style="width:24%">Feature</th><th style="width:38%">Intravenous ceftriaxone (answer)</th><th>Fresh frozen plasma (closest distractor)</th></tr></thead><tbody> <tr><td>Target</td><td>Bacterial infection from translocation</td><td>Elevated INR</td></tr> <tr><td>Evidence</td><td><b>Lower infection, rebleeding, and mortality</b></td><td>No benefit; volume load raises portal pressure</td></tr> <tr><td>Why the INR misleads</td><td>—</td><td>Rebalanced hemostasis: anticoagulant proteins are also low</td></tr> <tr><td>Timing</td><td>At admission, before endoscopy</td><td>Not recommended routinely</td></tr> </tbody></table> In variceal bleeding, the core bundle is restrictive transfusion, a vasoactive drug, antibiotics, and endoscopy within 12 hours; correcting laboratory numbers is not part of it.
- **오답 이유**:
  - (A) Oral propranolol would be correct after the acute bleed is controlled, as secondary prophylaxis combined with endoscopic band ligation.
  - (B) Intravenous vitamin K would help if the prolonged INR were from vitamin K deficiency, such as cholestasis or poor intake, not from reduced hepatic synthesis.
  - (D) Intravenous tranexamic acid would be chosen only in a trial setting; in gastrointestinal bleeding it did not lower mortality and increased venous thrombosis.
  - (E) Fresh frozen plasma would be considered only for a specific factor deficiency or warfarin-associated bleeding; in cirrhosis the INR does not predict bleeding.
- **함정**: Treating the INR of 1.8 with plasma — in cirrhosis the INR does not measure bleeding risk, whereas antibiotics reduce mortality.
- **학습목표**: 간경변 환자의 급성 정맥류 출혈에서 소생과 옥트레오타이드에 더해 예방적 항생제(세프트리악손)를 고르고 트라넥삼산·혈장 교정과 가른다
