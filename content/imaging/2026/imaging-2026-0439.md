## 문제
A 28-year-old man is admitted to the hospital because of a rapidly enlarging abdominal mass and night sweats for 3 weeks. CT shows bulky mesenteric and retroperitoneal lymphadenopathy, and biopsy shows Burkitt lymphoma. He is scheduled to begin chemotherapy tomorrow. His pulse is 98/min, and blood pressure is 124/76 mm Hg. Serum studies show LDH 2,850 U/L, uric acid 11.2 mg/dL, potassium 4.9 mEq/L, phosphorus 4.8 mg/dL, calcium 9.0 mg/dL, and creatinine 1.6 mg/dL (1.0 mg/dL 2 weeks ago). Glucose-6-phosphate dehydrogenase activity is normal. His urine output is 50 mL/h with intravenous fluids. Which of the following is the most appropriate pharmacotherapy to start in addition to aggressive hydration?

- A. Rasburicase
- B. Allopurinol
- C. Febuxostat
- D. Sodium bicarbonate infusion
- E. Probenecid

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: A

- **정답 핵심**: Bulky Burkitt lymphoma with a very high LDH is the highest-risk setting for tumor lysis syndrome, and he already has hyperuricemia (11.2 mg/dL) and acute kidney injury before chemotherapy. Rasburicase, a recombinant urate oxidase, rapidly converts existing uric acid to soluble allantoin; G6PD deficiency has been excluded, so it can be given safely.
- **원리**: <b>Where uric acid comes from</b>: lysed tumor cells release nucleic acids; purines are broken down to hypoxanthine, then xanthine, then uric acid by xanthine oxidase. Humans lack urate oxidase (uricase), so uric acid is the end product and precipitates in the acidic distal tubule, causing obstructive and inflammatory kidney injury.<br> <b>Why rasburicase here</b>: allopurinol and febuxostat block xanthine oxidase and prevent new uric acid formation, but they do nothing to uric acid already present and take days to lower levels. Rasburicase supplies the missing enzyme, converting uric acid to allantoin, which is 5–10 times more soluble, and lowers levels within hours — the priority when uric acid is already high and kidney function is falling.<br> <b>Why check G6PD</b>: the reaction produces hydrogen peroxide; in G6PD deficiency, red cells cannot regenerate glutathione and develop hemolysis and methemoglobinemia.
- **비교**: <table><thead><tr><th style="width:22%">Feature</th><th style="width:39%">Rasburicase (answer)</th><th>Allopurinol (closest distractor)</th></tr></thead><tbody> <tr><td>Mechanism</td><td><b>Degrades existing uric acid to allantoin</b></td><td>Blocks new uric acid formation</td></tr> <tr><td>Onset</td><td>Hours</td><td>Days; existing urate unchanged</td></tr> <tr><td>Best setting</td><td>High-risk TLS, uric acid ≥ 8, or AKI</td><td>Low–intermediate risk prophylaxis</td></tr> <tr><td>Contraindication</td><td>G6PD deficiency</td><td>Caution with azathioprine</td></tr> </tbody></table> Prevention of new urate is not enough when the urate that is already there is injuring the kidney.
- **오답 이유**:
  - (B) Allopurinol would be correct for intermediate-risk prophylaxis with normal uric acid and kidney function, or when rasburicase is contraindicated by G6PD deficiency.
  - (C) Febuxostat is an alternative to allopurinol for prophylaxis in patients with allopurinol hypersensitivity; it also does not remove existing uric acid.
  - (D) Bicarbonate alkalinization is no longer recommended: it promotes calcium phosphate precipitation and xanthine stones, and would be considered only with metabolic acidosis.
  - (E) Probenecid increases urinary uric acid excretion and is used for gout in underexcretors; in tumor lysis it would increase tubular uric acid precipitation.
- **함정**: Choosing allopurinol, the familiar prophylactic — it does not lower uric acid that is already high, and this patient already has kidney injury.
- **학습목표**: 항암 시작 전 이미 고요산혈증과 급성 신손상이 있는 고위험 종양용해증후군에서 G6PD 정상을 확인하고 라스부리카제를 고른다
