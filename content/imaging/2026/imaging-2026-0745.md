## 문제
A 71-year-old woman with atrial fibrillation who has taken warfarin for 3 years comes to the clinic because of easy bruising for 3 days. Her INR has been stable between 2.0 and 3.0 for the past year. Eight days ago she began a 7-day course of trimethoprim-sulfamethoxazole for cystitis. She has not changed her diet and takes no other new medications or supplements. Blood pressure is 132/78 mm Hg. There are several ecchymoses on the forearms. Serum creatinine and liver enzymes are within the reference ranges. Her INR is now 6.4. Which of the following best explains the change in her INR?

- A. Inhibition of P-glycoprotein in the intestine
- B. Inhibition of hepatic cytochrome P450 2C9
- C. Induction of hepatic cytochrome P450 3A4
- D. Reduced dietary vitamin K intake
- E. Inhibition of renal tubular secretion

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: B

- **정답 핵심**: Warfarin is a racemic mixture, and the more potent <b>S-warfarin is cleared mainly by CYP2C9</b>. Sulfamethoxazole is a CYP2C9 inhibitor, so S-warfarin accumulates within days and the INR rises far above the target range. Her diet, liver and kidney function, and other drugs are unchanged, and the timing follows the start of trimethoprim-sulfamethoxazole.
- **원리**: <b>왜 S-형이 중요한가</b>: 와파린은 R-형과 S-형이 반씩 섞인 약인데, 비타민 K 에폭사이드 환원효소(VKORC1)를 막는 힘은 <b>S-형이 3~5배 강하다</b>. S-형은 주로 간의 CYP2C9 가, R-형은 CYP3A4·1A2 가 대사한다. 그래서 CYP2C9 를 막는 약(sulfamethoxazole·metronidazole·fluconazole·amiodarone)은 효과가 큰 쪽의 농도를 올려 INR 을 크게 올리고, CYP3A4 만 막는 약은 상대적으로 영향이 작다.<br> <b>왜 며칠 뒤에 나타나는가</b>: 와파린은 이미 만들어진 응고인자를 없애지 않고 새로 만드는 것을 막는다. 대사가 줄어 와파린 농도가 오르면, 반감기가 짧은 VII 인자부터 줄면서 INR 이 2~5일에 걸쳐 오른다. 항생제 시작 일주일 무렵 멍이 드는 것은 이 시간 경과와 맞는다.<br> <b>처치 연결</b>: 출혈이 없거나 경미한 INR 4.5~10 은 와파린을 한두 번 거르고 INR 을 다시 재며, 출혈 위험이 높으면 경구 비타민 K 소량을 더한다. 이런 상호작용을 피하려면 와파린 복용자의 방광염에는 nitrofurantoin 이나 fosfomycin 같은 대안을 고려한다.
- **비교**: <table><thead><tr><th style="width:24%">Feature</th><th style="width:38%">CYP2C9 inhibition (answer)</th><th>CYP3A4 induction (closest mechanism distractor)</th></tr></thead><tbody> <tr><td>Effect on warfarin level</td><td><b>↑ S-warfarin</b> (potent enantiomer)</td><td>↓ R-warfarin (and some S)</td></tr> <tr><td>Effect on INR</td><td><b>Rises</b></td><td>Falls (risk of thrombosis)</td></tr> <tr><td>Typical drugs</td><td>Sulfamethoxazole, metronidazole, fluconazole, amiodarone</td><td>Rifampin, carbamazepine, phenytoin, St John's wort</td></tr> <tr><td>This patient</td><td>INR 6.4 after TMP-SMX — fits</td><td>Would lower the INR — does not fit</td></tr> </tbody></table> Ask first which way the INR moved: up means less metabolism (inhibition) or displaced vitamin K; down means induction or more vitamin K.
- **오답 이유**:
  - (A) Intestinal P-glycoprotein inhibition explains interactions with dabigatran or digoxin, which are P-gp substrates, not warfarin.
  - (C) CYP3A4 induction would explain the change if the INR had fallen after starting a drug such as rifampin or carbamazepine.
  - (D) Reduced vitamin K intake would be correct if she had stopped eating leafy greens or had prolonged anorexia without a new interacting drug.
  - (E) Inhibition of renal tubular secretion explains trimethoprim's rise in serum creatinine and potassium, but warfarin is not cleared by the kidney.
- **함정**: Attributing every antibiotic-warfarin interaction to loss of vitamin K-producing gut flora instead of recognizing CYP2C9 inhibition by sulfamethoxazole.
- **학습목표**: Explain a supratherapeutic INR after trimethoprim-sulfamethoxazole in a patient on warfarin by inhibition of CYP2C9, which metabolizes the more potent S-enantiomer
