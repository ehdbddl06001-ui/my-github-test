## 문제
A 26-year-old woman comes to the physician because of a 6-month history of bloating, intermittent loose stools, and fatigue. She has had three episodes of sinusitis in the past year. She eats a regular diet that includes bread and pasta daily. She has autoimmune thyroiditis treated with levothyroxine. BMI is 19 kg/m2. Abdominal examination shows mild distention without tenderness. Laboratory studies show a hemoglobin concentration of 10.1 g/dL, mean corpuscular volume of 71 fL, and ferritin of 6 ng/mL. Serum tissue transglutaminase IgA antibody is negative. Total serum IgA concentration is less than 5 mg/dL (N = 70-400). Which of the following is the most appropriate next step in diagnosis?

- A. Deamidated gliadin peptide IgG and tissue transglutaminase IgG testing
- B. HLA-DQ2 and HLA-DQ8 genotyping
- C. Trial of a gluten-free diet for 3 months
- D. Fecal calprotectin measurement
- E. Repeat tissue transglutaminase IgA testing in 6 months

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: A

- **정답 핵심**: Bloating, loose stools, and iron deficiency anemia in a young woman with autoimmune thyroiditis suggest celiac disease. Her tissue transglutaminase IgA is negative, but she has selective IgA deficiency (total IgA < 5 mg/dL), which is 10 to 15 times more common in celiac disease and makes every IgA-based test falsely negative. The next step is IgG-based serology (deamidated gliadin peptide IgG and/or tTG-IgG) while she is still eating gluten; if positive, duodenal biopsy confirms the diagnosis.
- **원리**: <b>Why total IgA must be checked</b>: the standard screening test, tTG-IgA, measures an IgA antibody. A patient who cannot make IgA cannot produce a positive result, no matter how active the disease. Selective IgA deficiency affects about 2 % to 3 % of patients with celiac disease, so guidelines recommend measuring total IgA with tTG-IgA or reflexing to IgG tests when IgA is low.<br> <b>Why IgG tests work here</b>: the autoimmune response to gluten still produces IgG against tissue transglutaminase and deamidated gliadin peptides. These IgG tests are less specific in IgA-sufficient patients, but in IgA deficiency they are the screening tests of choice.<br> <b>Why keep gluten in the diet</b>: both serology and the small-bowel biopsy (villous atrophy, crypt hyperplasia, intraepithelial lymphocytes) normalize on a gluten-free diet, so starting the diet before testing can make the diagnosis impossible to confirm. The recurrent sinusitis is a clue to the IgA deficiency itself.
- **비교**: <table><thead><tr><th style="width:22%">Feature</th><th style="width:39%">DGP-IgG / tTG-IgG (answer)</th><th>HLA-DQ2/DQ8 genotyping (closest distractor)</th></tr></thead><tbody> <tr><td>What it shows</td><td>Active immune response to gluten</td><td>Genetic susceptibility only</td></tr> <tr><td>Positive result</td><td>Supports celiac disease → biopsy</td><td>Present in about 30 % to 40 % of the general population</td></tr> <tr><td>Best use</td><td>Screening when IgA is deficient</td><td>To <b>exclude</b> celiac disease when serology and biopsy disagree or the patient is already gluten-free</td></tr> </tbody></table> A negative HLA test makes celiac disease very unlikely, but a positive one cannot diagnose it — the IgG serology is the step that moves the work-up forward.
- **오답 이유**:
  - (B) HLA-DQ2/DQ8 genotyping is useful to rule out celiac disease when serology and biopsy disagree or the patient already avoids gluten; a positive result is common in healthy people.
  - (C) A gluten-free diet trial is not diagnostic and would be appropriate only after the diagnosis is confirmed, because it normalizes serology and histology.
  - (D) Fecal calprotectin would be correct if inflammatory bowel disease were the main concern, with bloody diarrhea or nocturnal symptoms, rather than iron deficiency and bloating.
  - (E) Repeating tTG-IgA would be reasonable only if total IgA were normal and suspicion were low; with IgA deficiency it will remain falsely negative.
- **함정**: Concluding that celiac disease is excluded because tTG-IgA is negative — with total IgA below 5 mg/dL, any IgA-based test is falsely negative.
- **학습목표**: 셀리악병이 의심되는데 tTG-IgA 가 음성이고 총 IgA 가 결핍이면 IgA 검사를 믿을 수 없으므로 IgG 기반 혈청검사(DGP-IgG·tTG-IgG)를 한다
