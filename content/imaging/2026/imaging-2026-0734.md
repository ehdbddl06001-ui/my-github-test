## 문제
A 68-year-old man is brought to the emergency department by his wife because of fever, severe headache, and confusion that began 18 hours ago. He has type 2 diabetes mellitus treated with metformin. He has no drug allergies. His temperature is 39.4°C, pulse is 112/min, and blood pressure is 118/70 mm Hg. He is oriented only to person. Neck flexion is painful and limited. There is no rash. Funduscopic examination shows sharp optic disc margins, and there are no focal neurologic deficits. Blood cultures are obtained. CT of the head shows no abnormalities. Cerebrospinal fluid analysis shows a leukocyte count of 1,850/mm3 (88% neutrophils), glucose of 22 mg/dL (serum 168 mg/dL), and protein of 240 mg/dL. Gram stain is pending. Which of the following is the most appropriate pharmacotherapy?

- A. Ampicillin and gentamicin
- B. Dexamethasone, ceftriaxone, vancomycin, and ampicillin
- C. Dexamethasone, ceftriaxone, and vancomycin
- D. Ceftriaxone alone
- E. Acyclovir alone

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: B

- **정답 핵심**: Fever, headache, confusion, and neck stiffness with a neutrophilic CSF pleocytosis, a CSF:serum glucose ratio of about 0.13, and high protein indicate acute bacterial meningitis. Because he is older than 50 years, empiric therapy must cover Streptococcus pneumoniae (including penicillin-resistant strains), Neisseria meningitidis, and Listeria monocytogenes: vancomycin plus ceftriaxone plus ampicillin. Dexamethasone is given just before or with the first antibiotic dose.
- **원리**: <b>왜 나이가 약을 바꾸는가</b>: 경험적 치료는 「이 나이·이 상황에서 흔한 균」을 모두 덮어야 한다. 폐렴사슬알균과 수막알균은 3세대 세팔로스포린이 덮고, 페니실린·세팔로스포린 내성 폐렴사슬알균 때문에 반코마이신을 더한다. 그런데 <b>리스테리아</b>는 세팔로스포린에 본래 내성이다(페니실린결합단백질이 세팔로스포린에 결합하지 않는다). 세포 매개 면역이 약해지는 50세 이상·임신·면역억제·알코올 의존에서 리스테리아 수막염이 늘기 때문에 이들에게는 <b>암피실린</b>을 더한다. 당뇨병도 위험을 높인다.<br> <b>왜 덱사메타손을 먼저</b>: 항생제가 세균을 터뜨리면 세포벽 조각이 대량으로 풀려 염증이 갑자기 커지고, 이것이 뇌부종·청력 손실·사망을 늘린다. 첫 항생제 직전이나 함께 덱사메타손을 주면 이 염증 폭발을 줄인다 — 폐렴사슬알균 수막염에서 사망률·후유증 감소가 입증되었다.<br> <b>CSF 해석</b>: 세균은 포도당을 소비하고 혈액-뇌 장벽의 포도당 수송을 방해해 CSF 포도당 비(< 0.4)가 낮아지고, 호중구가 우세하다. 바이러스 수막염은 림프구 우세에 포도당이 정상이다.

- **오답 이유**:
  - (A) Ampicillin and gentamicin would be correct for neonatal meningitis caused by group B streptococci, Escherichia coli, or Listeria.
  - (C) Dexamethasone, ceftriaxone, and vancomycin would be correct for an immunocompetent adult between 2 and 50 years of age, in whom Listeria is uncommon.
  - (D) Ceftriaxone alone would be correct only after cultures show a cephalosporin-susceptible meningococcus or pneumococcus, when therapy is narrowed.
  - (E) Acyclovir alone would be correct for herpes simplex encephalitis with a lymphocytic CSF pleocytosis, normal glucose, and temporal lobe findings.
- **함정**: Using the standard adult regimen without checking age and immune status, leaving Listeria uncovered.
- **학습목표**: Choose empiric therapy for community-acquired bacterial meningitis in an adult older than 50 years, adding ampicillin for Listeria to vancomycin and a third-generation cephalosporin with dexamethasone
