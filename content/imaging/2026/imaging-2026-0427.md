## 문제
A 31-year-old woman with Hashimoto thyroiditis comes to the physician because a home pregnancy test was positive this morning. Her last menstrual period was 6 weeks ago. She has taken levothyroxine 100 µg daily for 4 years, and her serum TSH concentration 2 months ago was 1.6 µU/mL. She takes a prenatal vitamin with iron, separated from levothyroxine by 4 hours. She feels well. Her pulse is 76/min and blood pressure is 112/70 mm Hg. The thyroid gland is slightly enlarged and firm without nodules. Serum β-hCG is 14,800 mIU/mL, and transvaginal ultrasonography shows a single intrauterine gestational sac with a yolk sac. Which of the following is the most appropriate next step in management?

- A. Decrease levothyroxine by about 25% to avoid fetal hyperthyroidism
- B. Increase levothyroxine by about 30% now (two extra doses per week)
- C. Continue the current dose and measure TSH in 4 weeks
- D. Switch levothyroxine to liothyronine
- E. Continue the current dose and measure TSH after delivery

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: B

- **정답 핵심**: Thyroxine requirements rise by 25–50% from the first weeks of pregnancy, while the fetus depends entirely on maternal thyroxine until about 12 weeks. A woman with treated hypothyroidism should therefore increase her levothyroxine dose by about 25–30% as soon as pregnancy is confirmed — conveniently by taking two extra daily doses per week — and then check TSH every 4 weeks in the first half of pregnancy.
- **원리**: <b>Why the requirement rises</b>: estrogen increases hepatic production and sialylation of thyroxine-binding globulin, so more T4 is bound and free T4 falls unless production rises; the volume of distribution expands; placental type 3 deiodinase degrades T4; and transplacental transfer supplies the fetus. A normal gland meets this by increasing output (helped by hCG's TSH-like action) — a gland destroyed by Hashimoto thyroiditis cannot.<br> <b>Why act before checking TSH</b>: the fetal thyroid does not produce meaningful hormone until 10–12 weeks, and fetal brain development in the first trimester depends on maternal T4. Maternal hypothyroidism early in pregnancy is linked to miscarriage and poorer neurodevelopment, so waiting 4 weeks for a TSH result leaves the most vulnerable window uncovered.<br> <b>How much and how to follow</b>: an increase of about 25–30% (two extra tablets a week) is standard; TSH is checked every 4 weeks until midgestation, aiming for the lower half of the trimester-specific range, and the dose returns to the prepregnancy level after delivery. Iron and calcium are separated from levothyroxine by 4 hours because they reduce absorption.
- **비교**: <table><thead><tr><th style="width:22%">Feature</th><th style="width:39%">Increase dose now (answer)</th><th>Same dose, TSH in 4 weeks (closest distractor)</th></tr></thead><tbody> <tr><td>Who</td><td><b>Known hypothyroidism on levothyroxine</b></td><td>Euthyroid woman screened in early pregnancy</td></tr> <tr><td>Physiology</td><td>Gland cannot raise output</td><td>Gland can raise output</td></tr> <tr><td>First-trimester fetus</td><td>Protected from the start</td><td>Exposed to possible deficiency for weeks</td></tr> </tbody></table> Monitoring TSH every 4 weeks is still needed — it follows the increase rather than replacing it.
- **오답 이유**:
  - (A) Reducing the dose would fit iatrogenic overtreatment with suppressed TSH; maternal levothyroxine at replacement doses does not cause fetal hyperthyroidism.
  - (C) Keeping the dose and checking TSH in 4 weeks fits a woman with subclinical thyroid autoimmunity who is not on treatment; for treated hypothyroidism it delays the needed increase.
  - (D) Liothyronine crosses the placenta poorly and gives fluctuating T3 levels; levothyroxine is preferred in pregnancy, and switching would be right only in unusual malabsorption.
  - (E) Waiting until after delivery would be correct for a woman who is not pregnant; in pregnancy TSH must be followed every 4 weeks through midgestation.
- **함정**: Waiting for a TSH result because the last value was normal — the requirement rises from the first weeks, so the dose is increased at confirmation of pregnancy.
- **학습목표**: 레보티록신으로 치료 중인 갑상샘저하증 여성이 임신을 확인하면 TSH 결과를 기다리지 않고 용량을 약 25~30 % 늘린다
