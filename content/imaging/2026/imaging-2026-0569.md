## 문제
A 46-year-old woman comes to the physician because of low mood, loss of interest, poor sleep, and difficulty concentrating for 2 months. She is premenopausal and also has frequent hot flashes. Four months ago, she underwent lumpectomy for estrogen receptor-positive invasive ductal carcinoma of the breast and began adjuvant tamoxifen. She takes no other medications. She has no history of mania or suicide attempts and denies suicidal thoughts. Vital signs are within normal limits. Physical examination shows no abnormalities. The physician decides to begin an antidepressant. Which of the following is the most appropriate choice to avoid reducing the effectiveness of her cancer therapy?

- A. Fluoxetine
- B. Bupropion
- C. Duloxetine
- D. Venlafaxine
- E. Paroxetine

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: D

- **정답 핵심**: Tamoxifen is a prodrug: CYP2D6 converts it (via N-desmethyltamoxifen) to endoxifen, the metabolite responsible for most of its antiestrogenic effect. Strong CYP2D6 inhibitors such as paroxetine, fluoxetine, and bupropion lower endoxifen concentrations, and duloxetine is a moderate inhibitor. Venlafaxine is a weak inhibitor of CYP2D6, treats depression, and also reduces hot flashes, so it is preferred in women taking tamoxifen.
- **원리**: <b>Why the liver enzyme decides drug efficacy</b>: Tamoxifen itself binds the estrogen receptor weakly. Its effect depends on hepatic conversion, mainly through CYP2D6, to endoxifen, which has about 30-100 times greater affinity for the receptor and circulates at much higher levels than 4-hydroxytamoxifen. Anything that blocks CYP2D6 lowers endoxifen — a pharmacologic version of being a poor metabolizer.<br> <b>Why some antidepressants and not others</b>: SSRIs and SNRIs differ greatly in their inhibition of CYP2D6. Paroxetine and fluoxetine (with its long-lived metabolite norfluoxetine) are potent inhibitors; bupropion is also strong. Venlafaxine, desvenlafaxine, citalopram, escitalopram, and mirtazapine have weak or minimal effects.<br> <b>Why hot flashes matter</b>: Tamoxifen worsens vasomotor symptoms, and the same serotonergic-noradrenergic agents that are weak CYP2D6 inhibitors (venlafaxine, low-dose escitalopram) reduce hot flashes, solving two problems with one drug.
- **비교**: <table><thead><tr><th style="width:24%">Feature</th><th style="width:38%">Venlafaxine (answer)</th><th>Paroxetine (closest distractor)</th></tr></thead><tbody> <tr><td>CYP2D6 inhibition</td><td><b>Weak</b></td><td>Strong — lowers endoxifen</td></tr> <tr><td>Hot flashes</td><td>Reduces them</td><td>Reduces them (low-dose formulation is approved)</td></tr> <tr><td>Use with tamoxifen</td><td>Preferred</td><td>Avoid</td></tr> </tbody></table> Both drugs treat depression and hot flashes; only their effect on the enzyme that activates tamoxifen separates them.
- **오답 이유**:
  - (A) Fluoxetine would be correct for depression in a patient without CYP2D6-dependent drugs; its long half-life makes it a poor choice with tamoxifen.
  - (B) Bupropion would be correct for depression with fatigue or for smoking cessation in a patient not taking tamoxifen, since it strongly inhibits CYP2D6.
  - (C) Duloxetine would be correct for depression with neuropathic pain, but as a moderate CYP2D6 inhibitor it is less preferred with tamoxifen.
  - (E) Paroxetine would be correct for hot flashes or depression in a woman not taking tamoxifen, or after switching to an aromatase inhibitor in postmenopausal women.
- **함정**: Choosing paroxetine because it treats hot flashes — its strong CYP2D6 inhibition reduces formation of active tamoxifen.
- **학습목표**: 타목시펜은 CYP2D6 로 활성 대사물 엔독시펜이 되므로 강한 CYP2D6 억제제(파록세틴·플루옥세틴)를 피하고 영향이 적은 항우울제를 고른다
