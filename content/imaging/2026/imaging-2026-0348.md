## 문제
A 24-year-old woman of Greek descent comes to the physician for a routine examination before planning a pregnancy. She feels well. Menses occur at regular 28-day intervals and last 4 days with moderate flow. She eats a varied diet including meat. Pulse is 74/min, and blood pressure is 112/70 mm Hg. Examination shows no pallor or splenomegaly. Laboratory studies show a hemoglobin concentration of 11.2 g/dL, mean corpuscular volume of 63 µm3, erythrocyte count of 5.9 million/mm3, and red cell distribution width of 13% (N 11.5-14.5). Serum ferritin concentration is 68 ng/mL. A peripheral blood smear shows microcytosis and target cells. Which of the following is the most appropriate next step in diagnosis?

- A. Bone marrow aspiration with iron stain
- B. Serum lead concentration
- C. Fecal occult blood testing
- D. Hemoglobin electrophoresis
- E. A 3-month trial of oral ferrous sulfate

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: D

- **정답 핵심**: She has a mild microcytic anemia with a very low MCV (63) but a high erythrocyte count (5.9 million), normal RDW, normal ferritin, and target cells — a Mentzer index (MCV/RBC) of about 11, well below 13. This pattern points to thalassemia trait rather than iron deficiency. Hemoglobin electrophoresis showing increased HbA2 (>3.5%) confirms β-thalassemia trait, which matters before pregnancy for partner screening.
- **원리**: <b>Why thalassemia trait makes many small cells</b>: in β-thalassemia trait one β-globin gene is defective, so each red cell has less hemoglobin and is small. Erythropoiesis is otherwise intact, so the marrow makes a normal or increased number of uniformly small cells — a high RBC count with a uniform size distribution (normal RDW).<br> <b>Why iron deficiency looks different</b>: in iron deficiency the marrow cannot make enough cells at all, so the RBC count falls, and new cells become progressively smaller while older cells are normal — the RDW widens. Ferritin is low.<br> <b>Why electrophoresis confirms it</b>: when β chains are scarce, the free α chains pair with δ chains, so HbA2 (α2δ2) rises above 3.5%, often with a modest HbF increase. Iron deficiency can lower HbA2, so iron status should be known before interpreting a borderline result. In α-thalassemia trait the electrophoresis is normal and DNA testing is needed.
- **비교**: <table><thead><tr><th style="width:22%">Feature</th><th style="width:39%">β-thalassemia trait (answer)</th><th>Iron deficiency (closest distractor)</th></tr></thead><tbody> <tr><td>RBC count</td><td><b>High</b> (often &gt;5 million/mm3)</td><td>Low</td></tr> <tr><td>RDW</td><td>Normal — uniformly small cells</td><td>Increased</td></tr> <tr><td>Mentzer index (MCV/RBC)</td><td>&lt;13</td><td>&gt;13</td></tr> <tr><td>Ferritin</td><td>Normal</td><td>Low</td></tr> <tr><td>Confirming test</td><td>Hb electrophoresis: HbA2 &gt;3.5%</td><td>Response to iron; find the cause of loss</td></tr> </tbody></table> A very low MCV with a mild anemia and many cells is thalassemia trait until proven otherwise; iron should not be given empirically.
- **오답 이유**:
  - (A) Bone marrow iron staining is reserved for unclear cases such as suspected sideroblastic anemia with ring sideroblasts, not when ferritin is clearly normal.
  - (B) Serum lead would be the answer for a child or worker with exposure, abdominal pain, and basophilic stippling with microcytic anemia.
  - (C) Fecal occult blood testing would be correct in an older adult or man with iron deficiency to look for gastrointestinal blood loss.
  - (E) An iron trial would be correct if ferritin were low and the RBC count low with a wide RDW, as in iron deficiency from menstrual loss.
- **함정**: Treating every microcytic anemia as iron deficiency — a normal ferritin and high RBC count rule against it, and needless iron can cause overload.
- **학습목표**: 적혈구 수가 많고 RDW 가 정상인 소구성 빈혈에서 철 결핍보다 베타지중해빈혈 형질을 의심하고 혈색소 전기영동(HbA2)으로 확인한다
- **근거·출처**: Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine, 21st ed. Ch. Disorders of hemoglobin — thalassemia syndromes · Mentzer WC. Differentiation of iron deficiency from thalassaemia trait. Lancet 1973;1:882
