## 문제
A 56-year-old man with alcohol-associated cirrhosis is admitted to the hospital because of increasing abdominal girth and mild diffuse abdominal discomfort for 4 days. He stopped drinking alcohol 1 year ago. His medications are spironolactone and furosemide. His temperature is 37.9°C (100.2°F), pulse is 92/min, and blood pressure is 108/64 mm Hg. He is alert and oriented, without asterixis. The abdomen is distended with shifting dullness and mild diffuse tenderness without rigidity. Serum studies show a creatinine concentration of 1.4 mg/dL (baseline 0.9 mg/dL), BUN 34 mg/dL, total bilirubin 4.6 mg/dL, and albumin 2.6 g/dL. Diagnostic paracentesis shows an ascitic fluid neutrophil count of 620/mm3 and total protein 0.9 g/dL; Gram stain shows no organisms. Intravenous cefotaxime is started, and diuretics are held. Which of the following additional interventions is most likely to reduce his risk of renal failure and death?

- A. Oral rifaximin
- B. Addition of intravenous metronidazole
- C. Intravenous albumin on days 1 and 3
- D. Large-volume paracentesis with albumin
- E. Intravenous normal saline at 150 mL/h

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: C

- **정답 핵심**: An ascitic neutrophil count of 620/mm3 without a surgical source is spontaneous bacterial peritonitis. Because creatinine is above 1 mg/dL, BUN above 30 mg/dL, and bilirubin above 4 mg/dL, he is at high risk of hepatorenal kidney injury. Intravenous albumin (1.5 g/kg on day 1 and 1 g/kg on day 3) with cefotaxime reduced renal failure and in-hospital mortality compared with cefotaxime alone.
- **원리**: <b>Why SBP damages the kidneys</b>: in cirrhosis, splanchnic vasodilation already lowers effective arterial blood volume. Infection releases cytokines and nitric oxide that dilate the splanchnic bed further; the renin–angiotensin and sympathetic systems respond with intense renal vasoconstriction, producing hepatorenal-type acute kidney injury in about one-third of patients given antibiotics alone.<br> <b>Why albumin and not saline</b>: albumin stays in the circulation longer than crystalloid and expands effective arterial volume; it also binds endotoxin and nitric oxide and has antioxidant effects. In the landmark trial, albumin with cefotaxime lowered renal impairment from 33% to 10% and in-hospital mortality from 29% to 10%. Benefit was concentrated in patients with creatinine > 1 mg/dL, BUN > 30 mg/dL, or bilirubin > 4 mg/dL.<br> <b>Why stop diuretics and avoid big taps</b>: both reduce effective volume at the moment the kidneys are most vulnerable.
- **비교**: <table><thead><tr><th style="width:22%">Feature</th><th style="width:39%">IV albumin (answer)</th><th>IV normal saline (closest distractor)</th></tr></thead><tbody> <tr><td>Effect on volume</td><td><b>Sustained expansion of effective arterial volume</b></td><td>Mostly redistributes into ascites and edema</td></tr> <tr><td>Trial evidence in SBP</td><td>Less renal failure and death</td><td>No survival benefit; worsens ascites</td></tr> <tr><td>Who benefits most</td><td>Creatinine &gt; 1, BUN &gt; 30, bilirubin &gt; 4</td><td>—</td></tr> </tbody></table> In SBP, the kidney injury comes from vasodilation, not salt depletion — albumin answers the mechanism.
- **오답 이유**:
  - (A) Rifaximin is used to prevent recurrent hepatic encephalopathy; he has no asterixis or confusion, and it does not reduce renal failure in SBP.
  - (B) Metronidazole would be added for secondary peritonitis from a perforation or abscess, suggested by polymicrobial Gram stain or very high ascitic protein and LDH.
  - (D) Large-volume paracentesis is the treatment for tense ascites causing dyspnea; during SBP with rising creatinine it further reduces effective volume.
  - (E) Saline would be reasonable for prerenal injury from vomiting or diarrhea in a patient without ascites; here it accumulates in the peritoneum without protecting the kidney.
- **함정**: Treating the rising creatinine with crystalloid — in SBP the mechanism is splanchnic vasodilation, and only albumin has been shown to prevent renal failure.
- **학습목표**: 자발세균복막염(복수 호중구 ≥ 250/mm3)에서 크레아티닌·빌리루빈이 높으면 항생제에 더해 1일째·3일째 정맥 알부민을 주어 신부전과 사망을 줄인다
