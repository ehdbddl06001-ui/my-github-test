## 문제
A 34-year-old woman who works as an intensive care unit nurse is brought to the emergency department after she was found confused at home; her fingerstick glucose was 32 mg/dL, and she recovered within minutes after intravenous dextrose. She has had three similar episodes in the past 2 months. She has no history of diabetes mellitus and says she takes no medications. Her mother has type 2 diabetes mellitus treated with glipizide and insulin glargine. Blood pressure is 118/74 mm Hg and pulse is 88/min. BMI is 23 kg/m2. Examination is normal. During a supervised fast, symptoms of hypoglycemia occur at 14 hours. Laboratory studies at that time show: glucose 38 mg/dL, insulin 48 µU/mL (N < 3 when glucose < 55 mg/dL), C-peptide 0.1 ng/mL (N > 0.6 if insulin-mediated), proinsulin 2 pmol/L (N > 5 if insulin-mediated), and beta-hydroxybutyrate 0.1 mmol/L. A screen for sulfonylureas and meglitinides is negative. Which of the following is the most likely cause of this patient's hypoglycemia?

- A. Insulin-secreting pancreatic islet cell tumor
- B. Surreptitious ingestion of a sulfonylurea
- C. Autoantibodies against the insulin receptor
- D. Primary adrenal insufficiency
- E. Surreptitious administration of exogenous insulin

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: E

- **정답 핵심**: During the fast she has hypoglycemia with an inappropriately high insulin level and suppressed beta-hydroxybutyrate, so the hypoglycemia is insulin-mediated. The pancreas releases insulin and C-peptide in equal amounts after cleaving proinsulin, so endogenous hyperinsulinism (insulinoma or sulfonylurea) raises C-peptide and proinsulin. Here C-peptide and proinsulin are suppressed and the sulfonylurea screen is negative, which means the insulin came from outside: surreptitious insulin injection, made plausible by her access to insulin at work and at home.
- **원리**: <b>Why C-peptide separates the source</b>: beta cells make proinsulin and cleave it into insulin and C-peptide, secreting them in a 1:1 molar ratio. Commercial insulin contains no C-peptide. Injected insulin lowers glucose and also suppresses the patient's own beta cells, so C-peptide and proinsulin fall while insulin is high — a combination that only exogenous insulin produces.<br> <b>Why ketones matter</b>: insulin inhibits lipolysis and ketogenesis, so insulin-mediated hypoglycemia shows low beta-hydroxybutyrate (< 2.7 mmol/L); non-insulin causes (adrenal insufficiency, starvation) give high ketones. This is the first branch point.<br> <b>Why the drug screen is still needed</b>: sulfonylureas and meglitinides close beta-cell K-ATP channels, so they raise insulin, C-peptide, and proinsulin exactly like an insulinoma; only a drug screen separates them. Insulin autoantibodies (insulin autoimmune syndrome) bind and later release insulin, giving very high insulin with variably elevated C-peptide and positive antibodies, usually postprandially.
- **비교**: <table><thead><tr><th style="width:24%">Finding at hypoglycemia</th><th style="width:38%">Exogenous insulin (correct)</th><th>Insulinoma (closest distractor)</th></tr></thead><tbody> <tr><td>Insulin</td><td>High (often very high)</td><td>High</td></tr> <tr><td>C-peptide</td><td><b>Low</b> (&lt; 0.2 ng/mL)</td><td><b>High</b> (≥ 0.6 ng/mL)</td></tr> <tr><td>Proinsulin</td><td>Low</td><td>High (≥ 5 pmol/L)</td></tr> <tr><td>Sulfonylurea screen</td><td>Negative</td><td>Negative (positive only with drug ingestion)</td></tr> </tbody></table> Read in order: ketones low → insulin-mediated; then C-peptide — low means injected insulin, high means the pancreas (tumor or sulfonylurea, separated by the drug screen).
- **오답 이유**:
  - (A) An insulinoma produces fasting hypoglycemia with high insulin, high C-peptide (≥ 0.6 ng/mL), high proinsulin, and a negative drug screen; it would be the answer if C-peptide were elevated.
  - (B) Sulfonylurea ingestion raises insulin and C-peptide and proinsulin together because it stimulates the beta cell; it becomes the answer with elevated C-peptide and a positive drug screen.
  - (C) Insulin receptor antibodies (type B insulin resistance) usually cause severe insulin resistance with acanthosis nigricans in autoimmune disease; hypoglycemia occurs with suppressed insulin and C-peptide levels.
  - (D) Primary adrenal insufficiency causes hypoglycemia with low insulin and high ketones, with hyperpigmentation, hypotension, and hyponatremia with hyperkalemia — none of which is present.
- **함정**: Seeing a high insulin level and choosing insulinoma — without a matching rise in C-peptide and proinsulin, the insulin did not come from her pancreas.
- **학습목표**: 공복 저혈당에서 인슐린이 높고 C-펩타이드·프로인슐린이 낮으며 설폰요소 선별이 음성이면 외인성 인슐린 투여로 판단하고, 인슐린종·설폰요소 복용과 구분한다
