## 문제
A 34-year-old woman is brought to the emergency department because of confusion and slurred speech for 6 hours. For 3 days, she has had fatigue and easy bruising. She takes no medications. Her temperature is 37.9°C, pulse is 104/min, and blood pressure is 128/80 mm Hg. She is disoriented to time, and there are petechiae on her legs. Laboratory studies show a hemoglobin concentration of 8.1 g/dL, platelet count of 14,000/mm³, reticulocyte count of 6%, lactate dehydrogenase of 1450 U/L, haptoglobin below detection, and creatinine of 1.3 mg/dL. Prothrombin time and activated partial thromboplastin time are within the reference ranges. The direct antiglobulin test is negative, and a peripheral blood smear shows many schistocytes. Which of the following is the most appropriate next step in management?

- A. Hemodialysis
- B. Cryoprecipitate infusion
- C. Plasma exchange and glucocorticoids
- D. Platelet transfusion
- E. Intravenous immune globulin

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: C

- **정답 핵심**: Schistocytes, high LDH, undetectable haptoglobin, and reticulocytosis with a negative direct antiglobulin test indicate microangiopathic hemolytic anemia. With severe thrombocytopenia, neurologic symptoms, fever, and only mild renal impairment — and normal PT and aPTT, which argue against DIC — this is thrombotic thrombocytopenic purpura. Untreated mortality exceeds 90%, so plasma exchange with glucocorticoids starts immediately on clinical suspicion, before the ADAMTS13 result returns (caplacizumab is often added).
- **원리**: <b>Why ADAMTS13 deficiency causes the whole picture</b>: ADAMTS13 cleaves ultralarge von Willebrand factor multimers released from endothelium. When an autoantibody inhibits it, these multimers stay attached and spontaneously bind platelets under the high shear of arterioles and capillaries, forming platelet-rich microthrombi. Platelets are consumed (thrombocytopenia), red cells are sheared crossing the strands (schistocytes, LDH, low haptoglobin), and microthrombi in the brain cause fluctuating neurologic deficits.<br> <b>Why plasma exchange</b>: Exchange removes the anti-ADAMTS13 antibody and the ultralarge multimers while replacing functional ADAMTS13 from donor plasma; glucocorticoids (and rituximab) suppress antibody production; caplacizumab blocks the von Willebrand factor–platelet interaction.<br> <b>Why coagulation times are normal</b>: The clotting cascade is not consumed — this is platelet–von Willebrand factor thrombosis, not fibrin generation, which separates TTP from DIC.<br> <b>Why platelets are withheld</b>: Transfused platelets add fuel to ongoing microthrombosis and have been linked to neurologic deterioration; they are given only for life-threatening bleeding.
- **비교**: <table><thead><tr><th style="width:24%">Feature</th><th style="width:38%">Plasma exchange (answer — TTP)</th><th>Platelet transfusion (closest distractor)</th></tr></thead><tbody> <tr><td>Target</td><td><b>Removes the inhibitor and replaces ADAMTS13</b></td><td>Raises the platelet count temporarily</td></tr> <tr><td>Effect on thrombosis</td><td>Stops microthrombus formation</td><td>May worsen microthrombi</td></tr> <tr><td>Why</td><td>The disease is a missing protease, not a production failure</td><td>Platelets are consumed as fast as they are given</td></tr> <tr><td>When indicated</td><td>Suspected TTP, even before ADAMTS13 results</td><td>Only life-threatening bleeding or an unavoidable procedure</td></tr> <tr><td>This patient</td><td>Schistocytes, neurologic signs — indicated</td><td>Petechiae only, no major bleeding — not indicated</td></tr> </tbody></table> A low platelet count with schistocytes is a thrombotic disease, not a bleeding disease — treat the cause, not the number.
- **오답 이유**:
  - (A) Hemodialysis would be correct for Shiga toxin–associated hemolytic uremic syndrome with oliguric acute kidney injury after bloody diarrhea.
  - (B) Cryoprecipitate would be correct for DIC with prolonged PT and aPTT and a fibrinogen level below 150 mg/dL.
  - (D) Platelet transfusion would be correct for thrombocytopenia from marrow failure or for life-threatening bleeding such as intracranial hemorrhage.
  - (E) Intravenous immune globulin would be correct for immune thrombocytopenia, where the smear shows isolated thrombocytopenia without schistocytes or hemolysis.
- **함정**: Treating the platelet count of 14,000/mm³ with platelets — in TTP that feeds the microthrombi.
- **학습목표**: Recognize thrombotic thrombocytopenic purpura from microangiopathic hemolysis, severe thrombocytopenia, and neurologic symptoms with normal coagulation, and start plasma exchange immediately rather than transfusing platelets
