## 문제
A 67-year-old woman underwent total knee arthroplasty 7 days ago and has been receiving subcutaneous unfractionated heparin for thromboprophylaxis since surgery. Today she reports new swelling and pain of the left calf. Her platelet count was 245,000/mm³ before surgery and is 92,000/mm³ today; hemoglobin is 11.2 g/dL. She has no bleeding, and the prothrombin time and fibrinogen are within reference ranges. Compression ultrasonography shows a thrombus in the left popliteal vein. Creatinine is 0.8 mg/dL. Which of the following is the most appropriate next step in management?

- A. Stop heparin and start intravenous argatroban
- B. Switch to subcutaneous enoxaparin
- C. Stop heparin and start warfarin
- D. Transfuse platelets and continue heparin
- E. Increase the heparin to a therapeutic dose

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: A

- **정답 핵심**: A fall in platelet count of more than 50% that begins 5 to 10 days after starting heparin, accompanied by a new venous thrombosis, with no other cause (no bleeding, normal coagulation tests), gives a high 4Ts score for heparin-induced thrombocytopenia. All heparin must be stopped and a non-heparin anticoagulant such as argatroban (or a direct oral anticoagulant or fondaparinux in stable patients) started at a therapeutic dose, while the PF4–heparin antibody test is sent.
- **원리**: <b>왜 혈소판이 줄면서 혈전이 생기는가</b>: 헤파린은 혈소판에서 나온 혈소판 인자 4(PF4)와 결합해 새로운 항원을 만든다. 일부 환자는 5~10일 뒤 이 복합체에 대한 IgG 항체를 만들고, 항체–PF4–헤파린 복합체가 혈소판의 FcγRIIa 수용체에 붙어 <b>혈소판을 활성화</b>한다. 활성화된 혈소판은 응집·소모되어 수가 줄고, 동시에 미세입자와 트롬빈을 쏟아내 동맥·정맥 혈전을 만든다. 그래서 출혈이 아니라 <b>혈전</b>이 위험이다.<br> <b>왜 저분자량 헤파린도 안 되는가</b>: 저분자량 헤파린도 PF4 와 결합해 같은 항체와 교차 반응한다. 와파린을 바로 시작하면 반감기가 짧은 단백질 C 가 먼저 떨어져 사지 정맥 괴저·피부 괴사가 생길 수 있으므로, 혈소판이 회복(보통 15만 이상)된 뒤 겹쳐서 시작한다.<br> <b>혈소판 수혈</b>은 활성화될 연료를 더하는 것이라 출혈이 없으면 하지 않는다.
- **비교**: <table><thead><tr><th style="width:24%">Feature</th><th style="width:38%">Argatroban (answer)</th><th>Enoxaparin (closest distractor)</th></tr></thead><tbody> <tr><td>Mechanism</td><td>Direct thrombin inhibitor — no heparin structure</td><td>Low-molecular-weight heparin — binds PF4</td></tr> <tr><td>Cross-reactivity with HIT antibody</td><td><b>None</b></td><td><b>Present</b> — can sustain HIT</td></tr> <tr><td>Clearance</td><td>Hepatic (preferred in renal failure)</td><td>Renal</td></tr> <tr><td>Use</td><td>Suspected or confirmed HIT</td><td>Prophylaxis or treatment of VTE without HIT</td></tr> <tr><td>This patient</td><td>High 4Ts score with new DVT — fits</td><td>Would keep the antibody active — does not fit</td></tr> </tbody></table> Once HIT is suspected, every heparin product goes, and anticoagulation continues with a drug that does not bind PF4.
- **오답 이유**:
  - (B) Switching to enoxaparin would be correct for a deep vein thrombosis in a patient with a stable platelet count and no suspicion of heparin-induced thrombocytopenia.
  - (C) Starting warfarin would be correct only after the platelet count has recovered, overlapping with a non-heparin anticoagulant, to avoid venous limb gangrene.
  - (D) Platelet transfusion would be considered only if she had serious bleeding or needed an invasive procedure, and heparin would still be stopped.
  - (E) Increasing heparin to a therapeutic dose would be correct for a new deep vein thrombosis if the platelet count were stable and HIT were not suspected.
- **함정**: Treating the new clot with more heparin, or simply switching to low-molecular-weight heparin, without recognizing the platelet fall as HIT.
- **학습목표**: 헤파린 투여 5~10일에 혈소판이 50 % 넘게 떨어지고 새 혈전이 생기면 헤파린 유도 혈소판감소증으로 보고, 모든 헤파린을 끊고 비헤파린 항응고제(아르가트로반 등)를 시작한다
