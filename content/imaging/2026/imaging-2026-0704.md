## 문제
A 64-year-old man with hypertension and type 2 diabetes mellitus is admitted with crushing substernal chest pain that began 6 hours earlier. An ECG shows ST-segment elevation in leads V1 through V4, and serum troponin I is markedly elevated. Percutaneous intervention is delayed because of a contrast allergy reaction. On hospital day 3 (about 2 to 3 days after the onset of pain), he develops sudden pulseless electrical activity and cannot be resuscitated. At autopsy, the anterior wall of the left ventricle is pale and soft. Microscopic examination of this area is most likely to show which of the following?

- A. Dense collagenous scar with few cells
- B. Wavy myocardial fibers without inflammation
- C. Coagulative necrosis with dense neutrophilic infiltration
- D. Macrophages phagocytosing dead myocytes
- E. Granulation tissue with new capillaries and fibroblasts

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: C

- **정답 핵심**: About 2 to 3 days after infarction, the dead myocytes show coagulative necrosis (preserved outlines, loss of nuclei, hypereosinophilic cytoplasm) and the infarct is heavily infiltrated by neutrophils that arrive from the viable border. Macrophages dominate at 3 to 10 days, granulation tissue at 1 to 2 weeks, and a collagen scar after about 2 months. Wavy fibers are an early change within the first hours.
- **원리**: <b>왜 시간이 조직 소견을 정하는가</b>: 경색 뒤의 변화는 「죽음 → 염증 → 치유」라는 정해진 순서를 따른다. 심근세포가 죽으면 세포 안 단백질이 변성되어 효소가 일하지 못하므로 세포 윤곽이 며칠 동안 남는다 — <b>응고괴사</b>다. 죽은 세포가 내놓는 신호(손상 관련 분자 유형)와 보체가 살아 있는 경계부 혈관에서 <b>호중구</b>를 불러들이고, 이 침윤은 1~3일에 가장 심하다.<br> <b>그다음</b>: 3~7일에는 대식세포가 죽은 세포를 치우며 경색 부위가 가장 물러져 <b>심장 파열</b> 위험이 가장 높다. 1~2주에는 경계부부터 새 모세혈관과 섬유모세포로 된 육아조직이 자라고, 2개월쯤이면 세포가 적은 콜라겐 흉터가 된다.<br> <b>가장 이른 변화</b>: 처음 몇 시간에는 광학현미경 소견이 거의 없고, 죽은 섬유가 옆의 수축하는 섬유에 당겨져 물결 모양(wavy fiber)으로 보일 수 있을 뿐이다. 그래서 「며칠째인가」를 먼저 계산하면 답이 정해진다.

- **오답 이유**:
  - (A) A dense collagenous scar would be correct for an old infarction of 2 months or more, such as one found incidentally at autopsy.
  - (B) Wavy fibers without inflammation would be correct if death had occurred within the first few hours, before neutrophils arrive.
  - (D) Macrophages phagocytosing dead myocytes would be correct if death had occurred about 5 to 7 days after the infarction.
  - (E) Granulation tissue with new capillaries and fibroblasts would be correct if the patient had died about 1 to 2 weeks after the infarction.
- **함정**: Choosing macrophages because the patient died on hospital day 3 without counting from the onset of pain.
- **학습목표**: 심근경색 후 경과 시간에 따른 조직 소견을 알고, 1~3일째에는 응고괴사와 호중구 침윤이 주된 소견임을 안다
