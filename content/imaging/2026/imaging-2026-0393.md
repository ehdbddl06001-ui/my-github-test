## 문제
A 34-year-old man comes to the physician because of a painless lump in his neck that he noticed 1 month ago. He has had episodes of pounding headache with sweating several times a month for the past year. His father died of a stroke during surgery at the age of 45 years and was found to have an adrenal tumor at autopsy. His pulse is 88/min, and blood pressure is 152/96 mm Hg. Examination shows a 2.5-cm firm nodule in the right lobe of the thyroid gland and no cervical lymphadenopathy. Serum TSH is normal, serum calcium is 9.4 mg/dL, and serum calcitonin is markedly increased. Fine-needle aspiration of the nodule shows spindle-shaped cells with amyloid that stain for calcitonin. Germline RET mutation testing has been sent. Which of the following is the most appropriate next step in management?

- A. Radioactive iodine uptake scan
- B. Measurement of serum thyroglobulin
- C. Levothyroxine suppression therapy
- D. Measurement of plasma free metanephrines
- E. Total thyroidectomy with central neck dissection

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: D

- **정답 핵심**: Calcitonin-staining spindle cells with amyloid identify medullary thyroid carcinoma. With episodic headache and sweating, hypertension, and a father who died during surgery with an adrenal tumor, multiple endocrine neoplasia type 2 with pheochromocytoma is likely. A pheochromocytoma must be excluded with plasma free metanephrines before thyroidectomy, because anesthesia and surgery can provoke a fatal hypertensive crisis; if present, it is removed first after alpha blockade.
- **원리**: <b>Why MEN2 links these organs</b>: activating germline mutations of the RET proto-oncogene, a receptor tyrosine kinase expressed in neural crest-derived cells, drive hyperplasia and then neoplasia of thyroid C cells (medullary carcinoma, nearly 100 %), adrenal chromaffin cells (pheochromocytoma, about 50 %), and in type 2A the parathyroid glands.<br> <b>Why the order of surgery matters</b>: a pheochromocytoma releases catecholamines unpredictably; intubation, anesthetic induction, and surgical manipulation can trigger a surge that causes hypertensive crisis, arrhythmia, stroke, or death — the likely story of the father. So in every patient with medullary thyroid carcinoma, plasma free (or urine fractionated) metanephrines are measured before neck surgery; a pheochromocytoma is removed first after 1–2 weeks of alpha blockade.<br> <b>Why thyroid follicular-cell tests do not help</b>: C cells do not trap iodine or make thyroglobulin, so radioiodine and thyroglobulin are useless for medullary carcinoma; calcitonin and CEA are its tumor markers.
- **비교**: <table><thead><tr><th style="width:22%">Feature</th><th style="width:39%">Plasma metanephrines (answer)</th><th>Total thyroidectomy now (closest distractor)</th></tr></thead><tbody> <tr><td>Role</td><td>Screen for pheochromocytoma <b>before</b> any surgery</td><td>Definitive treatment of medullary carcinoma</td></tr> <tr><td>Why first</td><td>Unrecognized pheochromocytoma can cause intraoperative crisis</td><td>Proceeds once metanephrines are normal or the adrenal tumor is removed</td></tr> <tr><td>Clues here</td><td>Episodic headache and sweating, hypertension, father with adrenal tumor</td><td>Calcitonin-positive nodule</td></tr> </tbody></table> Thyroidectomy is the right operation for this nodule; the question is timing, and the adrenal comes first.
- **오답 이유**:
  - (A) A radioiodine uptake scan would be correct for a nodule in a patient with low TSH; medullary carcinoma arises from C cells that do not trap iodine.
  - (B) Serum thyroglobulin would be correct for following papillary or follicular carcinoma after thyroidectomy; calcitonin and CEA are the markers here.
  - (C) Levothyroxine suppression would be considered after surgery for differentiated follicular-cell cancer; C cells are not TSH dependent.
  - (E) Total thyroidectomy with central neck dissection would be correct once plasma metanephrines are normal or after any pheochromocytoma has been removed.
- **함정**: Going straight to thyroidectomy because the nodule is cancer — in medullary thyroid carcinoma, pheochromocytoma must be excluded first.
- **학습목표**: 갑상샘 수질암과 부신 종양 가족력·발작성 고혈압에서 다발성 내분비샘종양 2형을 의심하고, 갑상샘 수술 전에 혈장 메타네프린으로 갈색세포종을 먼저 배제한다
