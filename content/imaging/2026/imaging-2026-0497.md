## 문제
A 45-year-old woman comes to the physician because of recurrent episodes of spinning vertigo over the past 4 months. Each episode lasts 2 to 4 hours and is accompanied by nausea, a roaring sound, and a feeling of fullness in the left ear. Between episodes she feels well, but she thinks her hearing in the left ear has become worse and fluctuates. She has no headache, visual aura, or weakness. Otoscopic examination shows normal tympanic membranes. The Weber test lateralizes to the right ear, and the Rinne test shows air conduction greater than bone conduction bilaterally. Pure-tone audiometry shows a left low-frequency sensorineural hearing loss. Neurologic examination between attacks is normal. Which of the following is the most likely diagnosis?

- A. Benign paroxysmal positional vertigo
- B. Vestibular migraine
- C. Ménière disease
- D. Vestibular schwannoma
- E. Vestibular neuritis

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: C

- **정답 핵심**: Recurrent spontaneous vertigo lasting 20 minutes to 12 hours, with fluctuating low-frequency sensorineural hearing loss, tinnitus, and aural fullness in the same ear, fulfills the criteria for definite Ménière disease. Weber lateralizing to the right with air conduction greater than bone conduction on both sides indicates a left sensorineural loss, confirmed by audiometry. The normal examination between attacks and the absence of headache or aura make a central or migrainous cause less likely.
- **원리**: <b>Why endolymphatic hydrops gives these symptoms</b>: in Ménière disease, endolymph accumulates and distends the membranous labyrinth. In the cochlea, the apex — which encodes low frequencies — is the most compliant part, so distension first impairs low tones and causes a roaring tinnitus and a pressure sensation. When the distended membranes leak or rupture, potassium-rich endolymph mixes with perilymph and transiently depolarizes vestibular hair cells and nerve fibers, producing an attack of vertigo lasting hours until the ionic balance is restored.<br> <b>Why the hearing fluctuates</b>: early on, the hair cells are stressed rather than destroyed, so hearing recovers between attacks; repeated episodes cause permanent loss that later becomes flat.<br> <b>Why the duration matters</b>: seconds point to positional vertigo, hours to Ménière disease or migraine, days of continuous vertigo to vestibular neuritis, and slowly progressive imbalance to a mass.
- **비교**: <table><thead><tr><th style="width:24%">Feature</th><th style="width:38%">Ménière disease (answer)</th><th>Vestibular schwannoma (closest distractor)</th></tr></thead><tbody> <tr><td>Vertigo</td><td><b>Discrete attacks of 20 min to 12 h</b></td><td>Usually mild, chronic unsteadiness — central compensation for slow loss</td></tr> <tr><td>Hearing loss</td><td>Low frequency, fluctuating</td><td>High frequency, slowly progressive, poor speech discrimination</td></tr> <tr><td>Aural fullness</td><td>Prominent during attacks</td><td>Uncommon</td></tr> <tr><td>Key test</td><td>Audiometry over time</td><td>Gadolinium-enhanced MRI of the internal auditory canal</td></tr> </tbody></table> Any asymmetric sensorineural loss still warrants MRI to exclude a schwannoma — but episodic hours-long vertigo with fluctuating low-tone loss points to hydrops.
- **오답 이유**:
  - (A) Benign paroxysmal positional vertigo would be correct if vertigo lasted less than a minute, was triggered by head position, and hearing were normal.
  - (B) Vestibular migraine would be correct with migraine headache, photophobia, or visual aura accompanying the attacks and without progressive one-sided hearing loss.
  - (D) Vestibular schwannoma would be correct with slowly progressive unilateral high-frequency loss, poor speech discrimination, and constant imbalance rather than hours-long attacks.
  - (E) Vestibular neuritis would be correct with a single episode of continuous vertigo lasting days after a viral illness, with normal hearing.
- **함정**: Choosing vestibular schwannoma because of unilateral sensorineural loss and tinnitus — the hours-long attacks and fluctuating low-frequency loss indicate hydrops.
- **학습목표**: 수십 분~수 시간 지속되는 반복 회전성 어지럼, 변동하는 저음역 감각신경성 난청, 이명과 이충만감으로 메니에르병을 진단하고 전정신경집종·전정신경염과 가른다
