## 문제
A 19-year-old man is brought to the emergency department after he fainted while sprinting during basketball practice. He regained consciousness within a minute. He has had exertional chest tightness for several months. His uncle died suddenly at age 32. Blood pressure is 122/74 mm Hg and pulse is 68/min. Cardiac examination shows a grade 3/6 harsh systolic murmur at the left lower sternal border that increases during the strain phase of the Valsalva maneuver and decreases with squatting. The carotid upstroke is brisk. An ECG shows tall QRS voltage in the precordial leads with deep narrow Q waves in leads I and aVL. Which of the following is the most likely diagnosis?

- A. Dilated cardiomyopathy
- B. Hypertrophic cardiomyopathy
- C. Bicuspid aortic valve stenosis
- D. Mitral valve prolapse
- E. Ventricular septal defect

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: B

- **정답 핵심**: Exertional syncope, a family history of sudden death, and a systolic murmur that becomes louder with the strain phase of Valsalva (less preload) and softer with squatting (more preload and afterload) indicate dynamic left ventricular outflow tract obstruction. The brisk carotid upstroke and LVH voltage with septal Q waves fit hypertrophic cardiomyopathy. Aortic stenosis gives a delayed, weak carotid pulse and its murmur softens with Valsalva.
- **원리**: <b>왜 Valsalva 가 비후성 심근병증의 잡음을 키우는가</b>: 비후성 심근병증의 유출로 폐쇄는 고정된 판막 좁아짐이 아니라 <b>심실 크기에 따라 변하는</b> 폐쇄다. 두꺼운 중격과 수축기에 앞으로 끌려오는 승모판 전엽(SAM)이 유출로를 막는데, 좌심실이 작을수록 둘이 더 가까워진다. Valsalva 의 힘주기 단계와 갑자기 일어서기는 정맥 환류를 줄여 좌심실을 작게 만들어 폐쇄와 잡음을 키우고, 쪼그려 앉기·다리 들기·손 쥐기는 전부하·후부하를 늘려 좌심실을 키우므로 잡음이 작아진다.<br> <b>왜 대동맥판 협착은 반대인가</b>: 판막 협착은 구멍 크기가 고정이라 판막을 지나는 혈류량이 많을수록 잡음이 크다 — 전부하를 줄이는 Valsalva 에서는 작아진다. 경동맥 맥박도 협착에서는 느리고 약하게(pulsus parvus et tardus) 오르지만, 비후성 심근병증에서는 수축 초기 박출이 빨라 <b>빠르게</b> 오른 뒤 꺾인다.<br> <b>심전도</b>: 비후된 중격의 탈분극이 I·aVL·V5~V6 의 깊고 좁은 Q파로, 심근량 증가가 높은 QRS 전압으로 나타난다.
- **비교**: <table><thead><tr><th style="width:24%">Feature</th><th style="width:38%">Hypertrophic cardiomyopathy (answer)</th><th>Aortic stenosis (closest distractor)</th></tr></thead><tbody> <tr><td>Valsalva strain / standing</td><td><b>Louder</b> (smaller LV, more obstruction)</td><td>Softer (less flow across a fixed orifice)</td></tr> <tr><td>Squatting / handgrip</td><td>Softer</td><td>Squatting louder</td></tr> <tr><td>Carotid pulse</td><td>Brisk, sometimes bifid</td><td>Weak and delayed</td></tr> <tr><td>Typical patient</td><td>Young, familial, exertional syncope</td><td>Older (calcific) or 40–60 years (bicuspid)</td></tr> <tr><td>This patient</td><td>19 years · louder with Valsalva · brisk upstroke — fits</td><td>Brisk upstroke — does not fit</td></tr> </tbody></table> Dynamic obstruction changes with LV size; a fixed valvular obstruction changes with flow.
- **오답 이유**:
  - (A) Dilated cardiomyopathy would be correct if there were heart failure symptoms, an S3, a displaced apical impulse, and low ejection fraction rather than a loud dynamic murmur.
  - (C) Bicuspid aortic valve stenosis would be correct if the murmur radiated to the carotids, softened with Valsalva, and the carotid upstroke were weak and delayed.
  - (D) Mitral valve prolapse would be correct if a midsystolic click were followed by a late systolic murmur that moves earlier with Valsalva, without LVH or septal Q waves.
  - (E) Ventricular septal defect would be correct if a holosystolic murmur at the left lower sternal border became louder with handgrip rather than with Valsalva.
- **함정**: Assuming every harsh systolic murmur that radiates from the base is aortic stenosis without checking how it responds to preload changes.
- **학습목표**: Valsalva 에서 커지고 쪼그려 앉으면 작아지는 수축기 잡음과 운동 중 실신을 비후성 심근병증으로 진단하고 대동맥판 협착과 가른다
