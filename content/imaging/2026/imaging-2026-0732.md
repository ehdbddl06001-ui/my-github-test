## 문제
A 74-year-old woman is brought to the emergency department 3 hours after the sudden onset of severe, diffuse abdominal pain. She vomited once and had one loose bowel movement. She has atrial fibrillation and stopped taking warfarin 2 months ago because of bruising. Her temperature is 37.2°C, pulse is 118/min and irregularly irregular, and blood pressure is 142/84 mm Hg. She is writhing in pain, but the abdomen is soft and only mildly tender without guarding or rebound. Bowel sounds are present. Leukocyte count is 16,800/mm3, and serum lactate is 3.4 mmol/L. Serum lipase is within the reference range. Which of the following is the most appropriate next step in diagnosis?

- A. Upright abdominal radiograph
- B. Colonoscopy
- C. Observation with repeat abdominal examinations
- D. CT angiography of the abdomen
- E. Abdominal ultrasonography

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: D

- **정답 핵심**: Sudden severe abdominal pain out of proportion to a soft, minimally tender abdomen in a patient with atrial fibrillation off anticoagulation suggests embolic occlusion of the superior mesenteric artery. Leukocytosis and an elevated lactate support bowel ischemia. CT angiography is fast, widely available, and shows the occluded vessel and the state of the bowel wall, so it is the diagnostic test of choice before revascularization.
- **원리**: <b>왜 진찰보다 통증이 심한가</b>: 장간막 허혈 초기에는 장 점막이 산소 부족으로 아프지만(내장통, 위치가 모호하고 심함) 장벽 전층이 괴사해 복막이 자극되기 전까지는 복벽 근육의 방어·반발통이 없다. 그래서 「환자는 몸부림치는데 배는 부드럽다」는 불일치가 초기 단서다. 복막 자극 징후가 나타났다면 이미 경색이 진행된 것이다.<br> <b>왜 심방세동인가</b>: 심방세동에서 좌심방에 생긴 혈전이 떨어지면 대동맥에서 예각으로 갈라지는 상장간막동맥으로 잘 들어간다. 색전은 대개 중결장동맥이 갈라진 뒤에 걸려 근위 공장은 살아 있고 원위 소장·우측 대장이 허혈에 빠진다.<br> <b>왜 CT 혈관조영인가</b>: 시간이 장의 생존을 정한다. CT 혈관조영은 몇 분 안에 혈관 폐쇄 위치·장벽 조영 저하·장벽 내 공기·문맥 가스를 함께 보여 주어 바로 혈관 내 치료나 개복술로 이어진다. 젖산은 민감하지만 특이하지 않고 늦게 오를 수 있어 정상이어도 배제하지 못한다.
- **비교**: <table><thead><tr><th style="width:24%">Feature</th><th style="width:38%">CT angiography (answer)</th><th>Observation with serial examinations (closest distractor)</th></tr></thead><tbody> <tr><td>Assumption</td><td>Time-critical vascular occlusion is likely</td><td>Benign or self-limited cause is likely</td></tr> <tr><td>What it shows</td><td><b>Arterial cutoff, bowel-wall enhancement, pneumatosis</b></td><td>Waits for peritoneal signs</td></tr> <tr><td>Risk of delay</td><td>Minutes</td><td><b>Transmural infarction before signs appear</b></td></tr> <tr><td>This patient</td><td>AF off anticoagulation, lactate 3.4 — fits</td><td>Benign examination is the trap, not reassurance</td></tr> </tbody></table> In mesenteric ischemia, a soft abdomen is the early stage of the disease, not evidence against it — image the vessels before the examination changes.
- **오답 이유**:
  - (A) An upright abdominal radiograph would be correct to look for free air when perforation is suspected, but it is usually normal in early mesenteric ischemia.
  - (B) Colonoscopy would be correct for suspected ischemic colitis with hematochezia in a stable patient, but it does not show small-bowel or arterial occlusion and may perforate ischemic bowel.
  - (C) Observation would be correct for a mild, nonspecific pain with normal vital signs and laboratory values, not for severe pain with lactate elevation and an embolic source.
  - (E) Abdominal ultrasonography would be correct for right-upper-quadrant pain with suspected cholecystitis, but bowel gas and pain limit its view of the mesenteric arteries.
- **함정**: Being reassured by a soft, minimally tender abdomen and waiting for peritoneal signs.
- **학습목표**: Suspect acute mesenteric ischemia when severe abdominal pain is out of proportion to a benign abdominal examination in a patient with atrial fibrillation, and choose CT angiography as the diagnostic test
