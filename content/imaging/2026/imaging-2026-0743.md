## 문제
A 58-year-old woman with diffuse large B-cell lymphoma comes to the emergency department because of a temperature of 38.6°C for 4 hours. Her third cycle of chemotherapy was 9 days ago. She has no cough, dysuria, diarrhea, or skin lesions. She has no indwelling central venous catheter. Temperature is 38.6°C, pulse is 104/min, respirations are 18/min, and blood pressure is 118/72 mm Hg. Oxygen saturation is 97% on room air. The lungs are clear, and there is no mucositis or skin erythema. Leukocyte count is 400/mm3 with an absolute neutrophil count of 80/mm3. Chest x-ray shows no abnormalities. Two sets of blood cultures and a urine culture are obtained. Which of the following is the most appropriate initial antimicrobial therapy?

- A. Intravenous ceftriaxone alone
- B. Oral amoxicillin-clavulanate and ciprofloxacin
- C. Intravenous fluconazole alone
- D. Intravenous cefepime alone
- E. Intravenous cefepime and vancomycin

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: D

- **정답 핵심**: Fever with an absolute neutrophil count below 500/mm3 is febrile neutropenia; empiric therapy must begin within an hour and must cover <b>Pseudomonas aeruginosa</b> and other gram-negative bacilli, which cause the most rapid deaths. She is hemodynamically stable with no catheter, skin or soft-tissue infection, pneumonia, or severe mucositis, so there is no indication for empiric vancomycin. An antipseudomonal beta-lactam such as cefepime alone is appropriate.
- **원리**: <b>왜 녹농균부터 덮는가</b>: 호중구가 500/mm³ 아래로 떨어지면 몸에 사는 세균을 막는 1차 방어가 사라져 장점막·피부의 그람음성 막대균이 혈류로 쉽게 들어간다. 그중 녹농균 균혈증은 치료가 몇 시간만 늦어도 사망률이 크게 오르므로, 열 말고 다른 증상이 없어도 <b>녹농균에 듣는 베타락탐</b>(cefepime·piperacillin-tazobactam·meropenem)을 1시간 안에 시작한다. 호중구감소 환자는 염증 반응을 만들지 못해 폐렴·요로감염이 있어도 증상·영상이 약하게 나타나므로 「원인 부위가 없다」는 것이 안심할 근거가 되지 않는다.<br> <b>왜 반코마이신을 처음부터 넣지 않는가</b>: 그람양성균은 흔하지만 대개 진행이 느려 배양 결과를 기다려도 사망률이 늘지 않는다. 그래서 혈역학 불안정, 카테터 관련 감염 의심, 피부·연조직 감염, 폐렴, MRSA 집락, 혈액 배양의 그람양성균처럼 <b>특정 조건이 있을 때만</b> 더한다. 불필요한 반코마이신은 신독성과 내성(VRE)을 늘린다.<br> <b>외래 치료 조건</b>: MASCC 점수가 높은 저위험 환자는 경구 치료도 가능하지만, 빈맥과 깊은 호중구감소(80/mm³)가 있는 이 환자는 입원 정맥 치료가 원칙이다.
- **비교**: <table><thead><tr><th style="width:24%">Feature</th><th style="width:38%">Cefepime alone (answer)</th><th>Cefepime + vancomycin (closest distractor)</th></tr></thead><tbody> <tr><td>Hemodynamics</td><td><b>Stable</b> (BP 118/72)</td><td>Hypotension or septic shock</td></tr> <tr><td>Catheter / skin</td><td>No catheter, no skin infection</td><td>Suspected catheter infection, cellulitis</td></tr> <tr><td>Lungs / cultures</td><td>Clear lungs, cultures pending</td><td>Pneumonia, or gram-positive cocci in blood culture</td></tr> <tr><td>Other triggers</td><td>None</td><td>Known MRSA colonization, severe mucositis with fluoroquinolone prophylaxis</td></tr> </tbody></table> Gram-negative coverage is mandatory for every febrile neutropenic patient; gram-positive coverage is added only for a listed indication.
- **오답 이유**:
  - (A) Ceftriaxone would be appropriate for community-acquired infection in a patient with normal neutrophils, but it does not cover Pseudomonas.
  - (B) Oral outpatient therapy would be acceptable for a low-risk patient by MASCC criteria with expected brief neutropenia and stable vital signs.
  - (C) Antifungal therapy would be added if fever persisted for 4 to 7 days despite broad-spectrum antibiotics with prolonged neutropenia.
  - (E) Adding vancomycin would be correct if she were hypotensive or had a suspected catheter infection, cellulitis, pneumonia, or MRSA colonization.
- **함정**: Adding vancomycin reflexively to every febrile neutropenic patient, or choosing a non-antipseudomonal cephalosporin because no infection source is evident.
- **학습목표**: Choose antipseudomonal beta-lactam monotherapy for a hemodynamically stable patient with febrile neutropenia and no indication for gram-positive coverage, and recognize when vancomycin should be added
