## 문제
A 28-year-old woman comes to the physician because she has been unable to conceive despite regular unprotected intercourse for 14 months. Menses have occurred every 2 to 4 months since menarche. She has had acne and increasing hair growth on the chin and lower abdomen. She has tried a supervised diet and exercise program for 6 months and has lost 4 kg (8.8 lb). Her BMI is 31 kg/m2. There is coarse hair on the upper lip and chin. Serum thyroid-stimulating hormone and prolactin concentrations are normal, and serum 17-hydroxyprogesterone is normal. Transvaginal ultrasonography shows more than 20 follicles in each ovary. Hysterosalpingography shows patent fallopian tubes, and her husband's semen analysis is normal. Which of the following is the most appropriate pharmacotherapy to achieve pregnancy?

- A. Metformin alone
- B. Combined oral contraceptive
- C. Spironolactone
- D. Letrozole
- E. Clomiphene citrate

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: D

- **정답 핵심**: Oligomenorrhea since menarche, clinical hyperandrogenism, and polycystic ovarian morphology, with thyroid disease, hyperprolactinemia, and nonclassic congenital adrenal hyperplasia excluded, establish polycystic ovary syndrome. Tubes and semen are normal, so infertility is from anovulation. After lifestyle modification, letrozole is the first-line ovulation-induction drug because it gives higher live-birth rates than clomiphene in this syndrome.
- **원리**: <b>Why women with PCOS do not ovulate</b>: high LH pulse frequency drives theca cells to make androgens, and insulin resistance with hyperinsulinemia amplifies this and lowers SHBG. Many follicles start to grow, but intraovarian androgens and insufficient FSH stop them at a small size, so no dominant follicle is selected.<br> <b>How letrozole induces ovulation</b>: as an aromatase inhibitor, it briefly lowers estrogen, which removes negative feedback on the hypothalamus and pituitary; FSH rises and a single dominant follicle can mature. Its short half-life leaves the endometrium and cervical mucus unaffected.<br> <b>Why it beats clomiphene</b>: clomiphene blocks estrogen receptors for weeks, including those in the endometrium and cervix, thinning the lining and thickening mucus; in a large randomized trial letrozole produced more ovulations and live births in PCOS, especially with obesity.
- **비교**: <table><thead><tr><th style="width:22%">Feature</th><th style="width:39%">Letrozole (answer)</th><th>Clomiphene (closest distractor)</th></tr></thead><tbody> <tr><td>Mechanism</td><td>Aromatase inhibition — lowers estrogen</td><td>Estrogen-receptor blockade at the hypothalamus</td></tr> <tr><td>Half-life</td><td>About 2 days</td><td>About 5 days, with accumulation</td></tr> <tr><td>Endometrium and mucus</td><td>Not affected</td><td><b>Antiestrogenic</b> — thin lining, hostile mucus</td></tr> <tr><td>Live birth in PCOS</td><td>Higher</td><td>Lower; still used if letrozole is unavailable</td></tr> </tbody></table> Both raise FSH by removing estrogen feedback; letrozole does it without blocking estrogen at the target organs.
- **오답 이유**:
  - (A) Metformin alone would be considered for glucose intolerance or as an add-on in clomiphene resistance; by itself it induces ovulation less often.
  - (B) A combined oral contraceptive would be correct to regulate menses and treat hirsutism in a woman who does not want to become pregnant.
  - (C) Spironolactone would be correct for hirsutism once pregnancy is prevented, as its antiandrogen effect can feminize a male fetus.
  - (E) Clomiphene would be correct where letrozole is unavailable or not tolerated; it induces ovulation but has lower live-birth rates in this syndrome.
- **함정**: Choosing clomiphene from habit — in polycystic ovary syndrome, letrozole is first-line because of higher live-birth rates.
- **학습목표**: 다낭난소증후군 여성의 무배란 불임에서 생활 습관 교정 뒤 배란 유도 첫 약으로 레트로졸을 선택하고, 클로미펜·메트포르민·경구피임약·스피로노락톤의 자리와 구별한다
