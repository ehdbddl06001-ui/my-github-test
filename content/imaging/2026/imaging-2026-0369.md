## 문제
A 26-year-old woman, gravida 1, para 0, at 14 weeks' gestation comes to the physician for her first prenatal visit. She feels well. She had an urticarial rash and wheezing within 30 minutes of taking amoxicillin at the age of 15 years. Temperature is 36.9°C (98.4°F), pulse is 80/min, and blood pressure is 112/68 mm Hg. There are no genital ulcers, skin rash, mucous patches, or lymphadenopathy, and the neurologic examination shows no abnormalities. Serum rapid plasma reagin titer is 1:32, and a Treponema pallidum particle agglutination assay is positive. She has never been tested for syphilis before. Which of the following is the most appropriate management?

- A. Oral azithromycin as a single dose followed by repeat titers
- B. Repeat rapid plasma reagin titer in 4 weeks before deciding on treatment
- C. Penicillin desensitization followed by intramuscular benzathine penicillin G
- D. Oral doxycycline for 28 days without penicillin desensitization
- E. Intramuscular ceftriaxone daily for 10 days instead of penicillin

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: C

- **정답 핵심**: A reactive RPR at 1:32 confirmed by a positive treponemal test with no signs of primary or secondary disease and no prior testing is latent syphilis of unknown duration. In pregnancy, penicillin is the only treatment proven to cure maternal infection and prevent congenital syphilis. Because she had an IgE-mediated reaction (urticaria and wheezing), she should undergo penicillin desensitization and then receive benzathine penicillin G 2.4 million units intramuscularly weekly for 3 weeks.
- **원리**: <b>Why penicillin is irreplaceable in pregnancy</b>: Treponema pallidum crosses the placenta at any stage of pregnancy and causes stillbirth, hydrops, and congenital syphilis. Only penicillin has been shown to treat the fetus reliably; benzathine penicillin G maintains low but treponemicidal levels for weeks, matching the slow 30-hour division time of the organism.<br> <b>Why the alternatives fail</b>: tetracyclines (doxycycline) bind calcium in developing teeth and bone and are avoided after the first trimester; macrolides (azithromycin) have widespread resistance from a 23S rRNA mutation and do not reliably cross the placenta; ceftriaxone has limited data for preventing congenital infection.<br> <b>Why desensitization works</b>: giving gradually increasing doses over hours lets mast cells release mediators in small amounts and become temporarily unresponsive to penicillin. The tolerance lasts only while the drug remains in the body, so treatment must follow immediately and lapses of more than about 48 hours require repeating the procedure.
- **비교**: <table><thead><tr><th style="width:22%">Feature</th><th style="width:39%">Desensitization + benzathine penicillin (answer)</th><th>Doxycycline 28 days (closest distractor)</th></tr></thead><tbody> <tr><td>Use</td><td>Pregnant patients with penicillin allergy</td><td>Nonpregnant patients with penicillin allergy</td></tr> <tr><td>Fetal effect</td><td><b>Prevents</b> congenital syphilis</td><td>Does not reliably treat the fetus; tooth and bone deposition</td></tr> <tr><td>Latent of unknown duration</td><td>2.4 million units IM weekly × 3</td><td>100 mg twice daily × 28 days</td></tr> </tbody></table> If the same patient were not pregnant, doxycycline for 28 days would be an acceptable alternative to penicillin.
- **오답 이유**:
  - (A) Azithromycin would be correct for chlamydial infection in pregnancy; for syphilis, macrolide resistance is common and placental transfer is poor.
  - (B) Repeating the titer before treatment would be correct only if the treponemal test were negative and the RPR result were suspected to be a biologic false positive.
  - (D) Doxycycline would be correct for latent syphilis in a nonpregnant patient with penicillin allergy; in pregnancy it does not reliably prevent congenital infection.
  - (E) Ceftriaxone would be an option for neurosyphilis or early syphilis in a nonpregnant patient with non-IgE penicillin allergy, but it is not proven to protect the fetus.
- **함정**: Avoiding penicillin because of the allergy and choosing an alternative antibiotic — in pregnancy the answer is to desensitize, not to substitute.
- **학습목표**: 임신 중 매독은 페니실린만이 태아 감염을 확실히 막으므로, 페니실린 알레르기 임신부는 탈감작 뒤 벤자틴 페니실린 G 로 치료한다
