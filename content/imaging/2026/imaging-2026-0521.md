## 문제
A 56-year-old man with alcohol-associated cirrhosis comes to the emergency department because of increasing abdominal girth and diffuse abdominal discomfort for 3 days. He takes spironolactone and furosemide. Temperature is 38.0°C, blood pressure is 104/64 mm Hg, and pulse is 96/min. The abdomen is distended with shifting dullness and mild diffuse tenderness without rigidity. Diagnostic paracentesis shows a polymorphonuclear leukocyte count of 480/µL and a total protein of 0.8 g/dL; Gram stain shows no organisms. Serum creatinine is 1.6 mg/dL, blood urea nitrogen 34 mg/dL, and total bilirubin 4.8 mg/dL. Intravenous cefotaxime is started and diuretics are held. In addition, which of the following is most likely to reduce this patient's risk of renal failure and death?

- A. Intravenous furosemide
- B. Oral lactulose
- C. Exploratory laparotomy
- D. Intravenous albumin on day 1 and day 3
- E. Large-volume paracentesis

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: D

- **정답 핵심**: An ascitic neutrophil count of 250/µL or more without a surgical source is spontaneous bacterial peritonitis. About a third of patients develop renal failure from worsening effective arterial underfilling. In patients with serum creatinine above 1 mg/dL, BUN above 30 mg/dL, or bilirubin above 4 mg/dL — all present here — intravenous albumin (1.5 g/kg on day 1 and 1 g/kg on day 3) added to cefotaxime reduced renal failure and in-hospital mortality.
- **원리**: <b>Why SBP causes kidney failure</b>: Cirrhosis already causes splanchnic vasodilation and low effective arterial volume. Infection releases cytokines and nitric oxide that widen vessels further, so the renin–angiotensin and sympathetic systems constrict the renal arteries — a hepatorenal-type physiology.<br> <b>Why albumin helps</b>: Albumin expands plasma volume and also binds endotoxin and nitric oxide-related mediators, supporting arterial filling and renal perfusion. The benefit was shown in the high-risk group (creatinine > 1, BUN > 30, bilirubin > 4).<br> <b>Why low ascitic protein matters later</b>: Protein below 1.5 g/dL means few opsonins in the fluid; after recovery this patient needs long-term prophylaxis against recurrence.
- **비교**: <table><thead><tr><th style="width:24%">Feature</th><th style="width:38%">IV albumin with antibiotics (answer)</th><th>Large-volume paracentesis (closest distractor)</th></tr></thead><tbody> <tr><td>Effect on circulation</td><td><b>Expands effective arterial volume</b></td><td>Removes volume; can worsen arterial underfilling</td></tr> <tr><td>Proven benefit</td><td>Lower renal failure and mortality in SBP with Cr > 1, BUN > 30 or bilirubin > 4</td><td>Relief of tense ascites (with albumin if > 5 L)</td></tr> <tr><td>Timing here</td><td>Day 1 and day 3</td><td>Not for an infected, hypotensive patient's kidney protection</td></tr> </tbody></table> In SBP the goal is to protect the kidney by filling the circulation, not by removing fluid.
- **오답 이유**:
  - (A) Intravenous furosemide is correct for volume overload with adequate renal perfusion — here it would deepen arterial underfilling.
  - (B) Oral lactulose is correct when hepatic encephalopathy is present, such as confusion and asterixis, which this patient does not have.
  - (C) Exploratory laparotomy is correct for secondary peritonitis, suggested by polymicrobial Gram stain, very high protein, or low glucose in the fluid.
  - (E) Large-volume paracentesis is correct for tense, symptomatic ascites without infection and is paired with albumin when more than 5 L is removed.
- **함정**: Choosing to remove more ascites or keep diuretics in SBP — the kidney is threatened by underfilling, so albumin is given.
- **학습목표**: 복수 다형핵백혈구 250/µL 이상으로 자발세균복막염을 진단하고, 혈청 크레아티닌·BUN·빌리루빈이 높은 환자에게 항생제와 함께 알부민을 주어 신부전과 사망을 줄인다
