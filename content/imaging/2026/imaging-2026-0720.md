## 문제
A 34-year-old woman is brought to the emergency department because of confusion and slurred speech that began 6 hours ago. For 3 days she has had fatigue and easy bruising. She takes no medications. Temperature is 37.9°C, blood pressure is 138/84 mm Hg, and pulse is 102/min. She is disoriented to time and has petechiae on the legs. Hemoglobin is 7.8 g/dL, platelet count is 14,000/mm³, reticulocytes are 6%, LDH is 1,450 U/L, and haptoglobin is undetectable. A peripheral blood smear shows many schistocytes. Prothrombin time and activated partial thromboplastin time are normal. Creatinine is 1.3 mg/dL. Direct antiglobulin test is negative. Which of the following is the most appropriate next step in management?

- A. Eculizumab
- B. Unfractionated heparin infusion
- C. Therapeutic plasma exchange
- D. Platelet transfusion
- E. Intravenous immune globulin

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: C

- **정답 핵심**: Microangiopathic hemolytic anemia (schistocytes, high LDH, absent haptoglobin, negative direct antiglobulin test) with severe thrombocytopenia, neurologic symptoms, low-grade fever and normal coagulation tests is thrombotic thrombocytopenic purpura until proven otherwise. Untreated mortality is about 90%, so plasma exchange (with glucocorticoids, often caplacizumab) is started immediately, before the ADAMTS13 activity result returns.
- **원리**: <b>왜 혈소판이 소모되는가</b>: 혈관내피는 아주 큰 von Willebrand 인자 다량체를 분비하고, 혈장 단백분해효소 <b>ADAMTS13</b> 이 이를 잘라 크기를 줄인다. 후천성 혈전 혈소판 감소 자색반병에서는 ADAMTS13 에 대한 자가항체가 효소를 막아(활성 < 10 %) 초거대 다량체가 남고, 이것이 혈소판을 붙잡아 작은 혈관마다 혈소판 혈전을 만든다. 혈소판이 소모되어 줄고, 적혈구는 혈전 사이를 지나며 찢겨 분열적혈구가 된다. 뇌·콩팥·심장의 미세혈관이 막혀 신경 증상과 장기 손상이 생긴다.<br> <b>왜 혈장교환인가</b>: 혈장교환은 자가항체와 초거대 다량체를 걷어 내고 정상 ADAMTS13 을 보충한다 — 원인과 결과를 동시에 고친다. 스테로이드·리툭시맙은 항체 생성을 줄이고, 카플라시주맙은 von Willebrand 인자와 혈소판의 결합을 막는다.<br> <b>왜 응고 검사가 정상인가</b>: 소모되는 것은 혈소판이지 응고인자가 아니다 — PT·aPTT 가 정상이라는 점이 파종혈관내응고와 가르는 소견이다.
- **비교**: <table><thead><tr><th style="width:24%">Feature</th><th style="width:38%">TTP → plasma exchange (answer)</th><th>Platelet transfusion (closest distractor)</th></tr></thead><tbody> <tr><td>Mechanism targeted</td><td>Removes anti-ADAMTS13 antibody and ultralarge vWF; replaces ADAMTS13</td><td>Only replaces platelets</td></tr> <tr><td>Effect in TTP</td><td>Mortality falls from about 90% to 10–20%</td><td>May <b>fuel new microthrombi</b></td></tr> <tr><td>When transfusion is used</td><td colspan="2">Only for life-threatening bleeding or before an essential invasive procedure</td></tr> </tbody></table> Low platelets with schistocytes and normal PT/aPTT point to a thrombotic microangiopathy — treat the cause rather than the count.
- **오답 이유**:
  - (A) Eculizumab would be correct for complement-mediated (atypical) hemolytic uremic syndrome with dominant acute kidney injury and normal ADAMTS13 activity.
  - (B) Unfractionated heparin would be correct for disseminated intravascular coagulation with thrombosis or for venous thromboembolism, not for a platelet-rich microangiopathy.
  - (D) Platelet transfusion would be correct if the thrombocytopenia were from marrow failure or the patient had life-threatening bleeding needing an urgent procedure.
  - (E) Intravenous immune globulin would be correct for severe immune thrombocytopenia with bleeding, where the smear shows large platelets without schistocytes.
- **함정**: Treating the platelet number by transfusion instead of recognizing a thrombotic microangiopathy.
- **학습목표**: 미세혈관병 용혈성 빈혈과 혈소판 감소에 신경 증상이 있으면 혈전 혈소판 감소 자색반병으로 보고 ADAMTS13 결과를 기다리지 않고 혈장교환을 시작한다
