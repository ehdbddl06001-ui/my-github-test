## 문제
A 58-year-old man is brought to the emergency department 30 minutes after the onset of crushing substernal chest pain. Six weeks ago, he underwent placement of a drug-eluting stent in the proximal left anterior descending artery for an anterior ST-elevation myocardial infarction. Since then, he has taken aspirin 81 mg and clopidogrel 75 mg daily, and his wife confirms that he has not missed doses. He also takes atorvastatin and lisinopril. Blood pressure is 102/64 mm Hg and pulse is 104/min. ECG shows ST-segment elevation in leads V1 through V4. Emergency coronary angiography shows thrombotic occlusion within the stent with no edge dissection or stent underexpansion. Genotyping shows that he is homozygous for the CYP2C19*2 allele. Which of the following best explains the failure of therapy in this patient?

- A. Inhibition of clopidogrel absorption by atorvastatin
- B. Decreased formation of the active thiol metabolite of clopidogrel
- C. Increased hepatic clearance of the active metabolite of clopidogrel
- D. Mutation of the platelet P2Y12 receptor preventing drug binding
- E. Accelerated hydrolysis of aspirin by plasma esterases

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: B

- **정답 핵심**: Clopidogrel is an inactive prodrug; about 85% is hydrolyzed by carboxylesterase to an inactive acid, and the remainder must be oxidized by hepatic cytochrome P450 enzymes — chiefly CYP2C19 — in two steps to the active thiol metabolite that irreversibly blocks the platelet P2Y12 receptor. CYP2C19*2 is a loss-of-function allele; homozygous poor metabolizers form much less active metabolite, platelet inhibition is inadequate, and the risk of stent thrombosis is increased despite adherence.
- **원리**: <b>Why clopidogrel depends on the liver</b>: thienopyridines (clopidogrel, prasugrel) are prodrugs. Clopidogrel faces two competing pathways: most of the absorbed dose is wasted by carboxylesterase-1 hydrolysis, and only a small fraction undergoes two sequential CYP oxidations (CYP2C19 contributes to both steps) to a reactive thiol that forms a disulfide bond with the P2Y12 ADP receptor, inhibiting the platelet for its whole 7–10-day life.<br> <b>Why the genotype matters</b>: CYP2C19*2 (a splice defect) and *3 (a premature stop codon) abolish enzyme activity. Poor metabolizers (two loss-of-function alleles; about 2% of White and up to 15% of East Asian people) have substantially lower active metabolite levels, higher on-treatment platelet reactivity, and more stent thrombosis after PCI. This is why the FDA label carries a boxed warning.<br> <b>Why the alternatives work</b>: prasugrel is also a prodrug, but its activation needs only one CYP step through several enzymes (CYP3A4, CYP2B6) and is not CYP2C19-dependent; ticagrelor is a direct-acting, reversible P2Y12 antagonist that needs no activation. Guidelines recommend one of these for known poor metabolizers after acute coronary syndrome and PCI.
- **비교**: <table><thead><tr><th style="width:24%">Feature</th><th style="width:38%">Reduced activation (correct)</th><th>Increased clearance of active metabolite (closest distractor)</th></tr></thead><tbody> <tr><td>Step affected</td><td><b>Formation</b> of the thiol by CYP2C19</td><td>Elimination after formation</td></tr> <tr><td>Genetic basis</td><td>CYP2C19 *2/*3 loss-of-function — poor metabolizer</td><td>No such mechanism; the thiol binds covalently and is short-lived regardless</td></tr> <tr><td>Opposite phenotype</td><td>CYP2C19*17 (ultrarapid) → more active drug, more bleeding</td><td>—</td></tr> <tr><td>Drug interaction that mimics it</td><td>CYP2C19 inhibitors (omeprazole, fluconazole) reduce activation</td><td>CYP inducers would increase activation, not clearance failure</td></tr> </tbody></table> A loss-of-function allele lowers drug effect only when the enzyme makes the active form; for drugs inactivated by the enzyme, the same allele raises toxicity.
- **오답 이유**:
  - (A) Atorvastatin is a CYP3A4 substrate and was once suspected of competing with clopidogrel activation in vitro, but clinical studies showed no meaningful effect, and it does not block intestinal absorption.
  - (C) Increased clearance of the active metabolite would explain failure only if an enzyme inactivated the formed thiol; loss-of-function CYP2C19 alleles reduce formation, and the thiol binds the receptor irreversibly.
  - (D) A P2Y12 receptor mutation preventing binding would cause resistance to all P2Y12 inhibitors, including ticagrelor; the genotype here involves the activating enzyme, not the receptor.
  - (E) Aspirin is deacetylated to salicylate, but its effect comes from rapid irreversible COX-1 acetylation before hydrolysis; aspirin failure would be suspected with nonadherence or concurrent ibuprofen use.
- **함정**: Thinking a loss-of-function metabolizing enzyme always raises drug levels and effect — clopidogrel is a prodrug, so poor CYP2C19 metabolizers get less active drug.
- **학습목표**: 클로피도그렐이 CYP2C19 로 활성화되는 전구약물임을 알고, 기능 소실 대립유전자 동형접합에서 활성 대사물이 부족해 스텐트 혈전증이 생기는 기전과 대안(티카그렐러·프라수그렐)을 안다
