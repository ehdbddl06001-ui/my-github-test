## 문제
A 31-year-old woman, gravida 3, para 3, delivers a 4,200-g boy vaginally after an 18-hour labor augmented with oxytocin. The placenta is delivered intact 10 minutes later. Over the next 15 minutes, she has heavy vaginal bleeding with an estimated blood loss of 1,100 mL. Blood pressure is 102/64 mm Hg and pulse is 112/min. The uterine fundus is palpated above the umbilicus and is soft and boggy. Inspection of the cervix and vagina shows no lacerations. Which of the following is the most appropriate next step in management?

- A. Bimanual uterine massage and intravenous oxytocin
- B. Manual exploration of the uterine cavity for retained placenta
- C. Transfusion of fresh frozen plasma
- D. Emergency hysterectomy
- E. Repair of a cervical laceration

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: A

- **정답 핵심**: Heavy bleeding after delivery with a soft, boggy uterus above the umbilicus is uterine atony, the most common cause of postpartum hemorrhage. Risk factors here are a macrosomic infant, a long oxytocin-augmented labor and multiparity. The first step is bimanual uterine massage together with uterotonic drugs, starting with intravenous oxytocin. The placenta was intact and there are no lacerations.
- **원리**: <b>왜 자궁 수축이 지혈인가</b>: 태반이 떨어진 자리에는 나선동맥이 열린 채 남는다. 분만 뒤 이 혈관을 막는 주된 장치는 응고가 아니라 <b>자궁근의 수축</b>이다 — 서로 엇갈린 근섬유가 수축하면서 혈관을 졸라매는 「살아 있는 결찰」이다. 그래서 자궁이 수축하지 못하면(이완) 응고 기능이 정상이어도 피가 쏟아진다.<br> <b>무엇이 이완을 부르는가</b>: 자궁이 과하게 늘어난 경우(거대아·쌍태·양수과다), 근육이 지친 경우(긴 진통, 오래 쓴 옥시토신 — 수용체가 둔해진다), 다산, 융모양막염, 마그네슘·흡입 마취제.<br> <b>순서</b>: 출혈 원인은 4T(Tone·Trauma·Tissue·Thrombin)로 찾는다. 자궁이 무르고 크면 Tone 이므로 양손 마사지로 기계적으로 수축시키면서 옥시토신 → 메틸에르고노빈(고혈압 금기)·카보프로스트(천식 금기)·미소프로스톨을 차례로 더하고, 트라넥삼산을 일찍 준다. 그래도 멈추지 않으면 자궁 내 풍선 압박 → 동맥 색전술·수술이다.
- **비교**: <table><thead><tr><th style="width:24%">Feature</th><th style="width:38%">Uterine atony (answer)</th><th>Retained placental tissue (closest distractor)</th></tr></thead><tbody> <tr><td>Uterus</td><td><b>Soft, boggy, enlarged</b></td><td>May be firm or partly contracted</td></tr> <tr><td>Placenta</td><td>Delivered intact</td><td><b>Missing cotyledon</b> or fragmented membranes</td></tr> <tr><td>Timing</td><td>Immediately after delivery</td><td>Immediate or delayed (days)</td></tr> <tr><td>First step</td><td>Bimanual massage + oxytocin</td><td>Manual exploration / curettage</td></tr> <tr><td>This patient</td><td>Boggy uterus, intact placenta — fits</td><td>Placenta intact — does not fit</td></tr> </tbody></table> Check tone first because it is the most common cause and the quickest to treat; examine the placenta and the genital tract to exclude tissue and trauma.
- **오답 이유**:
  - (B) Manual exploration of the uterine cavity would be correct if the delivered placenta had a missing cotyledon or the bleeding persisted despite a firm uterus.
  - (C) Fresh frozen plasma would be correct if laboratory tests showed coagulopathy, such as a low fibrinogen after placental abruption or massive transfusion.
  - (D) Emergency hysterectomy would be correct only after uterotonic drugs, balloon tamponade and embolization or conservative surgery had failed to stop the bleeding.
  - (E) Repair of a cervical laceration would be correct if bleeding continued with a firm, well-contracted uterus and a laceration was seen on inspection.
- **함정**: Rushing to a surgical or transfusion step before restoring uterine tone, the cause in most cases.
- **학습목표**: 질식분만 직후 자궁이 무르고 커진 산후출혈은 자궁 이완이 원인이며 첫 처치는 양손 자궁 마사지와 옥시토신이다
