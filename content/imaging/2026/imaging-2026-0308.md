## 문제
A 58-year-old man comes to the physician because of headaches and itching after hot showers for 4 months. He does not smoke and has no history of lung disease or snoring. Temperature is 36.8°C (98.2°F), pulse is 78/min, and blood pressure is 148/90 mm Hg. His face is ruddy. The spleen tip is palpable 3 cm below the left costal margin. Oxygen saturation is 98% on room air. Hemoglobin concentration is 19.4 g/dL, hematocrit is 58%, leukocyte count is 13,200/mm3 with a normal differential and no immature cells, and platelet count is 520,000/mm3. Serum erythropoietin concentration is 2 mU/mL (N = 4-26). Which of the following is the most appropriate next step in diagnosis?

- A. Testing for the BCR::ABL1 fusion gene
- B. Overnight polysomnography
- C. Abdominal CT for an erythropoietin-secreting tumor
- D. Measurement of blood carboxyhemoglobin
- E. Testing for the JAK2 V617F mutation

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: E

- **정답 핵심**: He has erythrocytosis (hemoglobin 19.4 g/dL, hematocrit 58%) with a low serum erythropoietin, plus aquagenic pruritus, splenomegaly, leukocytosis, and thrombocytosis. Low erythropoietin means the red cell production is autonomous rather than driven by hypoxia or a tumor, which points to polycythemia vera. The next step is testing for the JAK2 V617F mutation, present in about 95% of cases (exon 12 mutations in most of the rest).
- **원리**: <b>Why erythropoietin sorts the causes</b>: normally the kidney releases erythropoietin when tissue oxygen delivery falls, and red cells rise in response (secondary erythrocytosis: lung disease, sleep apnea, smoking with carboxyhemoglobin, high altitude) or because a tumor secretes it (renal cell carcinoma, hepatoma, cerebellar hemangioblastoma). In both, erythropoietin is normal or high. In polycythemia vera the marrow makes red cells without needing the hormone, so the excess red cell mass suppresses erythropoietin to low levels.<br> <b>Why JAK2</b>: the erythropoietin receptor signals through the JAK2 kinase. The V617F mutation disables JAK2's inhibitory pseudokinase domain, so the kinase is constantly active and drives proliferation of all myeloid lines — hence the high white cells and platelets, splenomegaly from extramedullary hematopoiesis, and pruritus from basophil and mast cell histamine release after warm water.<br> <b>Why it matters</b>: thrombosis is the main cause of death; phlebotomy to a hematocrit below 45% and low-dose aspirin are first-line treatment.
- **비교**: <table><thead><tr><th style="width:22%">Feature</th><th style="width:39%">Polycythemia vera (correct)</th><th>Chronic myeloid leukemia (closest distractor)</th></tr></thead><tbody> <tr><td>Hemoglobin</td><td><b>High</b> (here 19.4 g/dL)</td><td>Normal or low</td></tr> <tr><td>White cells</td><td>Mildly raised, normal differential</td><td><b>Markedly raised</b> with myelocytes and basophilia</td></tr> <tr><td>Typical clue</td><td>Aquagenic pruritus, ruddy face, low erythropoietin</td><td>Massive splenomegaly, fatigue, left-shifted smear</td></tr> <tr><td>Diagnostic test</td><td>JAK2 V617F</td><td>BCR::ABL1 (Philadelphia chromosome)</td></tr> </tbody></table> Both are myeloproliferative neoplasms with splenomegaly; a high hemoglobin with low erythropoietin points to JAK2, whereas a striking leukocytosis with immature granulocytes points to BCR::ABL1.
- **오답 이유**:
  - (A) BCR::ABL1 testing is the key study for chronic myeloid leukemia, which presents with marked leukocytosis, myelocytes, and basophilia; it would be chosen if the smear were left-shifted and hemoglobin were not elevated.
  - (B) Polysomnography looks for obstructive sleep apnea, a cause of hypoxic secondary erythrocytosis; it would be appropriate if he snored, were obese, and had a normal or high erythropoietin.
  - (C) Abdominal CT searches for renal cell carcinoma or hepatoma secreting erythropoietin; it would be ordered if serum erythropoietin were inappropriately high with normal oxygen saturation.
  - (D) Carboxyhemoglobin measurement detects erythrocytosis from smoking or carbon monoxide exposure, in which erythropoietin is normal or high; he does not smoke and his erythropoietin is low.
- **함정**: Seeing leukocytosis, thrombocytosis, and splenomegaly and testing for BCR::ABL1 — a hemoglobin of 19.4 g/dL with suppressed erythropoietin is polycythemia vera.
- **학습목표**: 혈색소·적혈구용적률이 높고 혈청 에리트로포이에틴이 낮으며 목욕 뒤 가려움·비장비대가 있으면 진성적혈구증가증을 의심해 JAK2 V617F 돌연변이 검사를 한다
