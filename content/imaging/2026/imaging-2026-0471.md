## 문제
A 71-year-old woman is evaluated in the hospital because of 6 episodes of watery diarrhea daily for the past 2 days. Eight days ago, she was admitted for community-acquired pneumonia and treated with ceftriaxone and azithromycin, which were completed 2 days ago. She has never had a similar illness. Her temperature is 38.2°C (100.8°F), pulse is 98/min, and blood pressure is 124/76 mm Hg. The abdomen is soft and mildly tender in both lower quadrants without distention, rigidity, or guarding; bowel sounds are present. Leukocyte count is 18,600/mm3, serum creatinine is 1.7 mg/dL (baseline 0.9 mg/dL), and serum lactate is 1.4 mmol/L. Stool testing is positive for Clostridioides difficile toxin. An abdominal x-ray shows no colonic dilation. Which of the following is the most appropriate pharmacotherapy?

- A. Loperamide
- B. Oral rifaximin
- C. Oral vancomycin
- D. Oral metronidazole
- E. Intravenous vancomycin

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: C

- **정답 핵심**: This is a first episode of Clostridioides difficile infection after broad-spectrum antibiotics. A leukocyte count of 15,000/mm3 or more or a serum creatinine above 1.5 mg/dL defines severe disease; there is no hypotension, ileus, or megacolon, so it is not fulminant. Oral vancomycin (or fidaxomicin) is the treatment; it stays in the colonic lumen at high concentration, where the organism lives.
- **원리**: <b>Why the route matters</b>: C. difficile lives and makes toxin in the colonic lumen, not in tissue. Oral vancomycin is barely absorbed, so it reaches stool concentrations far above the organism's MIC. Intravenous vancomycin is not secreted into the colon and does nothing for this infection.<br> <b>Severity defines the plan</b>: nonsevere — leukocytes &lt; 15,000/mm3 and creatinine ≤ 1.5 mg/dL; severe — either threshold crossed; fulminant — hypotension or shock, ileus, or megacolon. First episodes (nonsevere or severe) are treated with fidaxomicin or oral vancomycin for 10 days. Fulminant disease adds higher-dose oral (± rectal) vancomycin plus intravenous metronidazole and a surgical consultation.<br> <b>Why metronidazole fell out of favor</b>: it is absorbed in the small intestine, reaches the colon mainly through inflamed mucosa, and had lower cure rates in trials, especially in severe disease.
- **비교**: <table><thead><tr><th style="width:22%">Feature</th><th style="width:39%">Oral vancomycin (answer)</th><th>Oral metronidazole (closest distractor)</th></tr></thead><tbody> <tr><td>Colonic drug level</td><td><b>Very high — not absorbed</b></td><td>Low — absorbed proximally</td></tr> <tr><td>Place in current guidelines</td><td>First episode, nonsevere or severe (with fidaxomicin)</td><td>Only for a nonsevere first episode when vancomycin and fidaxomicin are unavailable</td></tr> <tr><td>This patient</td><td>WBC 18,600/mm3 and creatinine 1.7 mg/dL = severe</td><td>Not appropriate for severe disease</td></tr> </tbody></table> Intravenous metronidazole is added (not substituted) only when disease is fulminant.
- **오답 이유**:
  - (A) Loperamide may help noninfectious diarrhea, but in C. difficile infection it slows toxin clearance and can precipitate toxic megacolon.
  - (B) Oral rifaximin is used for hepatic encephalopathy and traveler's diarrhea, and only as an adjunct after vancomycin for multiple recurrences.
  - (D) Oral metronidazole is acceptable only for a nonsevere first episode when neither oral vancomycin nor fidaxomicin can be obtained.
  - (E) Intravenous vancomycin treats bloodstream or tissue gram-positive infections such as MRSA bacteremia; it does not reach the colonic lumen.
- **함정**: Choosing metronidazole out of habit — current guidelines reserve it for nonsevere disease when vancomycin or fidaxomicin is unavailable, and this case meets severe criteria.
- **학습목표**: 항생제 치료 뒤 첫 클로스트리디오이데스 디피실레 감염에서 백혈구 ≥ 15,000/mm³ 또는 크레아티닌 > 1.5 mg/dL 이면 중증으로 분류하고 경구 반코마이신(또는 피닥소마이신)으로 치료한다
