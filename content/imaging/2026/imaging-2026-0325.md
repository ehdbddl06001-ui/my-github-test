## 문제
A 34-year-old man comes to the emergency department because of weakness of both legs that began 4 days ago and has progressed so that he now has difficulty climbing stairs. He also has tingling in his feet and hands. Two weeks ago, he had a 3-day episode of watery diarrhea that resolved without treatment. Temperature is 37.0°C, blood pressure is 128/78 mm Hg, pulse is 84/min, and respirations are 16/min. Muscle strength is 3/5 in the hip flexors and 4/5 in the ankle dorsiflexors bilaterally; strength in the upper extremities is 5/5. Deep tendon reflexes are absent in the lower extremities and decreased in the upper extremities. Sensation to vibration is mildly decreased in the toes. Forced vital capacity is 3.6 L (80% of predicted). Cerebrospinal fluid analysis shows a leukocyte count of 2/mm3 and a protein concentration of 110 mg/dL. Which of the following is the most appropriate treatment?

- A. Intravenous acyclovir
- B. Oral prednisone
- C. Intravenous immune globulin
- D. Intravenous methylprednisolone
- E. Oral pyridostigmine

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: C

- **정답 핵심**: Rapidly progressive, symmetric, ascending weakness with areflexia and distal paresthesias 2 weeks after diarrhea (Campylobacter jejuni) is Guillain-Barré syndrome. CSF shows albuminocytologic dissociation (high protein, normal cell count). He cannot climb stairs, so disease-modifying treatment is indicated: intravenous immune globulin (or plasma exchange), both of which speed recovery; corticosteroids are ineffective.
- **원리**: <b>Why the nerve fails</b>: in Guillain-Barré syndrome, antibodies raised against an infection (classically Campylobacter jejuni lipooligosaccharide) cross-react with gangliosides on peripheral nerve myelin or axolemma — molecular mimicry. Complement is activated, macrophages strip myelin at the nodes of Ranvier, and conduction slows or blocks. The longest fibers are affected first, so weakness ascends from the legs, and loss of afferent conduction abolishes reflexes early.<br> <b>Why protein rises without cells</b>: inflammation of the spinal nerve roots increases permeability of the blood–nerve barrier, letting protein into the CSF, whereas the process is not a meningeal infection, so cells stay normal. Protein may be normal in the first week, so a normal early CSF does not exclude the disease.<br> <b>Why IVIG or plasma exchange, not steroids</b>: plasma exchange removes the pathogenic antibodies and complement; IVIG neutralizes antibodies, blocks Fc receptors, and inhibits complement. Randomized trials showed both shorten time to walking when started within 2 weeks, while corticosteroids alone did not help. Combining them adds no benefit. Monitoring of forced vital capacity and autonomic function is essential because respiratory failure and arrhythmias are the main causes of death.
- **비교**: <table><thead><tr><th style="width:24%">Feature</th><th style="width:38%">Guillain-Barré → IVIG (correct)</th><th>CIDP → corticosteroids (closest distractor)</th></tr></thead><tbody> <tr><td>Time to nadir</td><td><b>Less than 4 weeks</b> (here 4 days)</td><td><b>More than 8 weeks</b>, progressive or relapsing</td></tr> <tr><td>Antecedent infection</td><td>Common (diarrhea, respiratory)</td><td>Uncommon</td></tr> <tr><td>CSF</td><td>High protein, normal cells</td><td>High protein, normal cells</td></tr> <tr><td>Steroids</td><td><b>Ineffective</b></td><td><b>Effective</b> (with IVIG or plasma exchange)</td></tr> </tbody></table> The CSF looks the same; the time course decides — acute (< 4 weeks) means IVIG or plasma exchange, chronic (> 8 weeks) allows steroids.
- **오답 이유**:
  - (A) Acyclovir is the treatment for herpes simplex encephalitis or VZV infection, which would be correct with fever, altered mental status, and CSF lymphocytic pleocytosis.
  - (B) Oral prednisone is effective for chronic inflammatory demyelinating polyneuropathy, which would be correct if weakness had progressed over more than 8 weeks or relapsed.
  - (D) Intravenous methylprednisolone is used for acute exacerbations of multiple sclerosis or for CIDP, but in acute Guillain-Barré syndrome trials showed no benefit from corticosteroids.
  - (E) Pyridostigmine improves neuromuscular transmission in myasthenia gravis, which would be correct with fatigable ptosis, diplopia, and preserved reflexes rather than areflexia and sensory symptoms.
- **함정**: Choosing corticosteroids for an inflammatory neuropathy — steroids help CIDP but not acute Guillain-Barré syndrome, where IVIG or plasma exchange is the therapy.
- **학습목표**: 설사 뒤 상행성 대칭 근력 약화·반사 소실·뇌척수액 단백-세포 해리로 길랭-바레 증후군을 진단하고, 치료는 정맥 면역글로불린 또는 혈장교환이며 스테로이드는 효과가 없음을 안다
