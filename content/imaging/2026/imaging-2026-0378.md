## 문제
A 41-year-old man comes to the emergency department because of a 1-week history of fatigue, gum bleeding when brushing his teeth, and bruising on his arms and legs. His temperature is 37.4°C (99.3°F), pulse is 104/min, and blood pressure is 118/70 mm Hg. Examination shows pallor, gingival oozing, and scattered ecchymoses; there is no lymphadenopathy or splenomegaly. Laboratory studies show a hemoglobin concentration of 8.2 g/dL, leukocyte count of 1800/mm3, platelet count of 22,000/mm3, prothrombin time of 19 seconds (INR 1.7), fibrinogen of 85 mg/dL, and markedly elevated D-dimer. A peripheral blood smear shows occasional large abnormal cells with bilobed nuclei, heavy cytoplasmic granules, and bundles of Auer rods. Bone marrow aspiration has been performed, and cytogenetic and molecular studies are pending. In addition to transfusion of platelets and cryoprecipitate, which of the following is the most appropriate next step in management?

- A. Wait for PML-RARA confirmation before starting treatment
- B. Start continuous intravenous unfractionated heparin
- C. Start induction with cytarabine and daunorubicin alone
- D. Start hydroxyurea to lower the leukocyte count
- E. Start all-trans retinoic acid immediately

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: E

- **정답 핵심**: Pancytopenia with disseminated intravascular coagulation (prolonged PT, fibrinogen 85 mg/dL, high D-dimer) and abnormal promyelocytes with heavy granules and bundles of Auer rods (faggot cells) indicate acute promyelocytic leukemia. Early hemorrhagic death from the coagulopathy is the main threat, so all-trans retinoic acid is started as soon as APL is suspected, without waiting for genetic confirmation, together with aggressive blood product support.
- **원리**: <b>Why APL bleeds</b>: the granules of leukemic promyelocytes are full of tissue factor and annexin II. When the cells release them, tissue factor triggers DIC and annexin II amplifies plasmin generation, producing hyperfibrinolysis. Fibrinogen falls rapidly, and intracranial or pulmonary hemorrhage can kill within days of presentation.<br> <b>Why ATRA works immediately</b>: the t(15;17) fusion protein PML-RARA blocks retinoic acid–dependent transcription and freezes cells at the promyelocyte stage. Pharmacologic doses of ATRA overcome this block, so the cells mature into neutrophils and stop releasing procoagulant granules; the coagulopathy improves within days.<br> <b>Why not wait</b>: confirming PML-RARA by FISH or PCR can take one to several days, and most early deaths occur in that window. Because ATRA is safe if the diagnosis turns out to be another leukemia, suspicion from the smear and coagulation profile is enough to start it. Watch for differentiation syndrome (fever, dyspnea, edema, pulmonary infiltrates), treated with dexamethasone.
- **비교**: <table><thead><tr><th style="width:22%">Feature</th><th style="width:39%">ATRA immediately (answer)</th><th>Wait for PML-RARA (closest distractor)</th></tr></thead><tbody> <tr><td>Basis</td><td>Clinical suspicion: smear + DIC</td><td>Genetic confirmation</td></tr> <tr><td>Timing</td><td>Hours</td><td>One to several days</td></tr> <tr><td>Effect on coagulopathy</td><td>Matures promyelocytes, <b>stops granule release</b></td><td>DIC and fibrinolysis continue</td></tr> <tr><td>Risk if diagnosis is wrong</td><td>Low — ATRA is stopped</td><td>Early hemorrhagic death during the wait</td></tr> </tbody></table> Confirmation still matters for the full regimen (ATRA plus arsenic trioxide, with or without chemotherapy), but it never delays the first dose of ATRA.
- **오답 이유**:
  - (A) Waiting for PML-RARA confirmation would be appropriate for treatment choices in other acute myeloid leukemias, but in suspected APL it allows fatal hemorrhage.
  - (B) Heparin would be considered only for DIC dominated by thrombosis, such as large-vessel clots; in APL the bleeding is driven by consumption and fibrinolysis.
  - (C) Cytarabine and daunorubicin alone is standard induction for non-APL acute myeloid leukemia; in APL, chemotherapy without ATRA worsens DIC from cell lysis.
  - (D) Hydroxyurea would be correct for cytoreduction in hyperleukocytosis from other leukemias; this patient's leukocyte count is low, not high.
- **함정**: Waiting for the genetic test before treating — APL is treated on suspicion because early death comes from the coagulopathy, not the leukemia burden.
- **학습목표**: 범혈구감소·DIC·다발 Auer 막대를 가진 미성숙 과립구로 급성 전골수구백혈병을 의심하고, 유전자 확인을 기다리지 않고 바로 ATRA 를 시작한다
