## 문제
A 19-year-old woman comes to the physician before a scheduled extraction of impacted wisdom teeth. Since menarche she has had heavy menstrual periods lasting 8 days that soak through pads hourly on the first 2 days, and she bled for 2 days after a tooth was pulled at age 12. She bruises easily and has frequent nosebleeds. Her mother has similar symptoms. She takes no medications. Hemoglobin is 10.8 g/dL, platelet count is 245,000/mm3, PT is 12 seconds (normal), and aPTT is 39 seconds (upper limit of normal, 35 seconds). Von Willebrand factor antigen is 32 IU/dL, von Willebrand factor activity is 30 IU/dL, and factor VIII activity is 41 IU/dL. A desmopressin trial previously raised all three values above 100 IU/dL. Which of the following is the most appropriate agent to prevent bleeding during the procedure?

- A. Recombinant factor VIIa
- B. Platelet transfusion
- C. Vitamin K
- D. Cryoprecipitate
- E. Desmopressin

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: E

- **정답 핵심**: Mucocutaneous bleeding (menorrhagia, epistaxis, prolonged dental bleeding), autosomal dominant inheritance, normal platelet count and PT, mildly prolonged aPTT, and proportionally reduced VWF antigen, VWF activity, and factor VIII indicate type 1 von Willebrand disease. Because a desmopressin trial raised the levels to normal, desmopressin — which releases stored VWF and factor VIII from endothelial Weibel-Palade bodies — is the agent of choice for minor procedures, often with tranexamic acid.
- **원리**: <b>Why VWF deficiency looks like a platelet problem</b>: VWF links platelet glycoprotein Ib to exposed subendothelial collagen under high shear, so its deficiency impairs primary hemostasis — mucosal bleeding, easy bruising, heavy periods — even though the platelet count is normal.<br> <b>Why the aPTT is long</b>: VWF also carries factor VIII and protects it from clearance; low VWF lowers factor VIII and mildly prolongs the aPTT.<br> <b>How desmopressin works</b>: this V2-receptor agonist triggers endothelial cells to release VWF (and factor VIII) stored in Weibel-Palade bodies, raising levels 2–5-fold within 30–60 minutes. It works only when the patient makes normal VWF (type 1); it is less useful in type 3 (no VWF) and may worsen thrombocytopenia in type 2B. Repeated doses cause tachyphylaxis and hyponatremia, so fluids are restricted.
- **비교**: <table><thead><tr><th style="width:24%">Feature</th><th style="width:38%">Desmopressin (answer)</th><th>Cryoprecipitate (closest distractor)</th></tr></thead><tbody> <tr><td>Source of VWF</td><td><b>Patient's own stores</b></td><td>Pooled donor plasma</td></tr> <tr><td>Best for</td><td>Type 1 with proven response, minor procedures</td><td>Only when VWF concentrate is unavailable</td></tr> <tr><td>Risks</td><td>Hyponatremia, tachyphylaxis</td><td>Infection transmission, volume, allergic reactions</td></tr> </tbody></table> When the patient's own stores are proven adequate, use them first; donor products are for nonresponders, type 3, or major surgery.
- **오답 이유**:
  - (A) Recombinant factor VIIa is correct for hemophilia with inhibitors or Glanzmann thrombasthenia; it bypasses but does not replace missing VWF.
  - (B) Platelet transfusion is correct for thrombocytopenia or a qualitative platelet defect; her platelet count is normal and the defect is in VWF.
  - (C) Vitamin K is correct for warfarin effect or vitamin K deficiency, which prolong the PT first; her PT is normal.
  - (D) Cryoprecipitate contains VWF but is a backup when VWF/factor VIII concentrate is unavailable, for nonresponders or type 3 disease.
- **함정**: Reaching for a blood product because the aPTT is long — a proven desmopressin responder with type 1 disease uses her own VWF stores.
- **학습목표**: 점막 출혈 병력과 혈소판 수 정상·aPTT 경도 연장·폰빌레브란트 인자 활성 저하를 보이는 1형 폰빌레브란트병에서 소수술 전 데스모프레신을 쓴다
