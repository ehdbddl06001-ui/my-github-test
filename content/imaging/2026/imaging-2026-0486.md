## 문제
A 61-year-old man comes to the physician because of headaches and itching after hot showers for 3 months. He has never smoked and does not have snoring or daytime sleepiness. He takes no medications, including testosterone. Blood pressure is 148/88 mm Hg. His face is ruddy, and the spleen tip is palpable 2 cm below the left costal margin. Oxygen saturation is 97% on room air. Hemoglobin is 19.2 g/dL, hematocrit is 58%, leukocyte count is 13,200/mm3, and platelet count is 520,000/mm3. Serum erythropoietin concentration is low. Which of the following is the most appropriate next step in diagnosis?

- A. JAK2 V617F mutation analysis
- B. BCR-ABL1 fusion analysis
- C. Overnight polysomnography
- D. CT of the abdomen to look for a renal mass
- E. Bone marrow aspiration and biopsy

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: A

- **정답 핵심**: Erythrocytosis with leukocytosis, thrombocytosis, splenomegaly, aquagenic pruritus, and a low serum erythropoietin points to a primary (marrow-driven) erythrocytosis — polycythemia vera. JAK2 V617F is present in about 95% of cases and, with the hemoglobin level, is a major diagnostic criterion; JAK2 exon 12 testing follows if V617F is negative.
- **원리**: <b>Why erythropoietin sorts primary from secondary</b>: in secondary erythrocytosis the kidney senses low oxygen (smoking, sleep apnea, high altitude, lung disease) or an erythropoietin-secreting tumor makes the hormone — erythropoietin is normal or high. In polycythemia vera, red cell precursors grow without needing erythropoietin, the raised red-cell mass suppresses the kidney, and erythropoietin is low.<br> <b>Why JAK2</b>: the V617F mutation removes the inhibitory pseudokinase control of JAK2, so the erythropoietin-receptor signal is switched on without the hormone. Because JAK2 also serves the thrombopoietin and G-CSF receptors, all three lines rise — explaining the leukocytosis and thrombocytosis.<br> <b>Why mast cells itch</b>: clonal basophils and mast cells release histamine on warm water exposure, producing aquagenic pruritus; the treatment target is a hematocrit below 45% with phlebotomy and low-dose aspirin.
- **비교**: <table><thead><tr><th style="width:24%">Feature</th><th style="width:38%">JAK2 V617F (answer)</th><th>Bone marrow biopsy (closest distractor)</th></tr></thead><tbody> <tr><td>Role in WHO criteria</td><td><b>Major criterion; noninvasive blood test</b></td><td>Major criterion, but may be omitted when Hb is very high with JAK2 and low EPO</td></tr> <tr><td>Best use</td><td>First confirmatory test after low EPO</td><td>Staging fibrosis, unclear cases, or suspected other MPN</td></tr> <tr><td>Burden</td><td>Peripheral blood</td><td>Invasive</td></tr> </tbody></table> Low EPO plus trilineage rise → JAK2 first; marrow becomes necessary when the molecular test is negative or the picture suggests fibrosis.
- **오답 이유**:
  - (B) BCR-ABL1 analysis would be correct with marked leukocytosis, a left-shifted differential, basophilia, and splenomegaly but without erythrocytosis (chronic myeloid leukemia).
  - (C) Polysomnography would be correct in an obese snorer with daytime sleepiness and a normal or high erythropoietin suggesting hypoxia-driven erythrocytosis.
  - (D) Abdominal imaging for a renal mass would be correct if erythropoietin were inappropriately high without hypoxemia, suggesting a tumor secreting the hormone.
  - (E) Bone marrow biopsy would be the next step if JAK2 V617F and exon 12 testing were negative or if marrow fibrosis needed to be assessed.
- **함정**: Going straight to bone marrow biopsy — a blood JAK2 test is the confirmatory step once erythropoietin is low.
- **학습목표**: 이차 원인 없이 높은 혈색소와 낮은 혈청 에리스로포이에틴을 보이는 환자에서 진성적혈구증가증을 확인하기 위해 JAK2 V617F 돌연변이 검사를 한다
