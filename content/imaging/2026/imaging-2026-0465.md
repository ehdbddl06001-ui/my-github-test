## 문제
A 45-year-old woman who works as a city public health officer reviews a 10-year prospective cohort study that followed 10,000 adults in a city who were free of chronic obstructive pulmonary disease at baseline. At enrollment, 2,000 participants are current smokers and 8,000 have never smoked; this proportion is representative of the city's adult population. During follow-up, 80 of the smokers and 80 of the never-smokers develop the disease. She wants to know what proportion of all new cases of the disease in the city's adult population could be prevented if smoking were completely eliminated, assuming the association is causal and there is no confounding. Which of the following is the closest estimate?

- A. 50 %
- B. 25 %
- C. 3 %
- D. 38 %
- E. 75 %

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: D

- **정답 핵심**: Incidence in smokers is 80/2,000 = 4.0 %; in never-smokers 80/8,000 = 1.0 %; relative risk = 4. The total population incidence is 160/10,000 = 1.6 %. The population attributable fraction is (1.6 − 1.0)/1.6 = 37.5 %, which by the prevalence formula is Pe(RR − 1)/[1 + Pe(RR − 1)] = 0.2 × 3/(1 + 0.6) = 0.375. This rounds to 38 %; 75 % is the attributable fraction among the exposed only.
- **원리**: <b>Two different questions</b>: the attributable fraction in the exposed asks, "Among smokers who got the disease, what share is due to smoking?" — (RR − 1)/RR = 3/4 = 75 %. The population attributable fraction asks, "Among all cases in the population, what share would disappear without the exposure?" — it shrinks when few people are exposed, because many cases occur in never-smokers anyway.<br> <b>Computing it two ways</b>: (incidence in total population − incidence in unexposed)/incidence in total population = (1.6 − 1.0)/1.6 = 37.5 %. Or Pe(RR − 1)/[1 + Pe(RR − 1)] with Pe = 0.20 and RR = 4, giving 0.6/1.6 = 37.5 %. Half of the cases (80 of 160) occur in smokers, but even without smoking those 2,000 people would have had 20 cases (1 %), so 60 of 160 cases (37.5 %) are preventable.<br> <b>Why it matters for policy</b>: a strong risk factor that few people have can have a smaller population impact than a modest risk factor that many people have.

- **오답 이유**:
  - (A) 50 % is the share of all cases that occurred in smokers (80 of 160); it ignores the cases smokers would have had anyway.
  - (B) 25 % is the reciprocal of the relative risk (1/4), the proportion of smokers' risk they would have even without smoking.
  - (C) 3 % is the risk difference (4.0 % − 1.0 %), an absolute measure in the exposed, not a fraction of all cases.
  - (E) 75 % is correct if the question asks what proportion of cases among smokers is attributable to smoking, (RR − 1)/RR.
- **함정**: Answering 75 % — that is the attributable fraction among smokers; the question asks about all cases in the whole population.
- **학습목표**: 코호트 연구에서 노출군 기여위험분율과 인구집단 기여위험분율을 구별하고, 노출 유병률과 상대위험도로 인구집단 기여위험분율을 계산한다
