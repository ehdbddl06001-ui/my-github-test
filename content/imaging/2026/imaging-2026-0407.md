## 문제
A 27-year-old woman, gravida 2, para 1, at 30 weeks' gestation comes to the emergency department because of a sudden gush of clear fluid from the vagina 3 hours ago. She has had no contractions, abdominal pain, or vaginal bleeding. Her temperature is 36.9°C (98.4°F), pulse is 84/min, and blood pressure is 118/72 mm Hg. The uterus is soft and nontender. Sterile speculum examination shows pooling of clear fluid in the posterior fornix; nitrazine paper turns blue, and a ferning pattern is seen on microscopy. The cervix appears closed. Fetal heart rate tracing shows a baseline of 140/min with moderate variability and accelerations, and no contractions are recorded. Ultrasonography shows a singleton fetus in cephalic presentation with decreased amniotic fluid. Which of the following is the most appropriate next step in management?

- A. Cesarean delivery within the next few hours
- B. Indomethacin tocolysis without antibiotics
- C. Discharge home with weekly ultrasonography
- D. Betamethasone plus ampicillin and erythromycin
- E. Induction of labor with intravenous oxytocin

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: D

- **정답 핵심**: Pooling, a positive nitrazine test, and ferning confirm preterm prelabor rupture of membranes at 30 weeks. There is no fever, uterine tenderness, labor, or fetal compromise, so expectant management is chosen: a course of antenatal corticosteroids to mature the fetal lungs and latency antibiotics (ampicillin plus erythromycin/azithromycin) to prolong pregnancy and reduce infection, with inpatient monitoring.
- **원리**: <b>Why gestational age decides</b>: after membranes rupture, the risks of staying pregnant (chorioamnionitis, abruption, cord compression) must be weighed against the risks of prematurity. Before 34 weeks, prematurity (respiratory distress syndrome, intraventricular hemorrhage, necrotizing enterocolitis) dominates, so pregnancy is prolonged if mother and fetus are well; from 34 weeks on, delivery is recommended.<br> <b>Why corticosteroids</b>: betamethasone crosses the placenta and induces surfactant production and structural lung maturation; the full benefit appears 24 hours to 7 days after the first dose.<br> <b>Why antibiotics</b>: ascending infection is both a cause and a consequence of rupture. A 7-day course of ampicillin plus a macrolide prolongs the latency period and lowers rates of chorioamnionitis and neonatal sepsis. Amoxicillin-clavulanate is avoided because it increases necrotizing enterocolitis.<br> <b>Other pieces</b>: magnesium sulfate is added for neuroprotection if delivery before 32 weeks becomes imminent, and group B streptococcal prophylaxis is given in labor.
- **비교**: <table><thead><tr><th style="width:22%">Feature</th><th style="width:39%">Expectant management (answer)</th><th>Induction of labor (closest distractor)</th></tr></thead><tbody> <tr><td>Gestational age</td><td><b>Below 34 weeks</b></td><td>34 weeks or later</td></tr> <tr><td>Infection</td><td>No fever, nontender uterus</td><td>Chorioamnionitis at any gestational age</td></tr> <tr><td>Fetus</td><td>Reassuring tracing</td><td>Nonreassuring status or abruption</td></tr> </tbody></table> At 30 weeks with a well mother and fetus, the gain from each additional week outweighs the infection risk; the same rupture at 35 weeks, or with fever and a tender uterus, calls for delivery.
- **오답 이유**:
  - (A) Cesarean delivery would be correct for an obstetric indication such as malpresentation in labor, cord prolapse, or a nonreassuring tracing that cannot wait for a vaginal birth.
  - (B) Tocolysis is not recommended in ruptured membranes because it does not improve outcomes and may mask infection; antibiotics are needed regardless.
  - (C) Outpatient care would only be considered after a period of stable inpatient observation in selected cases; on the day of rupture she needs admission and treatment.
  - (E) Induction of labor would be correct if the rupture occurred at 34 weeks or later, or if chorioamnionitis or nonreassuring fetal status developed.
- **함정**: Delivering because membranes have ruptured — before 34 weeks without infection or fetal compromise, prolonging pregnancy with steroids and antibiotics is better.
- **학습목표**: 임신 34주 미만의 조기 양막 파열에서 감염·태아 곤란이 없으면 기대 요법으로 코르티코스테로이드와 잠복기 항생제를 주고, 34주 이후 분만과 구별한다
