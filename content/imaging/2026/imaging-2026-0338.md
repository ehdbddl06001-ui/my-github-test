## 문제
A 42-year-old woman comes to the physician because of difficulty swallowing both solids and liquids for 1 year. She often regurgitates undigested food at night and has lost 5 kg. She has no heartburn, Raynaud phenomenon, or skin tightening. She was born in and has always lived in the United States. Vital signs are within normal limits. Physical examination shows no abnormalities. Upper endoscopy shows retained food in a dilated esophagus and no mass at the gastroesophageal junction. High-resolution esophageal manometry shows incomplete relaxation of the lower esophageal sphincter and absent peristalsis in the esophageal body. Which of the following is the most likely underlying cause of this patient's condition?

- A. Eosinophilic infiltration of the esophageal mucosa
- B. Destruction of ganglion cells by Trypanosoma cruzi
- C. Tumor infiltration at the gastroesophageal junction
- D. Loss of inhibitory neurons in the myenteric plexus
- E. Smooth muscle atrophy and fibrosis of the distal esophagus

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: D

- **정답 핵심**: Dysphagia to both solids and liquids from the start, nocturnal regurgitation of undigested food, a dilated esophagus with retained food, and manometry showing incomplete lower esophageal sphincter (LES) relaxation with absent peristalsis define achalasia. Primary achalasia results from loss of inhibitory (nitric oxide– and VIP-releasing) neurons in the myenteric (Auerbach) plexus, so the LES cannot relax and peristalsis is lost.
- **원리**: <b>Why losing inhibitory neurons closes the sphincter</b>: the LES has intrinsic tone from its smooth muscle and excitatory cholinergic input. Swallowing normally triggers inhibitory myenteric neurons that release nitric oxide and vasoactive intestinal peptide, relaxing the sphincter so the bolus can pass. In achalasia, an inflammatory (probably autoimmune, possibly virus-triggered) process destroys these inhibitory neurons; the excitatory input remains, so the LES fails to relax and its resting pressure is often high.<br> <b>Why peristalsis disappears</b>: coordinated peristalsis also depends on a gradient of inhibitory neural activity along the esophageal body — the latency of inhibition increases distally, producing a sequential wave. Without inhibitory neurons, contractions are absent or simultaneous.<br> <b>Why solids and liquids from the start</b>: the obstruction is functional and complete at every swallow, unlike a mechanical stricture or tumor, which first blocks solids. Pseudoachalasia from a cardia tumor must be excluded, especially in older patients with rapid weight loss.
- **비교**: <table><thead><tr><th style="width:22%">Feature</th><th style="width:39%">Achalasia (answer)</th><th>Systemic sclerosis esophagus (closest distractor)</th></tr></thead><tbody> <tr><td>Mechanism</td><td><b>Loss of inhibitory myenteric neurons</b> (NO, VIP)</td><td>Smooth muscle atrophy and fibrosis</td></tr> <tr><td>LES</td><td>Fails to relax; resting pressure normal or high</td><td>Low pressure — incompetent</td></tr> <tr><td>Main symptom</td><td>Dysphagia to solids and liquids, regurgitation</td><td>Severe reflux (heartburn), strictures</td></tr> <tr><td>Associated findings</td><td>Dilated esophagus, bird-beak narrowing</td><td>Raynaud phenomenon, skin thickening</td></tr> </tbody></table> Both lose peristalsis; the sphincter separates them — non-relaxing in achalasia, wide open in scleroderma.
- **오답 이유**:
  - (A) Eosinophilic infiltration would be correct in eosinophilic esophagitis, typically a young atopic man with food impaction and rings or furrows on endoscopy but normal LES relaxation.
  - (B) Trypanosoma cruzi destruction of ganglion cells would be correct for Chagas disease in a patient from rural Latin America, often with cardiomyopathy or megacolon.
  - (C) Tumor infiltration at the junction would be correct for pseudoachalasia in an older patient with rapid weight loss and a mass at the cardia on endoscopy or endoscopic ultrasonography.
  - (E) Smooth muscle atrophy and fibrosis would be correct in systemic sclerosis, with heartburn, Raynaud phenomenon, skin tightening, and a hypotensive rather than non-relaxing LES.
- **함정**: Choosing systemic sclerosis because peristalsis is absent — both lose peristalsis, but achalasia has a non-relaxing LES while scleroderma has a weak, open LES with reflux.
- **학습목표**: 고형식·액체 모두의 연하곤란과 식도 내압검사(하부식도괄약근 이완 장애·연동 소실)로 이완불능증을 진단하고, 근육층 신경얼기의 억제 신경세포 소실을 기전으로 고르며 전신경화증·샤가스병과 구별한다
