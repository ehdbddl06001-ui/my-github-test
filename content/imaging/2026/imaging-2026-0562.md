## 문제
A 27-year-old man comes to the emergency department because of chest pain for 2 days. The pain is sharp, worsens when he breathes in or lies down, and improves when he sits up and leans forward. Ten days ago, he had a runny nose and sore throat. His temperature is 37.8°C (100°F), pulse is 96/min, respirations are 18/min, and blood pressure is 124/76 mm Hg. There is no jugular venous distention. A three-component scratching sound is heard at the left lower sternal border. An ECG shows concave ST-segment elevation and PR-segment depression in multiple leads. High-sensitivity troponin is below the upper reference limit. Echocardiography shows a small pericardial effusion with normal wall motion. Which of the following is the most appropriate treatment?

- A. Pericardiocentesis
- B. Intravenous heparin
- C. Ibuprofen and colchicine
- D. High-dose prednisone
- E. Aspirin, clopidogrel, and coronary angiography

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: C

- **정답 핵심**: Positional pleuritic chest pain, a pericardial friction rub, diffuse concave ST elevation with PR depression, and a small effusion meet the diagnostic criteria for acute pericarditis (at least two of four). The preceding upper respiratory infection suggests a viral (idiopathic) cause, and normal troponin and wall motion make myocardial involvement or infarction unlikely. With no high-risk features (fever above 38°C, large effusion, tamponade, immunosuppression, anticoagulation), he is treated as an outpatient with a high-dose NSAID (or aspirin) plus colchicine for 3 months; colchicine speeds symptom relief and roughly halves recurrence.
- **원리**: <b>Why ST rises in many leads</b>: The inflammation of pericarditis spreads over the whole epicardial surface of the myocardium, so it is not limited to one coronary territory. Concave ST elevation appears in nearly all leads except aVR and V1, and atrial surface injury produces PR depression (with PR elevation in aVR). The absence of reciprocal ST depression and Q waves separates it from infarction.<br> <b>Why sitting forward helps</b>: Pain and the rub arise when the inflamed parietal and visceral pericardium rub against each other. Leaning forward moves the heart away from the anterior pericardium and lessens friction.<br> <b>Why an NSAID plus colchicine</b>: NSAIDs reduce prostaglandins and relieve pain and inflammation, while colchicine blocks neutrophil microtubule assembly and inflammasome activation, breaking the inflammatory loop that underlies recurrence. Early glucocorticoids increase recurrences and are reserved for NSAID contraindications or autoimmune causes.
- **비교**: <table><thead><tr><th style="width:24%">Feature</th><th style="width:38%">Acute pericarditis (answer)</th><th>ST-elevation MI (closest distractor)</th></tr></thead><tbody> <tr><td>Pain</td><td>Sharp, changes with <b>position and breathing</b></td><td>Pressure-like, unrelated to position</td></tr> <tr><td>ST change</td><td>Diffuse concave elevation + PR depression, no reciprocal change</td><td>Convex elevation in one territory + reciprocal depression</td></tr> <tr><td>Troponin, wall motion</td><td>Normal (elevated if myocarditis coexists)</td><td>Elevated, regional wall-motion abnormality</td></tr> </tbody></table> The same ST elevation means different things: its distribution, PR change, troponin, and wall motion decide whether the patient goes to the catheterization laboratory.
- **오답 이유**:
  - (A) Pericardiocentesis would be correct for a large effusion with tamponade (hypotension, jugular venous distention, pulsus paradoxus) or suspected purulent or malignant effusion.
  - (B) Heparin would be correct for pulmonary embolism or acute coronary syndrome; in pericarditis it risks hemorrhage into the pericardial space.
  - (D) High-dose prednisone would be correct when NSAIDs and colchicine are contraindicated or when pericarditis is due to autoimmune disease; used first, it increases recurrence.
  - (E) Antiplatelet therapy and angiography would be correct for convex ST elevation in one coronary territory with reciprocal depression, elevated troponin, and regional wall-motion loss.
- **함정**: Treating the ST elevation as myocardial infarction — diffuse distribution, PR depression, and normal troponin and wall motion indicate pericarditis.
- **학습목표**: 감기 뒤 앉아서 앞으로 숙이면 나아지는 흉통·심막마찰음·광범위 ST 상승과 PR 하강을 급성 심막염으로 판단하고 NSAID 와 콜히친을 함께 쓴다
