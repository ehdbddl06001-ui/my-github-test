## 문제
A 62-year-old woman comes to the emergency department because of crampy periumbilical abdominal pain, vomiting, and abdominal distention for 18 hours. She has not passed flatus since this morning. She underwent open hysterectomy 6 years ago. Temperature is 37.2°C (99°F), pulse is 94/min, and blood pressure is 128/80 mm Hg. The abdomen is distended and tympanic with high-pitched bowel sounds and mild diffuse tenderness without guarding or rebound. There is no hernia. Leukocyte count is 9,800/mm3, and serum lactate concentration is 1.4 mmol/L. CT of the abdomen with contrast shows dilated small-bowel loops with a transition point in the lower abdomen, collapsed distal ileum and colon, normal bowel-wall enhancement, and no free air or mesenteric edema. Which of the following is the most appropriate next step in management?

- A. Oral polyethylene glycol bowel preparation
- B. Nasogastric decompression and intravenous fluids
- C. Emergency exploratory laparotomy
- D. Colonoscopic decompression of the bowel
- E. Intravenous neostigmine infusion

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: B

- **정답 핵심**: Crampy pain, vomiting, distention, and obstipation after previous open pelvic surgery, with a CT <b>transition point and collapsed distal bowel</b>, indicate adhesive small-bowel obstruction. There are <b>no signs of strangulation</b>: no fever, normal leukocyte count and lactate, no peritonitis, and normal bowel-wall enhancement without mesenteric edema or free air. Most such obstructions resolve with <b>bowel rest, nasogastric decompression, and fluid and electrolyte replacement</b>.
- **원리**: <b>Why most adhesive obstructions resolve</b>: Adhesions usually kink the bowel rather than occlude it completely. Decompressing the proximal bowel with a nasogastric tube lowers intraluminal pressure, reduces wall edema, and lets the kink relax. About 70–80% of partial adhesive obstructions resolve within 48–72 hours without surgery.<br> <b>Why strangulation changes everything</b>: When a loop is twisted or trapped in a closed loop, venous outflow stops first, then arterial inflow; the wall becomes ischemic, then necrotic, and perforates. Fever, constant pain, peritoneal signs, leukocytosis, rising lactate, reduced wall enhancement, mesenteric edema, or free fluid suggest this, and the patient then needs laparotomy without delay.<br> <b>Role of contrast</b>: A water-soluble contrast challenge can predict resolution — contrast reaching the colon within about 24 hours — and may shorten the course.
- **비교**: <table><thead><tr><th style="width:24%">Feature</th><th style="width:38%">Uncomplicated (answer)</th><th>Strangulated — laparotomy (closest distractor)</th></tr></thead><tbody> <tr><td>Pain</td><td>Crampy, intermittent</td><td>Constant, out of proportion</td></tr> <tr><td>Examination</td><td><b>Mild tenderness, no peritonitis</b></td><td>Guarding, rebound, fever, tachycardia</td></tr> <tr><td>Laboratory</td><td>Normal leukocytes and lactate</td><td>Leukocytosis, rising lactate</td></tr> <tr><td>CT</td><td><b>Normal wall enhancement</b></td><td>Poor enhancement, mesenteric edema, closed loop, pneumatosis</td></tr> </tbody></table> Treat the obstruction nonoperatively only while every sign of ischemia is absent; any one of them moves the patient to the operating room.
- **오답 이유**:
  - (A) A polyethylene glycol preparation would be appropriate before colonoscopy or for constipation, but giving it in a mechanical obstruction worsens vomiting and distention.
  - (C) Emergency laparotomy would be correct if there were peritonitis, fever, rising lactate, or CT evidence of a closed loop or reduced bowel-wall enhancement.
  - (D) Colonoscopic decompression would be correct for acute colonic pseudo-obstruction or sigmoid volvulus with a dilated colon, not for small-bowel obstruction.
  - (E) Neostigmine would be correct for acute colonic pseudo-obstruction (Ogilvie syndrome) without a mechanical transition point after conservative measures fail.
- **함정**: A transition point on CT sounds surgical — without signs of ischemia, adhesive obstruction is first treated with decompression.
- **학습목표**: Manage uncomplicated adhesive small-bowel obstruction with bowel rest, nasogastric decompression, and fluids, and recognize the findings that would require urgent laparotomy
