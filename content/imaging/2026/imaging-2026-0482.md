## 문제
A 52-year-old woman comes to the physician for follow-up of hypertension. Her blood pressure has been difficult to control for 4 years despite amlodipine, losartan, and hydrochlorothiazide; 6 weeks ago, losartan and hydrochlorothiazide were replaced with doxazosin and diltiazem. Today, blood pressure is 162/98 mm Hg. Serum potassium is 3.1 mEq/L and creatinine is 0.8 mg/dL. Plasma aldosterone concentration is 28 ng/dL, and plasma renin activity is less than 0.2 ng/mL/h. After an oral sodium loading test, 24-hour urinary aldosterone excretion is 18 μg. CT of the abdomen shows a 1.1-cm low-attenuation nodule in the left adrenal gland; the right adrenal gland appears normal. She says she would choose surgery if it could cure her condition. Which of the following is the most appropriate next step in management?

- A. Left laparoscopic adrenalectomy
- B. Lifelong spironolactone without further testing
- C. Fludrocortisone suppression test
- D. MRI of the abdomen
- E. Adrenal venous sampling

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: E

- **정답 핵심**: Hypokalemia with resistant hypertension, a high aldosterone-to-renin ratio, and failure of aldosterone to suppress after salt loading confirm primary aldosteronism. Because she is over 35 years old and wants surgery, adrenal venous sampling is needed to prove that aldosterone excess comes from the left gland — a small CT nodule may be a nonfunctioning incidentaloma while the true disease is bilateral hyperplasia or a contralateral adenoma.
- **원리**: <b>Why renin is suppressed</b>: autonomous aldosterone makes the kidney retain sodium and excrete potassium and hydrogen. The expanded volume shuts off renin release — so a high aldosterone with low renin points to a primary adrenal source, and salt loading that should suppress aldosterone fails to do so.<br> <b>Why CT is not enough</b>: CT shows anatomy, not hormone secretion. Nonfunctioning adrenal nodules become common after age 35–40, and bilateral adrenal hyperplasia (the cause in about two thirds of cases) may show small nodules or none. Relying on CT alone sends many patients to the wrong operation or to an operation that cannot cure them.<br> <b>What adrenal venous sampling measures</b>: aldosterone is sampled from both adrenal veins and corrected for cortisol (to account for dilution). A clearly higher ratio on one side (lateralization index usually > 4) means unilateral disease cured or improved by adrenalectomy; no lateralization means bilateral disease treated with a mineralocorticoid receptor antagonist.
- **비교**: <table><thead><tr><th style="width:24%">Feature</th><th style="width:38%">Adrenal venous sampling first (answer)</th><th>Adrenalectomy now (closest distractor)</th></tr></thead><tbody> <tr><td>Question answered</td><td><b>Which gland secretes the excess aldosterone</b></td><td>Assumes the CT nodule is the source</td></tr> <tr><td>When skipping AVS is acceptable</td><td>—</td><td>Age &lt; 35, spontaneous hypokalemia, marked excess, clear unilateral adenoma</td></tr> <tr><td>Risk if wrong</td><td>Invasive but diagnostic</td><td>Removing a normal gland; disease persists</td></tr> </tbody></table> In a younger patient with a classic picture, surgery may follow CT directly; at 52 the chance of an incidental nodule is too high to skip lateralization.
- **오답 이유**:
  - (A) Adrenalectomy becomes correct after adrenal venous sampling lateralizes to the left, or in a patient under 35 with a classic unilateral adenoma and marked aldosterone excess.
  - (B) Lifelong spironolactone without further testing is correct when the patient declines or cannot undergo surgery, or when sampling shows bilateral disease.
  - (C) A fludrocortisone suppression test is an alternative confirmatory test, but confirmation has already been achieved by the oral sodium loading result here.
  - (D) MRI offers no advantage over CT for adrenal anatomy and, like CT, cannot show which gland is secreting aldosterone.
- **함정**: Removing the gland that shows the nodule on CT — at age 52, the nodule may be nonfunctioning; secretion must be lateralized before surgery.
- **학습목표**: 저칼륨혈증을 동반한 저항성 고혈압에서 알도스테론/레닌 비 상승과 확진 검사로 원발알도스테론증을 확인한 뒤, 35세 이상에서는 CT 결절이 한쪽이어도 수술 전 부신정맥 채혈로 편측성을 확인한다
