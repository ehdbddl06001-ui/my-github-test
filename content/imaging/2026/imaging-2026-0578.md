## 문제
A 34-year-old man comes to the physician because of fatigue and episodes of dark urine for 4 months. The urine is darkest in the morning. Two weeks ago, he was evaluated for abdominal pain and was found to have thrombosis of the hepatic veins on Doppler ultrasonography. He takes no medications. His pulse is 96/min, and blood pressure is 122/76 mm Hg. Examination shows conjunctival pallor and mild scleral icterus; the liver is palpable 3 cm below the right costal margin. Hemoglobin is 8.9 g/dL, mean corpuscular volume is 104 μm3, leukocyte count is 3,100/mm3, platelet count is 88,000/mm3, and reticulocyte count is 6%. Serum lactate dehydrogenase is 1,850 U/L, and haptoglobin is undetectable. A direct antiglobulin (Coombs) test is negative. A peripheral blood smear shows no schistocytes or spherocytes. Urinalysis shows a positive dipstick test for blood with no red blood cells on microscopy. Which of the following is the most appropriate test to confirm the diagnosis?

- A. Bone marrow biopsy
- B. Flow cytometry for CD55 and CD59 on peripheral blood cells
- C. JAK2 V617F mutation analysis
- D. Osmotic fragility test
- E. ADAMTS13 activity assay

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: B

- **정답 핵심**: Hemoglobinuria (positive dipstick without red cells), very high LDH, and absent haptoglobin indicate intravascular hemolysis; the direct antiglobulin test is negative, and the smear shows neither schistocytes nor spherocytes. Hemolysis together with leukopenia and thrombocytopenia and thrombosis at an unusual site (hepatic veins) is the classic triad of paroxysmal nocturnal hemoglobinuria. Flow cytometry showing loss of GPI-anchored proteins (CD55, CD59) on red cells and of FLAER binding on granulocytes and monocytes confirms the diagnosis and quantifies the clone.
- **원리**: <b>Why complement attacks the cells</b>: PNH arises from an acquired somatic mutation of PIGA in a hematopoietic stem cell. PIGA is needed to build the glycosylphosphatidylinositol (GPI) anchor, so the clone's progeny lack all GPI-anchored proteins, including CD55 (decay-accelerating factor) and CD59 (membrane inhibitor of reactive lysis). Without CD59, the membrane attack complex assembles on red cells and lyses them inside vessels, releasing free hemoglobin into the urine.<br> <b>Why thrombosis</b>: Free hemoglobin scavenges nitric oxide, and complement-activated platelets and monocytes release procoagulant microparticles. Thrombosis occurs at unusual sites such as the hepatic, portal, mesenteric, and cerebral veins, and is the leading cause of death.<br> <b>Why cytopenias</b>: The PIGA clone often expands in a marrow injured by immune attack, so PNH frequently overlaps with aplastic anemia, producing leukopenia and thrombocytopenia. Complement C5 inhibitors (eculizumab, ravulizumab) stop intravascular hemolysis and reduce thrombosis.
- **비교**: <table><thead><tr><th style="width:24%">Feature</th><th style="width:38%">PNH (answer)</th><th>Myeloproliferative neoplasm with Budd-Chiari (JAK2 distractor)</th></tr></thead><tbody> <tr><td>Blood counts</td><td>Anemia + <b>leukopenia + thrombocytopenia</b></td><td>Often high hemoglobin, platelets, or leukocytes</td></tr> <tr><td>Hemolysis</td><td>Intravascular, Coombs negative, hemoglobinuria</td><td>Absent</td></tr> <tr><td>Confirming test</td><td>Flow cytometry (CD55, CD59, FLAER)</td><td>JAK2 V617F</td></tr> </tbody></table> Both cause hepatic vein thrombosis in young adults; hemolysis and low counts send the work-up to flow cytometry rather than JAK2.
- **오답 이유**:
  - (A) Bone marrow biopsy would be correct for unexplained pancytopenia without hemolysis to assess aplastic anemia, myelodysplasia, or infiltration.
  - (C) JAK2 V617F analysis would be correct for hepatic vein thrombosis with high hematocrit or platelet count and no hemolysis, suggesting a myeloproliferative neoplasm.
  - (D) An osmotic fragility test (or EMA binding) would be correct for extravascular hemolysis with spherocytes, splenomegaly, and a family history.
  - (E) ADAMTS13 activity would be correct for microangiopathic hemolysis with schistocytes, severe thrombocytopenia, and neurologic or renal involvement.
- **함정**: Ordering JAK2 because of hepatic vein thrombosis — Coombs-negative intravascular hemolysis with low leukocyte and platelet counts points to PNH.
- **학습목표**: 쿰스 음성 혈관내 용혈·혈구감소·간정맥 혈전을 발작야간혈색소뇨증으로 판단하고 유세포분석(CD55·CD59, FLAER)으로 확진한다
