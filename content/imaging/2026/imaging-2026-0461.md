## 문제
A 56-year-old man with alcohol-associated cirrhosis is brought to the emergency department 1 hour after vomiting large amounts of bright red blood. He has no fever or abdominal pain. Blood pressure is 92/58 mm Hg, pulse is 116/min, and temperature is 36.8°C. He is alert. The abdomen is mildly distended with shifting dullness and is nontender. Hemoglobin is 7.4 g/dL, platelet count is 72,000/mm3, and INR is 1.6. Two large-bore intravenous lines are placed, and a restrictive transfusion of packed red blood cells is started with a target hemoglobin of 7–8 g/dL. Intravenous octreotide is begun, and endoscopy is scheduled within 12 hours. In addition to these measures, which of the following is most likely to decrease this patient's mortality?

- A. Intravenous pantoprazole infusion
- B. Intravenous ceftriaxone
- C. Intravenous propranolol
- D. Intravenous vitamin K
- E. Fresh frozen plasma to correct the INR

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: B

- **정답 핵심**: Cirrhotic patients with gastrointestinal bleeding have a high risk of bacterial infection (spontaneous bacterial peritonitis, bacteremia) from gut bacterial translocation, and infection increases rebleeding and death. Short-term antibiotic prophylaxis — ceftriaxone 1 g/day for up to 7 days in advanced cirrhosis — reduces infection, rebleeding, and mortality, so it is given alongside vasoactive drugs and endoscopic band ligation.
- **원리**: <b>Why bleeding invites infection</b>: in cirrhosis, portal hypertension makes the gut wall edematous and leaky, bacteria overgrow, and immune function (complement, neutrophils, Kupffer cells) is weak. Hemorrhagic shock further damages the mucosa, so bacteria translocate to mesenteric nodes, ascites, and blood within days.<br> <b>Why infection makes bleeding worse</b>: endotoxin and cytokines raise portal pressure and impair hemostasis, so infected patients rebleed more and die more often. Prophylactic antibiotics are one of the few interventions shown to lower mortality in variceal bleeding.<br> <b>Other parts of the bundle</b>: restrictive transfusion (overtransfusion raises portal pressure), octreotide or terlipressin to reduce splanchnic flow, and band ligation within 12 hours. Nonselective beta-blockers are for secondary prevention after the patient is stable, not during shock.
- **비교**: <table><thead><tr><th style="width:24%">Measure</th><th style="width:38%">Ceftriaxone prophylaxis (answer)</th><th>Propranolol (closest distractor)</th></tr></thead><tbody> <tr><td>Timing</td><td><b>At admission, during the acute bleed</b></td><td>After bleeding stops and hemodynamics are stable</td></tr> <tr><td>Target</td><td>Bacterial translocation and infection</td><td>Portal pressure (prevent rebleeding)</td></tr> <tr><td>In shock</td><td>Safe</td><td>Worsens hypotension, blunts compensatory tachycardia</td></tr> </tbody></table> Both lower long-term risk, but only one belongs in the first hour of a bleeding, hypotensive patient.
- **오답 이유**:
  - (A) A proton pump inhibitor infusion is correct for bleeding peptic ulcer with high-risk stigmata; it has no proven effect on variceal bleeding outcomes.
  - (C) Propranolol is correct for secondary prophylaxis after the acute bleed has stopped and blood pressure is stable; during shock it worsens hypotension.
  - (D) Vitamin K would be correct for vitamin K deficiency or warfarin effect; in cirrhosis the INR reflects poor synthesis and does not correct meaningfully.
  - (E) Plasma to correct the INR is not recommended because the INR does not predict bleeding in cirrhosis and the volume raises portal pressure.
- **함정**: Focusing on the abnormal INR — in cirrhosis the step that lowers mortality is antibiotic prophylaxis, not correcting the number.
- **학습목표**: 간경변 환자의 정맥류 출혈에서는 혈관작용약·내시경 결찰과 함께 예방적 항생제(세프트리악손)를 주어 감염과 재출혈·사망을 줄임을 안다
