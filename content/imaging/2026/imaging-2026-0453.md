## 문제
A 74-year-old man is brought to the physician by his daughter because of progressive difficulty walking for 1 year. He feels as if his feet are stuck to the floor, and he has fallen twice. During the past 6 months, he has developed urinary urgency with occasional incontinence and has become slow in planning daily activities. He has hypertension treated with lisinopril. There is no tremor. Examination shows a broad-based gait with short, shuffling steps and difficulty initiating and turning; arm swing is preserved. Muscle tone is normal, and there is no bradykinesia of finger tapping. Mini-Mental State Examination score is 25/30. MRI of the brain shows enlargement of the lateral and third ventricles out of proportion to sulcal widening, with tight high-convexity sulci and no obstructing mass. Which of the following is the most appropriate next step?

- A. Large-volume lumbar puncture with gait assessment before and after
- B. Trial of carbidopa-levodopa
- C. Ventriculoperitoneal shunt placement without further testing
- D. Donepezil therapy
- E. Oxybutynin therapy

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: A

- **정답 핵심**: Gait apraxia that began first, followed by urinary urgency/incontinence and executive slowing, with ventriculomegaly out of proportion to sulcal atrophy and tight high-convexity sulci, is idiopathic normal pressure hydrocephalus. Before committing to a shunt, a high-volume (30–50 mL) lumbar tap with timed gait testing before and after identifies patients likely to benefit.
- **원리**: <b>Why the gait goes first</b>: the enlarged lateral ventricles stretch periventricular white matter fibers — the medial corticospinal and frontal-subcortical fibers that run to the legs and to the bladder control centers. Leg fibers lie closest to the ventricles, so a magnetic, broad-based gait appears before incontinence and dementia.<br> <b>What "normal pressure" means</b>: opening pressure is usually normal or only intermittently high, because CSF absorption is impaired and ventricular compliance has changed; the damage comes from pulsatile stretch rather than sustained high pressure.<br> <b>Why test before shunting</b>: a shunt carries risks of subdural hematoma, infection, and overdrainage in older adults, and many look-alike conditions (vascular parkinsonism, Alzheimer disease, atrophy with ex vacuo ventriculomegaly) will not improve. Temporary removal of CSF mimics the shunt; a clear gait improvement predicts a good surgical response.
- **비교**: <table><thead><tr><th style="width:22%">Feature</th><th style="width:39%">Normal pressure hydrocephalus — tap test (answer)</th><th>Parkinson disease — levodopa trial (closest distractor)</th></tr></thead><tbody> <tr><td>Gait</td><td><b>Broad-based, magnetic, preserved arm swing</b></td><td>Narrow-based, festinating, reduced arm swing</td></tr> <tr><td>Limb signs</td><td>Normal tone, no finger-tap bradykinesia</td><td>Rigidity, bradykinesia, rest tremor</td></tr> <tr><td>Imaging</td><td>Ventricles out of proportion to sulci, tight convexity</td><td>Usually normal</td></tr> <tr><td>Next step</td><td>High-volume lumbar tap → shunt if responsive</td><td>Dopaminergic therapy</td></tr> </tbody></table> "Lower-body parkinsonism" with normal upper limbs and enlarged ventricles points to the CSF, not the substantia nigra.
- **오답 이유**:
  - (B) A levodopa trial is correct when asymmetric bradykinesia, rigidity, rest tremor, and reduced arm swing suggest Parkinson disease; his upper limbs are normal.
  - (C) Shunt placement is correct after a positive tap test or external lumbar drainage confirms responsiveness; doing it first exposes him to risk without predicting benefit.
  - (D) Donepezil is correct for Alzheimer disease with early memory loss and atrophy matching ventricular size; here gait came first and the ventricles are disproportionate.
  - (E) Oxybutynin is correct for isolated overactive bladder; here urgency is part of a treatable syndrome, and anticholinergics can worsen cognition in the elderly.
- **함정**: Treating each symptom separately — the triad of gait, bladder, and cognition with disproportionate ventricles is one CSF disorder that needs a tap test.
- **학습목표**: 걸음 장애가 먼저 오고 요실금·인지 저하가 뒤따르며 뇌고랑에 비해 뇌실이 큰 정상압수두증에서 다량 요추천자(배액 검사)로 단락술 반응을 예측함을 안다
