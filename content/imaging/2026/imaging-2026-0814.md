## 문제
A 19-year-old woman is referred before extraction of an impacted wisdom tooth. She has had heavy menstrual periods since menarche, frequent nosebleeds, and prolonged bleeding after a previous tooth extraction. Her mother has similar symptoms. She takes no medications. Physical examination shows no petechiae or joint deformity. Laboratory studies show a platelet count of 250,000/mm3, normal prothrombin time, and activated partial thromboplastin time of 38 seconds (reference 25–35). Von Willebrand factor antigen is 28%, von Willebrand factor activity is 26%, and factor VIII activity is 40%. Multimer analysis shows all multimer sizes present in normal proportions. A trial infusion 2 months ago raised her von Willebrand factor activity to 85%. Which of the following is the most appropriate prophylaxis before the procedure?

- A. Desmopressin
- B. Recombinant factor VIII concentrate
- C. Platelet transfusion
- D. Fresh frozen plasma
- E. Vitamin K

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: A

- **정답 핵심**: Mucocutaneous bleeding (menorrhagia, epistaxis, dental bleeding) with <b>autosomal dominant inheritance</b>, a normal platelet count, and a <b>proportionate reduction of VWF antigen and activity (ratio ≈ 0.9)</b> with <b>normal multimers</b> is <b>type 1 von Willebrand disease</b>. The mildly prolonged aPTT reflects low factor VIII, which VWF normally carries. Her documented response to a trial dose shows that her endothelial stores can be released, so <b>desmopressin</b> is the prophylaxis of choice, often with tranexamic acid for dental work.
- **원리**: <b>What desmopressin does</b>: Desmopressin (DDAVP) is a vasopressin analogue that acts on V2 receptors of endothelial cells and releases preformed VWF from Weibel-Palade bodies. In type 1 disease, the VWF that patients make is normal in structure but reduced in amount, so releasing stores raises VWF and factor VIII 2- to 5-fold within an hour.<br> <b>Why a trial infusion is done first</b>: Response varies between patients, so guidelines recommend a test dose with measurement of VWF activity before relying on it for surgery.<br> <b>Why factor VIII concentrate alone is wrong</b>: The primary defect is platelet adhesion to collagen via VWF. Factor VIII without VWF is cleared quickly and does not correct platelet adhesion. When desmopressin fails or is contraindicated (type 3, some type 2), a plasma-derived VWF/factor VIII concentrate or recombinant VWF is used. Repeated doses of desmopressin cause tachyphylaxis and hyponatremia, so fluid intake is limited.
- **비교**: <table><thead><tr><th style="width:24%">Feature</th><th style="width:38%">Type 1 VWD → desmopressin (answer)</th><th>Hemophilia A → factor VIII concentrate (closest distractor)</th></tr></thead><tbody> <tr><td>Bleeding pattern</td><td><b>Mucocutaneous</b> (menorrhagia, epistaxis)</td><td>Deep tissue: hemarthrosis, muscle hematoma</td></tr> <tr><td>Inheritance</td><td>Autosomal dominant (mother affected)</td><td>X-linked (affected males)</td></tr> <tr><td>VWF antigen/activity</td><td><b>Both low, ratio ≈ 1</b></td><td>Normal</td></tr> <tr><td>Factor VIII</td><td>Mildly low (secondary)</td><td>Primarily low</td></tr> </tbody></table> Both prolong the aPTT. Low VWF with mucosal bleeding in a woman points to VWD; isolated factor VIII deficiency with joint bleeding in a man points to hemophilia A.
- **오답 이유**:
  - (B) Recombinant factor VIII concentrate would be the answer for a man with hemophilia A and normal VWF levels before surgery.
  - (C) Platelet transfusion would be chosen for severe thrombocytopenia or a platelet function defect such as Glanzmann thrombasthenia.
  - (D) Fresh frozen plasma would be used for multiple factor deficiencies, such as massive transfusion or liver failure, not for targeted VWD prophylaxis.
  - (E) Vitamin K would correct a prolonged PT from warfarin, malabsorption, or deficiency of factors II, VII, IX, and X.
- **함정**: Seeing a prolonged aPTT with low factor VIII and choosing factor VIII replacement — the primary defect is VWF, and her stores respond to desmopressin.
- **학습목표**: Diagnose type 1 von Willebrand disease from mucocutaneous bleeding with proportionally low VWF antigen and activity, and choose desmopressin for procedural prophylaxis after a documented response
