## 문제
A 19-year-old man comes to the physician because of swelling and pain of the right knee for 1 day after playing soccer; he does not recall a direct injury. He had prolonged bleeding after a tooth extraction at age 12 years and a large thigh hematoma after a fall at age 15 years. He takes no medications. His maternal uncle had similar joint problems. The right knee is warm, swollen, and tense, with a palpable effusion. There are no petechiae. Platelet count is 260,000/mm3, prothrombin time is 12 seconds (INR 1.0), activated partial thromboplastin time is 68 seconds (N 25-35), and bleeding time is normal. The prolonged aPTT corrects to 33 seconds after mixing with an equal volume of normal plasma. Which of the following is the most likely underlying cause of this patient's condition?

- A. Antiphospholipid antibodies (lupus anticoagulant)
- B. Deficiency of vitamin K-dependent clotting factors
- C. Deficiency of factor VIII
- D. Deficiency of von Willebrand factor
- E. Deficiency of factor VII

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: C

- **정답 핵심**: Spontaneous hemarthrosis and deep muscle hematoma are bleeding of the coagulation (secondary hemostasis) type. The isolated prolonged aPTT with normal PT points to the intrinsic pathway (factors VIII, IX, XI, XII), correction on mixing indicates a factor deficiency rather than an inhibitor, and the normal bleeding time with no petechiae argues against a platelet or von Willebrand defect. With an affected maternal uncle (X-linked inheritance), the most likely cause is factor VIII deficiency, hemophilia A.
- **원리**: <b>Why the bleeding pattern localizes the defect</b>: platelets and von Willebrand factor form the primary plug within seconds; failure causes immediate mucocutaneous bleeding — petechiae, gum and nose bleeding, menorrhagia. Coagulation factors make the fibrin that stabilizes the plug; when they are missing, a plug forms but falls apart, so bleeding starts late and goes deep, into joints and muscles. Hemarthrosis is therefore the signature of a coagulation-factor deficiency.<br> <b>Why the mixing study separates deficiency from inhibitor</b>: normal plasma supplies at least 50% of every factor, enough to normalize the clotting time. If the aPTT corrects, a factor was simply missing; if it stays prolonged, something in the patient's plasma (an anti-factor VIII antibody or lupus anticoagulant) blocks the normal factors too.<br> <b>Why factor VIII is the most likely</b>: hemophilia A is about five times more common than hemophilia B (factor IX); both are X-linked and appear in boys through maternal relatives. Specific factor assays confirm the diagnosis and grade severity (< 1% severe, 1-5% moderate, > 5-40% mild).
- **비교**: <table><thead><tr><th style="width:22%">Feature</th><th style="width:39%">Hemophilia A (correct)</th><th>von Willebrand disease (closest distractor)</th></tr></thead><tbody> <tr><td>Bleeding type</td><td><b>Deep</b> — joints, muscles, delayed bleeding</td><td><b>Mucocutaneous</b> — nose, gums, menorrhagia</td></tr> <tr><td>Inheritance</td><td>X-linked recessive — affected males, maternal relatives</td><td>Usually autosomal dominant — both sexes</td></tr> <tr><td>aPTT</td><td>Prolonged, corrects on mixing</td><td>Normal or mildly prolonged (low factor VIII)</td></tr> <tr><td>Bleeding time / PFA-100</td><td><b>Normal</b></td><td><b>Prolonged</b></td></tr> </tbody></table> von Willebrand factor carries factor VIII, so vWD can also prolong the aPTT; the normal bleeding time and joint bleeding point to a primary factor VIII deficiency.
- **오답 이유**:
  - (A) Lupus anticoagulant prolongs the aPTT but does not correct on mixing, and it causes thrombosis and pregnancy loss rather than joint bleeding.
  - (B) Vitamin K deficiency or warfarin lowers factors II, VII, IX, and X and prolongs the PT first; it fits a patient with malabsorption or anticoagulant use and an elevated INR.
  - (D) Von Willebrand factor deficiency is the answer when mucocutaneous bleeding predominates with a prolonged bleeding time or abnormal platelet function test, often in women and with autosomal dominant inheritance.
  - (E) Factor VII deficiency is suggested by an isolated prolonged PT with a normal aPTT, because factor VII acts only in the extrinsic pathway; here the PT is normal.
- **함정**: Choosing von Willebrand disease because it is the most common inherited bleeding disorder — joint bleeding, X-linked family history, and a normal bleeding time point to hemophilia.
- **학습목표**: 관절 출혈과 근육 출혈, 혼합 검사로 교정되는 단독 aPTT 연장, 정상 출혈 시간에서 내인 경로 인자(VIII) 결핍을 진단하고 폰빌레브란트병과 구분한다
