## 문제
A 34-year-old woman is brought to the emergency department by her husband because of 2 days of headache and episodes of confusion. She has had fatigue for 1 week and noticed small red spots on her legs. She has no diarrhea. She takes no medications. Her temperature is 37.4°C, blood pressure is 128/78 mm Hg, and pulse is 102/min. She is oriented to person only. Petechiae are present on the lower extremities. There is no lymphadenopathy or splenomegaly. Laboratory studies show a hemoglobin concentration of 7.9 g/dL, reticulocyte count of 6%, platelet count of 14,000/mm³, serum lactate dehydrogenase of 1,480 U/L, undetectable haptoglobin, indirect bilirubin of 2.6 mg/dL, and creatinine of 1.1 mg/dL. Prothrombin time, partial thromboplastin time, and fibrinogen are within the reference ranges. A direct antiglobulin test is negative. A peripheral blood smear shows numerous schistocytes. An ADAMTS13 activity assay has been sent. Which of the following is the most appropriate next step in management?

- A. Plasma exchange
- B. Eculizumab therapy
- C. Platelet transfusion
- D. Hemodialysis
- E. Intravenous immune globulin

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: A

- **정답 핵심**: She has microangiopathic hemolytic anemia (schistocytes, high LDH, undetectable haptoglobin, negative direct antiglobulin test), severe thrombocytopenia, and neurologic signs, with normal coagulation studies that exclude disseminated intravascular coagulation and only mild renal involvement. This picture, with a high PLASMIC score, is thrombotic thrombocytopenic purpura caused by severe ADAMTS13 deficiency, usually from an autoantibody. Untreated mortality is about 90 %, so plasma exchange with glucocorticoids is started immediately without waiting for the ADAMTS13 result; caplacizumab and rituximab are added in immune TTP.
- **원리**: <b>Why ADAMTS13 deficiency causes clots</b>: Endothelial cells release von Willebrand factor as ultralarge multimers. ADAMTS13 cleaves them into smaller forms. When an autoantibody blocks ADAMTS13 (activity < 10 %), ultralarge multimers bind platelets spontaneously under high shear, forming platelet-rich microthrombi in small vessels of the brain, heart, and kidneys. Platelets are consumed, and red cells shear across the thrombi to form schistocytes.<br> <b>Why plasma exchange works</b>: Exchange removes the autoantibody and ultralarge multimers and replaces ADAMTS13 with donor plasma. Glucocorticoids and rituximab suppress antibody production, and caplacizumab blocks the von Willebrand factor–platelet interaction.<br> <b>Why not wait</b>: Death can follow within days from cerebral or myocardial microthrombi. Clinical scores such as PLASMIC (platelets < 30,000, hemolysis, no active cancer, no transplant, MCV < 90, INR < 1.5, creatinine < 2.0) identify likely TTP before the assay returns.
- **비교**: <table><thead><tr><th style="width:24%">Feature</th><th style="width:38%">TTP — plasma exchange (answer)</th><th>Complement-mediated HUS — eculizumab (closest distractor)</th></tr></thead><tbody> <tr><td>Platelet count</td><td><b>Very low</b> (often < 30,000)</td><td>Moderately low</td></tr> <tr><td>Kidney</td><td>Mild creatinine rise</td><td><b>Severe acute kidney injury</b>, often dialysis</td></tr> <tr><td>Brain</td><td>Confusion, headache, focal signs common</td><td>Less common</td></tr> <tr><td>ADAMTS13 activity</td><td>< 10 %</td><td>> 10 %</td></tr> </tbody></table> Severe thrombocytopenia with neurologic signs and preserved kidney function points to TTP; severe kidney injury with a less profound platelet drop points to complement-mediated HUS.
- **오답 이유**:
  - (B) Eculizumab therapy would be appropriate for complement-mediated HUS with severe acute kidney injury and ADAMTS13 activity above 10 %.
  - (C) Platelet transfusion is avoided in TTP because it may fuel microthrombi; it is reserved for life-threatening bleeding or a required procedure.
  - (D) Hemodialysis would be indicated for severe acute kidney injury with volume overload or hyperkalemia, which her creatinine of 1.1 mg/dL does not show.
  - (E) Intravenous immune globulin would be first-line for immune thrombocytopenia with isolated thrombocytopenia and no schistocytes or hemolysis.
- **함정**: Transfusing platelets for a count of 14,000 or waiting for the ADAMTS13 result — suspected TTP is treated with plasma exchange at once.
- **학습목표**: Recognize thrombotic thrombocytopenic purpura from microangiopathic hemolytic anemia, severe thrombocytopenia, and neurologic signs with normal coagulation, and start plasma exchange without waiting for ADAMTS13 results
