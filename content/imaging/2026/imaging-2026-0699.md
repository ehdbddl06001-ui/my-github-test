## 문제
A 61-year-old woman comes to the physician because of three episodes of light vaginal spotting over the past month. Her last menstrual period was 9 years ago. She has hypertension and type 2 diabetes mellitus. She has never taken hormone therapy. Her BMI is 34 kg/m². Pelvic examination shows an atrophic vagina and a normal-appearing cervix with no bleeding at the time of examination; the uterus is normal in size. A cervical cytology screen 1 year ago was negative. Transvaginal ultrasonography shows an endometrial thickness of 9 mm and no adnexal masses. Which of the following is the most appropriate next step in management?

- A. Endometrial biopsy
- B. Topical vaginal estrogen and follow-up
- C. Repeat transvaginal ultrasonography in 6 months
- D. Total abdominal hysterectomy
- E. Serum CA-125 measurement

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: A

- **정답 핵심**: Any postmenopausal bleeding must be evaluated for endometrial carcinoma. Transvaginal ultrasonography showing an endometrial thickness above 4 mm does not exclude cancer, so tissue sampling is required; office endometrial biopsy is the next step. Her obesity and diabetes (unopposed estrogen from adipose aromatization, hyperinsulinemia) add to the risk.
- **원리**: <b>왜 폐경 후 출혈은 늘 조사하는가</b>: 폐경 후 출혈의 가장 흔한 원인은 위축성 내막·질염이지만, 자궁내막암 환자의 약 90 % 가 출혈로 처음 나타나고 폐경 후 출혈의 약 10 % 가 암이다. 일찍 출혈로 드러나기 때문에 바로 조직을 확인하면 대부분 초기에 잡을 수 있다.<br> <b>왜 4 mm 가 기준인가</b>: 에스트로겐이 없는 폐경 후 내막은 얇아(보통 4 mm 이하) 그 두께면 암의 음성 예측도가 99 % 이상이다. 4 mm 를 넘으면 증식증·용종·암을 영상으로 구별할 수 없으므로 <b>조직</b>으로 확인한다. 외래 흡인 생검으로 시작하고, 조직이 부족하거나 출혈이 계속되면 자궁경 검사와 소파술로 넘어간다.<br> <b>위험 인자</b>: 비만은 지방 조직의 방향화효소가 안드로겐을 에스트론으로 바꿔 프로게스테론 없이 내막을 자극하고, 당뇨·고인슐린혈증은 성장 인자로 작용한다 — 1형(자궁내막양) 암의 전형적 배경이다.
- **비교**: <table><thead><tr><th style="width:24%">Feature</th><th style="width:38%">Endometrial biopsy (answer)</th><th>Vaginal estrogen and follow-up (closest distractor)</th></tr></thead><tbody> <tr><td>Endometrial thickness</td><td><b>&gt; 4 mm</b> — cancer not excluded</td><td>≤ 4 mm — atrophy most likely</td></tr> <tr><td>Goal</td><td>Tissue diagnosis (hyperplasia, cancer)</td><td>Treat atrophic vaginitis/endometrium</td></tr> <tr><td>Risk factors</td><td>Obesity, diabetes, unopposed estrogen</td><td>Low-risk woman with thin stripe</td></tr> <tr><td>This patient</td><td>9 mm · BMI 34 · diabetes — fits</td><td>Thick stripe — does not fit despite atrophic vagina</td></tr> </tbody></table> An atrophic vagina explains spotting only after a thin endometrium has been confirmed.
- **오답 이유**:
  - (B) Topical vaginal estrogen would be correct if the endometrial thickness were 4 mm or less and the bleeding was attributed to atrophic vaginitis.
  - (C) Repeat ultrasonography in 6 months would be reasonable only for an incidentally thickened endometrium without any bleeding, not for postmenopausal bleeding.
  - (D) Total abdominal hysterectomy would be correct after biopsy confirms endometrial carcinoma or atypical hyperplasia, as part of surgical staging.
  - (E) Serum CA-125 would be useful if an adnexal mass suggested ovarian cancer, or to plan surgery once endometrial cancer is diagnosed; it is not a diagnostic test for bleeding.
- **함정**: Blaming the atrophic vagina for the spotting and skipping endometrial sampling despite a thick endometrial stripe.
- **학습목표**: 폐경 후 출혈에서 질식 초음파 자궁내막 두께가 4 mm 를 넘으면 자궁내막암을 배제하기 위해 자궁내막 조직검사를 한다
