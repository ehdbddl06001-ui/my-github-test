## 문제
A 74-year-old woman is brought to the emergency department because of fever, right upper quadrant pain, and confusion for 1 day. She has a history of gallstones. Temperature is 39.4°C, blood pressure is 84/50 mm Hg, pulse is 118/min, and respirations are 24/min. She is oriented only to person. The sclerae are icteric, and there is tenderness in the right upper quadrant without peritoneal signs. Laboratory studies show a leukocyte count of 19,800/mm³, total bilirubin of 5.6 mg/dL, alkaline phosphatase of 420 U/L, and lipase within the reference range. Ultrasonography shows a common bile duct of 12 mm with an echogenic focus in its distal portion; the gallbladder wall is not thickened. Intravenous fluids and piperacillin-tazobactam are started. Which of the following is the most appropriate next step in management?

- A. Magnetic resonance cholangiopancreatography
- B. Continue antibiotics and reassess in 48 hours
- C. Hepatobiliary iminodiacetic acid scan
- D. Urgent endoscopic retrograde cholangiopancreatography
- E. Laparoscopic cholecystectomy within 72 hours

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: D

- **정답 핵심**: Fever, jaundice, and right upper quadrant pain (Charcot triad) plus hypotension and confusion (Reynolds pentad) indicate severe acute cholangitis from an obstructing common bile duct stone already seen on ultrasonography. Antibiotics cannot sterilize an obstructed, pressurized biliary tree, so after resuscitation the next step is urgent biliary drainage, preferably by ERCP with sphincterotomy and stone extraction or stent placement.
- **원리**: <b>왜 배액이 핵심인가</b>: 담관이 막히면 담즙이 고이고 장내 세균이 올라와 증식한다. 담관 내압이 오르면(약 20 cmH2O 이상) 세균과 내독소가 간세포 사이의 연결을 지나 간정맥·림프로 넘어가 <b>균혈증·패혈성 쇼크</b>가 된다. 항생제는 이 압력과 막힘을 풀지 못하고, 고인 담즙에는 항생제가 잘 들어가지도 않는다. 그래서 쇼크·의식 변화가 있는 중증 담관염은 소생술과 함께 가능한 빨리(보통 24시간 안, 쇼크면 더 빨리) 막힌 곳을 뚫어 압력을 빼야 한다.<br> <b>왜 ERCP 인가</b>: 내시경으로 유두를 절개해 돌을 꺼내거나 스텐트를 넣으면 진단과 치료가 한 번에 된다. 경피 경간 담도 배액은 해부 구조 때문에 ERCP 가 불가능하거나 실패했을 때의 대안이다.<br> <b>담낭 절제는 원인 치료지만 나중이다</b>: 급성 담관염의 문제는 담관이지 담낭이 아니다. 환자가 안정된 뒤 같은 입원 중에 담낭절제술로 재발을 막는다.

- **오답 이유**:
  - (A) MRCP would be correct if the probability of a duct stone were intermediate and the patient stable, to decide whether ERCP is needed; here the stone is already visible.
  - (B) Continuing antibiotics and reassessing would be acceptable for mild cholangitis with stable vital signs and normal mentation, followed by elective ERCP.
  - (C) A HIDA scan would be correct to confirm acute cholecystitis when ultrasonography is equivocal, not to treat an obstructed common bile duct.
  - (E) Laparoscopic cholecystectomy within 72 hours would be correct for acute calculous cholecystitis with a thickened gallbladder wall and no ductal stone or jaundice.
- **함정**: Treating septic shock from cholangitis with antibiotics alone and waiting — source control by drainage is what reverses it.
- **학습목표**: 저혈압·의식 변화를 동반한 급성 담관염(Reynolds 5징후)은 수액·항생제와 함께 내시경 담도 배액을 서둘러야 한다
