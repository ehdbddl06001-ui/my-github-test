## 문제
A 68-year-old man comes to the emergency department because of diffuse muscle aching and weakness for 3 days and dark urine since yesterday. Ten days ago he began a course of clarithromycin for community-acquired pneumonia. He has taken simvastatin 40 mg daily for 5 years without problems, as well as lisinopril. He has not exercised strenuously. Examination shows tender proximal thigh and arm muscles with 4/5 strength. Serum creatine kinase is 18,500 U/L and creatinine is 1.9 mg/dL (baseline 1.0). Urine dipstick is positive for blood, but microscopy shows no erythrocytes. Which of the following best explains the mechanism of this drug interaction?

- A. Inhibition of CYP2C9-mediated simvastatin metabolism
- B. Inhibition of hepatic OATP1B1 uptake of simvastatin
- C. Induction of P-glycoprotein in skeletal muscle
- D. Displacement of simvastatin from plasma albumin
- E. Inhibition of CYP3A4-mediated simvastatin metabolism

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: E

- **정답 핵심**: Statin myopathy progressing to rhabdomyolysis (CK over 10 times normal, myoglobinuria with heme-positive dipstick but no red cells, acute kidney injury) began after clarithromycin was added to long-standing simvastatin. Simvastatin is extensively metabolized by CYP3A4 in the gut wall and liver; clarithromycin is a strong mechanism-based CYP3A4 inhibitor, which raises simvastatin and simvastatin acid exposure several-fold and increases muscle toxicity. Simvastatin should be withheld during macrolide therapy, or azithromycin chosen instead.
- **원리**: <b>Why simvastatin is so sensitive to CYP3A4</b>: simvastatin is a lipophilic lactone prodrug with very high first-pass metabolism by CYP3A4 in the intestinal wall and liver — normally only about 5% of an oral dose reaches the circulation. Blocking CYP3A4 removes most of this first-pass loss, so plasma levels of simvastatin and its active acid can increase 10-fold. Atorvastatin and lovastatin share this pathway; pravastatin and rosuvastatin are largely not CYP3A4 substrates.<br> <b>Why muscle is injured</b>: higher statin concentrations in muscle inhibit HMG-CoA reductase there, reducing mevalonate products such as coenzyme Q10 and prenylated proteins, which impairs mitochondrial function and membrane integrity; myocytes break down and release CK and myoglobin.<br> <b>Why the urine is heme-positive without red cells</b>: the dipstick detects the heme in myoglobin, which is filtered freely and is toxic to tubules — the cause of the acute kidney injury.
- **비교**: <table><thead><tr><th style="width:24%">Feature</th><th style="width:38%">CYP3A4 inhibition (answer)</th><th>OATP1B1 inhibition (closest distractor)</th></tr></thead><tbody> <tr><td>Typical perpetrators</td><td><b>Clarithromycin, erythromycin, azole antifungals, HIV protease inhibitors, grapefruit juice</b></td><td>Gemfibrozil, cyclosporine, rifampin (single dose)</td></tr> <tr><td>Step blocked</td><td>Metabolic clearance in gut wall and liver</td><td>Hepatic uptake of the statin from blood</td></tr> <tr><td>Statins affected</td><td>Simvastatin, lovastatin, atorvastatin</td><td>All statins, including rosuvastatin and pravastatin</td></tr> </tbody></table> Name the added drug: a macrolide or azole means CYP3A4; a fibrate (gemfibrozil) or cyclosporine adds transporter inhibition.
- **오답 이유**:
  - (A) CYP2C9 inhibition would explain toxicity with fluvastatin, which is metabolized by CYP2C9, when combined with a CYP2C9 inhibitor such as fluconazole or amiodarone.
  - (B) OATP1B1 inhibition would be the main mechanism if the added drug were gemfibrozil or cyclosporine, which block hepatic statin uptake and affect all statins.
  - (C) P-glycoprotein induction would lower, not raise, statin exposure; induction by rifampin or St. John's wort reduces the statin effect rather than causing myopathy.
  - (D) Albumin displacement would matter only for highly bound drugs with narrow margins and causes a transient change; it would not explain sustained statin toxicity.
- **함정**: Choosing OATP1B1 inhibition because it is a well-known statin interaction — that mechanism belongs to gemfibrozil and cyclosporine, not macrolides.
- **학습목표**: 심바스타틴 복용 중 클래리스로마이신을 더한 뒤 생긴 근육병증·횡문근융해의 기전을 CYP3A4 억제에 의한 스타틴 노출 증가로 설명하고, OATP1B1 억제(겜피브로질·사이클로스포린)와 가른다
