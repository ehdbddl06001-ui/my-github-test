## 문제
A 58-year-old woman who was hospitalized for a diabetic foot infection develops agitation, sweating, tremor, and diarrhea. Cultures from the wound grew vancomycin-resistant Enterococcus faecium, and she was started on a new intravenous antibiotic 2 days ago. She has major depressive disorder treated with sertraline for 3 years and takes metformin and atorvastatin. Her temperature is 39.1°C (102.4°F), pulse is 124/min, and blood pressure is 168/94 mm Hg. Pupils are dilated. Deep tendon reflexes are 4+ in the lower extremities with sustained ankle clonus, and there is no muscle rigidity in the upper extremities. Bowel sounds are hyperactive. Serum creatine kinase is 410 U/L. Which of the following mechanisms best explains this reaction?

- A. Blockade of dopamine D2 receptors
- B. Blockade of muscarinic acetylcholine receptors
- C. Abnormal activation of ryanodine receptors in skeletal muscle
- D. Inhibition of monoamine oxidase
- E. Inhibition of cytochrome P450 2D6

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: D

- **정답 핵심**: The antibiotic used for vancomycin-resistant Enterococcus faecium is linezolid. Within 2 days of adding it to sertraline, she has agitation, hyperthermia, tachycardia, hypertension, mydriasis, diarrhea, hyperreflexia, and sustained clonus most marked in the legs, which meets the Hunter criteria for serotonin syndrome. Linezolid is a weak, reversible, nonselective inhibitor of monoamine oxidase, so serotonin released by the SSRI-treated neurons is not degraded and accumulates in the synapse.
- **원리**: <b>Why an antibiotic acts on neurotransmitters</b>: linezolid is an oxazolidinone that inhibits bacterial protein synthesis at the 50S ribosome, but its chemical structure also resembles MAO inhibitors, and it reversibly inhibits both MAO-A and MAO-B. MAO-A is the main enzyme that breaks down serotonin in presynaptic neurons.<br> <b>Why the combination is dangerous</b>: an SSRI blocks serotonin reuptake, raising synaptic serotonin; MAO inhibition removes the main degradation pathway for the serotonin that is taken back up. Together they over-stimulate 5-HT2A and 5-HT1A receptors, producing the triad of altered mental status, autonomic hyperactivity, and neuromuscular hyperexcitability.<br> <b>Why clonus points to serotonin</b>: the neuromuscular signs of serotonin syndrome are hyperreflexia and inducible or spontaneous clonus, greater in the lower limbs, and they appear within 24 hours. This distinguishes it from neuroleptic malignant syndrome, which develops over days with lead-pipe rigidity and bradyreflexia.
- **비교**: <table><thead><tr><th style="width:22%">Feature</th><th style="width:39%">MAO inhibition → serotonin syndrome (answer)</th><th>CYP2D6 inhibition (closest distractor)</th></tr></thead><tbody> <tr><td>Drug</td><td>Linezolid (also methylene blue)</td><td>Fluoxetine, paroxetine, bupropion, quinidine</td></tr> <tr><td>What changes</td><td>Pharmacodynamic: serotonin breakdown in the synapse</td><td>Pharmacokinetic: plasma level of a substrate drug</td></tr> <tr><td>Onset with sertraline</td><td><b>Within 1 to 2 days</b> of adding the drug</td><td>Gradual as levels accumulate</td></tr> <tr><td>Linezolid's role</td><td>Nonselective reversible MAO inhibitor</td><td>Linezolid is not a CYP substrate or inhibitor</td></tr> </tbody></table> Linezolid is cleared mainly by nonenzymatic oxidation, so its interactions are pharmacodynamic; the same reaction occurs with methylene blue, another MAO inhibitor.
- **오답 이유**:
  - (A) Dopamine D2 blockade causes neuroleptic malignant syndrome, which would be correct with an antipsychotic, slow onset over days, lead-pipe rigidity, and decreased reflexes.
  - (B) Muscarinic blockade would be correct with dry, flushed skin, urinary retention, and decreased bowel sounds, whereas she is sweating with hyperactive bowel sounds.
  - (C) Ryanodine receptor activation causes malignant hyperthermia, which would be correct during volatile anesthesia or succinylcholine with rigidity and a rising end-tidal CO2.
  - (E) CYP2D6 inhibition would be correct if the new drug were fluoxetine, paroxetine, or bupropion raising levels of a substrate; linezolid does not use the CYP system.
- **함정**: Attributing the reaction to a pharmacokinetic interaction that raises sertraline levels — linezolid is not a CYP inhibitor; it inhibits MAO.
- **학습목표**: SSRI 복용자에게 리네졸리드를 추가한 뒤 생긴 클로누스·과다반사·고열·자율신경 항진을 세로토닌 증후군으로 판단하고, 리네졸리드의 비선택적 MAO 억제가 기전임을 안다
