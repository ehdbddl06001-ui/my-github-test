## 문제
A 34-year-old man with HIV infection who has not taken antiretroviral therapy comes to the emergency department because of a headache that has gradually worsened over 2 weeks. He has had low-grade fevers and nausea. His temperature is 38.1°C, blood pressure is 124/76 mm Hg, and pulse is 92/min. He is oriented, with mild neck stiffness and no focal neurologic deficits. CD4+ T-lymphocyte count is 38/mm3. CT of the head shows no mass lesion. Lumbar puncture shows an opening pressure of 32 cm H2O; cerebrospinal fluid analysis shows a leukocyte count of 18/mm3 (90% lymphocytes), glucose concentration of 32 mg/dL, and a positive cryptococcal antigen test. Which of the following is the most appropriate initial pharmacotherapy?

- A. Fluconazole alone
- B. Immediate initiation of antiretroviral therapy
- C. Ceftriaxone, vancomycin, and ampicillin
- D. Dexamethasone and fluconazole
- E. Amphotericin B and flucytosine

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: E

- **정답 핵심**: A subacute headache with fever, a CD4+ count below 100/mm3, a high opening pressure, lymphocytic cerebrospinal fluid with low glucose, and a positive cryptococcal antigen establish cryptococcal meningitis. Induction therapy with an amphotericin B formulation plus flucytosine sterilizes the cerebrospinal fluid fastest and lowers mortality, followed by fluconazole consolidation and maintenance. The high opening pressure also requires therapeutic lumbar drainage, and antiretroviral therapy is deferred for 4 to 6 weeks.
- **원리**: <b>Why combination induction</b>: Cryptococcus neoformans in cerebrospinal fluid reaches very high burdens in advanced HIV. Amphotericin B binds ergosterol and kills the yeast; flucytosine is converted inside fungal cells to 5-fluorouracil and blocks nucleic acid synthesis. Together they clear the organism faster than either alone, and the speed of clearance predicts survival. Fluconazole is fungistatic and clears the fluid slowly, so it is used only after induction or when the combination is unavailable.<br> <b>Why antiretroviral therapy waits</b>: Restoring CD4 cells early unmasks a strong inflammatory response against cryptococcal antigens in a closed space, the immune reconstitution inflammatory syndrome. Starting antiretroviral therapy within the first weeks increased mortality in a randomized trial, so it is started 4 to 6 weeks after antifungal therapy begins.<br> <b>Why pressure matters</b>: Polysaccharide capsule blocks the arachnoid villi and raises intracranial pressure; repeated lumbar drainage to below 20 cm H2O reduces death and blindness, whereas corticosteroids increased harm.
- **비교**: <table><thead><tr><th style="width:24%">Feature</th><th style="width:38%">Amphotericin B and flucytosine (answer)</th><th>Fluconazole alone (closest distractor)</th></tr></thead><tbody> <tr><td>Action</td><td>Fungicidal combination</td><td>Fungistatic</td></tr> <tr><td>CSF clearance</td><td><b>Fastest</b></td><td>Slow</td></tr> <tr><td>Phase</td><td>Induction (1–2 weeks)</td><td>Consolidation and maintenance; induction only if no alternative</td></tr> <tr><td>Mortality</td><td>Lowest</td><td>Higher as induction monotherapy</td></tr> </tbody></table> Both drugs belong in the treatment course, but in different phases: the fungicidal combination first, fluconazole afterward.
- **오답 이유**:
  - (A) Fluconazole alone would be used for consolidation after induction, or as induction only where amphotericin B and flucytosine are unavailable.
  - (B) Immediate antiretroviral therapy would be correct for most opportunistic infections, but in cryptococcal meningitis it is deferred 4 to 6 weeks because early start increased mortality.
  - (C) Ceftriaxone, vancomycin, and ampicillin would be the empiric regimen for acute bacterial meningitis with neutrophilic cerebrospinal fluid before the cryptococcal antigen result.
  - (D) Dexamethasone and fluconazole would be wrong because adjunctive corticosteroids increased disability and adverse events in HIV-associated cryptococcal meningitis.
- **함정**: Starting antiretroviral therapy right away because the CD4+ count is very low — in cryptococcal meningitis early antiretroviral therapy increases mortality.
- **학습목표**: CD4 가 낮은 HIV 감염인의 크립토콕쿠스 수막염에서 암포테리신 B 와 플루시토신 유도요법을 고르고 플루코나졸 단독·즉시 항레트로바이러스 치료와 가른다
