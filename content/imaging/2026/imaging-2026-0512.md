## 문제
A 34-year-old man is brought to the emergency department because of confusion and fever for 1 day. He has schizophrenia, and 4 days ago his haloperidol dose was increased because of worsening agitation. He takes no other medications and does not use illicit drugs. Temperature is 40.1°C (104.2°F), pulse is 122/min, respirations are 24/min, and blood pressure is 168/98 mm Hg. He is diaphoretic and disoriented. There is generalized lead-pipe rigidity of the extremities. Deep tendon reflexes are 1+ and symmetric, and there is no clonus. Pupils are 4 mm and reactive to light. Bowel sounds are normal. The neck is stiff in all directions of movement. Serum creatine kinase is 18,400 U/L, and leukocyte count is 14,200/mm3. Which of the following is the most likely diagnosis?

- A. Malignant hyperthermia
- B. Anticholinergic toxicity
- C. Bacterial meningitis
- D. Neuroleptic malignant syndrome
- E. Serotonin syndrome

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: D

- **정답 핵심**: Days after a haloperidol dose increase, he has the tetrad of neuroleptic malignant syndrome: hyperthermia, generalized lead-pipe rigidity, altered mental status, and autonomic instability (tachycardia, hypertension, diaphoresis), with a markedly elevated creatine kinase from muscle breakdown and leukocytosis. Reflexes are diminished and there is no clonus, which argues against serotonin syndrome. Normal pupils and bowel sounds with sweating argue against anticholinergic toxicity, and the neck stiffness is part of generalized rigidity rather than isolated meningismus.
- **원리**: <b>Why dopamine blockade causes this syndrome</b>: D2 receptor blockade in the nigrostriatal pathway produces intense muscle rigidity, and blockade in the hypothalamus disturbs temperature set-point and autonomic control. Sustained contraction of rigid muscle generates heat and breaks down muscle, so temperature and creatine kinase rise together. It typically develops over days, often after starting or rapidly increasing a high-potency antipsychotic.<br> <b>Why reflexes separate it from serotonin syndrome</b>: serotonin excess stimulates 5-HT2A receptors in the brainstem and spinal cord, increasing neuromuscular excitability — hyperreflexia, inducible or spontaneous clonus (especially in the legs), tremor, mydriasis, and diarrhea, usually within 24 hours of a serotonergic drug. NMS gives a "quiet," slowly evolving rigidity with normal or decreased reflexes.<br> <b>Why it matters</b>: treatment differs — NMS needs the antipsychotic stopped, cooling, and sometimes dantrolene or bromocriptine; serotonin syndrome needs serotonergic drugs stopped and cyproheptadine.
- **비교**: <table><thead><tr><th style="width:22%">Feature</th><th style="width:39%">Neuroleptic malignant syndrome (answer)</th><th>Serotonin syndrome (closest distractor)</th></tr></thead><tbody> <tr><td>Drug</td><td>Dopamine antagonist (haloperidol)</td><td>SSRI, MAOI, linezolid, tramadol …</td></tr> <tr><td>Onset</td><td>Days</td><td>Hours (usually &lt; 24 h)</td></tr> <tr><td>Neuromuscular</td><td><b>Lead-pipe rigidity, reflexes normal or decreased</b></td><td><b>Hyperreflexia, clonus</b>, tremor</td></tr> <tr><td>Other</td><td>Very high CK, normal pupils</td><td>Mydriasis, diarrhea, hyperactive bowel sounds</td></tr> </tbody></table> Both cause fever and autonomic instability — tap the reflexes and look for clonus to tell them apart.
- **오답 이유**:
  - (A) Malignant hyperthermia would be correct if rigidity and rising temperature began during general anesthesia with a volatile agent or succinylcholine.
  - (B) Anticholinergic toxicity would be correct if he had hot, dry skin, dilated pupils, absent bowel sounds, and urinary retention without lead-pipe rigidity.
  - (C) Bacterial meningitis would be correct if neck stiffness were isolated with headache and photophobia, and CSF showed neutrophilic pleocytosis, without generalized rigidity.
  - (E) Serotonin syndrome would be correct if he had started a serotonergic drug within the last day and showed hyperreflexia, ankle clonus, mydriasis, and diarrhea.
- **함정**: Choosing serotonin syndrome because of fever and agitation — the deciding signs are the reflexes and clonus, which are absent here.
- **학습목표**: 도파민 길항제 증량 뒤 고열·납관 강직·의식 변화·CK 상승·반사 저하가 오면 신경이완제 악성 증후군으로 진단하고 반사 항진·간대성 경련이 특징인 세로토닌 증후군과 가른다
