## 문제
A 39-year-old woman comes to the physician because of fatigue and muscle weakness for 3 months and an episode of left flank pain with hematuria 2 weeks ago. She has had dry eyes and dry mouth for 2 years. She has no diarrhea and takes no medications. Blood pressure is 112/70 mm Hg. Examination shows bilateral parotid enlargement and mild proximal muscle weakness. Serum studies show: Na+ 139 mEq/L, K+ 2.9 mEq/L, Cl− 115 mEq/L, HCO3− 15 mEq/L, glucose 90 mg/dL, and creatinine 0.8 mg/dL. Arterial pH is 7.30. Urine pH is 6.8; urine Na+ 40 mEq/L, K+ 30 mEq/L, Cl− 50 mEq/L. Urinalysis shows no glucose and no protein. Renal ultrasonography shows bilateral medullary nephrocalcinosis. Which of the following is the most likely cause of the acid-base disorder?

- A. Impaired HCO3− reabsorption in the proximal tubule
- B. Aldosterone deficiency or resistance in the collecting duct
- C. Gastrointestinal loss of bicarbonate
- D. Accumulation of unmeasured organic acids
- E. Impaired H+ secretion by α-intercalated cells of the collecting duct

## 정답 및 해설
> 정답: E

- **정답 핵심**: Anion gap is 139 − (115 + 15) = 9 — a normal anion gap (hyperchloremic) metabolic acidosis. The urine anion gap is positive (40 + 30 − 50 = +20), so the kidney is not excreting NH4+, and urine pH stays 6.8 despite systemic acidosis. With hypokalemia, nephrocalcinosis, and sicca symptoms with parotid enlargement (Sjögren syndrome), this is distal (type 1) renal tubular acidosis — failure of α-intercalated cells to secrete H+.
- **원리**: <b>Two ways to lose base without an anion gap</b>: bicarbonate leaves through the gut (diarrhea) or the kidney fails to reclaim or regenerate it (renal tubular acidosis). The urine anion gap (Na+ + K+ − Cl−) estimates urinary NH4+: a healthy kidney facing acidosis excretes large amounts of NH4Cl, making the gap negative; a positive gap means the kidney is part of the problem.<br> <b>Why distal RTA keeps urine alkaline</b>: α-intercalated cells pump H+ into the collecting duct lumen via H+-ATPase. In Sjögren syndrome, autoantibodies against this pump (or carbonic anhydrase II) disable it, so urine cannot be acidified below about 5.5 — even with serum HCO3− of 15.<br> <b>Why stones and low K+</b>: chronic acidosis releases calcium phosphate from bone and increases proximal citrate reabsorption; alkaline urine with low citrate precipitates calcium phosphate, causing nephrocalcinosis. Sodium delivered distally is reabsorbed in exchange for K+ instead of H+, wasting potassium.
- **비교**: <table><thead><tr><th style="width:22%">Feature</th><th style="width:39%">Distal (type 1) RTA (answer)</th><th>Proximal (type 2) RTA (closest distractor)</th></tr></thead><tbody> <tr><td>Defect</td><td><b>H+ secretion in collecting duct</b></td><td>HCO3− reabsorption in proximal tubule</td></tr> <tr><td>Urine pH during acidosis</td><td>Always &gt; 5.5</td><td>&lt; 5.5 once serum HCO3− falls below the lowered threshold</td></tr> <tr><td>Serum HCO3−</td><td>Often &lt; 10–15</td><td>Usually 12–20</td></tr> <tr><td>Stones</td><td>Nephrocalcinosis common</td><td>Rare</td></tr> <tr><td>Other clues</td><td>Sjögren, amphotericin, lithium</td><td>Fanconi (glycosuria, phosphaturia), multiple myeloma, tenofovir</td></tr> </tbody></table> Both cause hypokalemic, normal-gap acidosis — urine pH during acidosis and nephrocalcinosis separate them.
- **오답 이유**:
  - (A) Impaired proximal HCO3− reabsorption is correct when urine pH falls below 5.5 at steady state and Fanconi features (glycosuria with normal serum glucose, phosphaturia) appear.
  - (B) Aldosterone deficiency or resistance (type 4 RTA) is correct when the normal-gap acidosis comes with hyperkalemia, as in diabetic nephropathy; this patient's K+ is low.
  - (C) Gastrointestinal HCO3− loss is correct when diarrhea produces a normal-gap acidosis with a negative urine anion gap; she has no diarrhea and the urine gap is positive.
  - (D) Unmeasured organic acids (ketoacids, lactate, toxic alcohols) are correct when the anion gap is elevated; here it is 9.
- **함정**: Choosing proximal RTA because of hypokalemia — both types waste potassium; an alkaline urine during acidosis and nephrocalcinosis mark the distal defect.
- **학습목표**: 정상 음이온차 대사산증에서 양의 소변 음이온차·소변 pH > 5.5·저칼륨혈증·신석회화로 원위(1형) 신세관산증을 진단하고, 설사와 근위(2형) 신세관산증과 구별한다
