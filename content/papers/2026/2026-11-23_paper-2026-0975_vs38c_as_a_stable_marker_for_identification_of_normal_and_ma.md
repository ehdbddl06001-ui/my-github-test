## Title
VS38c as a stable marker for identification of normal and malignant plasma cells

## Authors
Kleibergen Thyra, Kühnau Jesper, van Duin Mark, Kamstrup Andreas, van der Velden Vincent H J

## Journal / DOI
American journal of clinical pathology · DOI: 10.1093/ajcp/aqag134 · PMID: 43068470
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43068470/

## Abstract
**OBJECTIVES:** Flow cytometric minimal residual disease analysis in patients with multiple myeloma (MM) often relies on CD38 and CD138 gating, but CD38 may be lost by targeted therapies, and CD138 expression rapidly decreases after sampling. We aimed to evaluate the stability of the alternative plasma cell marker VS38c.

**METHODS:** Bone marrow samples of patients with (suspected) MM were obtained in heparin or EDTA tubes. We evaluated CD38, CD138, and VS38c expression by flow cytometry from days 0 to 4. Expression levels were evaluated relative to day 0.

**RESULTS:** CD38 expression on normal and malignant plasma cells remained stable, whereas CD138 expression rapidly decreased after sampling (down to <10% at day 4, more prominent in heparin samples). VS38c expression remained stable and even slightly increased, independent of the type of anticoagulant used. In patients treated with daratumumab, CD138 expression was rapidly lost, whereas VS38c expression remained stable.

**CONCLUSIONS:** VS38c may be used as an alternative or additional marker to identify MM cells, including in samples with extended intervals between collection and processing.
