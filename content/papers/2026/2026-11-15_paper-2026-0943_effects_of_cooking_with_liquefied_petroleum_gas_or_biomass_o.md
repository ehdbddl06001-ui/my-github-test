## Title
Effects of Cooking With Liquefied Petroleum Gas or Biomass on Lung Function Among Pre-School Aged Children in Peru: A Prospective Follow-Up of a Randomized Trial

## Authors
Nicolaou Laura, Checkley William, Rha Jennifer, McCollum Eric D

## Journal / DOI
Pediatric pulmonology · DOI: 10.1002/ppul.71901 · PMID: 43033191
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43033191/

## Abstract
**INTRODUCTION:** Billions worldwide are exposed to household air pollution (HAP) from solid fuel use, increasing risk of lung function impairment and chronic respiratory disease. Understanding HAP effects on early life lung function may identify critical windows for intervention.

**METHODS:** We performed respiratory oscillometry in 3-5-year-olds whose mothers participated in a randomized trial of LPG versus usual solid fuel use during pregnancy and infancy. We assessed resistance (R7) and reactance (X7) at 7 Hertz, frequency dependence of resistance (R7-19), resonance frequency (fres) and area above the reactance curve from 7 Hertz (AX7). We computed z-scores for R7, X7, fres, and AX7 from pediatric reference equations. Rigorous quality control was applied. Exposure-response analyses examined the association between R7 or AX7 z-scores and prenatal or postnatal PM2.5 or CO exposures.

**RESULTS:** We tested 101 children (mean [±SD] age 4.4 ± 0.6 years, 51.5% male, 50.5% intervention). Mean R7, X7, fres and AX7 z-scores were similar between intervention and control groups: -0.1 ± 1.0 vs -0.2 ± 1.2, -0.8 ± 1.0 vs -1.0 ± 1.1, 0.9 ± 1.1 vs 0.4 ± 1.1, and 1.2 ± 1.0 vs 1.3 ± 1.3, respectively. Intention-to-treat analysis showed no differences between arms in pre-bronchodilator lung function or bronchodilator response, except for a larger post-bronchodilator decrease in mean fres z-score in the intervention arm (-0.36, 95% CI -0.71 to -0.02). Postnatal CO exposure was associated with higher AX7 z-scores, indicating worse reactance (0.041 per 1 ppm CO increase, 95% CI 0.003 to 0.078). No associations were found for PM2.5 exposure.

**CONCLUSION:** Early-life LPG use did not improve oscillometric lung function compared with those exposed to biomass smoke. An exploratory association between postnatal CO exposure and impaired lung reactance warrants confirmation in larger cohorts.
