## Title
Recurrence After Robotic eTEP Incisional Hernia Repair Emerges Beyond 24 Months: A Consecutive Series Challenging Short-Term Outcome Reporting

## Authors
Da Canal Francesca, Facchini Tullio A V, Di Pietro Martinelli Claudine, Gantert Walter, Peros Georgios, Lazaridis Ioannis I, Delko Tarik

## Journal / DOI
Surgical laparoscopy, endoscopy & percutaneous techniques · DOI: 10.1097/SLE.0000000000001527 · PMID: 43044050
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43044050/

## Abstract
**BACKGROUND:** Most published robotic enhanced-view totally extraperitoneal (eTEP) hernia repair series report follow-up under 12 months and recurrence rates of 1% to 3%.

**METHODS:** We retrospectively analyzed 65 consecutive patients undergoing robotic eTEP incisional hernia repair at a single center (2019 to 2025; Da Vinci Xi system); recurrence-free survival was estimated by the Kaplan-Meier method.

**RESULTS:** Median follow-up was 27.9 months (range: 11.7-76.2). The overall recurrence rate was 4/65 (6.2%); all 4 recurrences occurred in patients with follow-up ≥24 months (4/41, 9.8%), with none before 24 months. Kaplan-Meier recurrence-free survival was 97.1% (95% CI: 91.8-100%) at 36 and 48 months. Primary fascial closure was achieved in 100% of cases, and the short-term complication rate was 9.2% (Clavien-Dindo grade I-II only).

**CONCLUSIONS:** Because all recurrences emerged beyond 24 months, follow-up periods shorter than 24 months may fail to detect late clinical recurrences after eTEP repair.
