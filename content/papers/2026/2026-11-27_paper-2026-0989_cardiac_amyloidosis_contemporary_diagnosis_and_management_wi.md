## Title
Cardiac amyloidosis: contemporary diagnosis and management with illustrative cases of ATTR and AL subtypes

## Authors
Das Bibhuti B, Ashwath Ravi, Ashwath Mahi L

## Journal / DOI
Expert review of cardiovascular therapy · DOI: 10.1080/14779072.2026.2771661 · PMID: 43097435
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43097435/

## Abstract
**INTRODUCTION:** Cardiac amyloidosis (CA) is an increasingly recognized, treatable cause of heart failure. Prompt distinction between transthyretin amyloid cardiomyopathy (ATTR-CM) and light-chain cardiac amyloidosis (AL-CA) is critical because delayed AL diagnosis can be fatal.

**AREAS COVERED:** This narrative review examines epidemiology, mechanisms, multimodality imaging, diagnostic algorithms, and established and emerging therapies. Literature was identified through PubMed/MEDLINE, Embase, and Cochrane Library searches and reference screening; the original search dates require author confirmation. Emphasis is placed on immediate serum free light-chain and serum/urine immunofixation testing, nonbiopsy ATTR diagnosis in eligible patients, and TTR sequencing after ATTR confirmation. Three clinical cases illustrate diagnostic and therapeutic challenges.

**EXPERT OPINION:** Earlier diagnosis, urgent exclusion of AL-CA, universal TTR genetic testing, and multisystem evaluation of hereditary ATTR are essential. Multidisciplinary, phenotype-guided care and validated treatment-response measures will be increasingly important as therapies expand.
