## Title
Innovative roadmaps for sustainable antimicrobial resistance interventions

## Authors
Gasperini Gianmarco, Paccani Silvia Rossi, O'Dwyer Karen, Aanensen David M, Armstrong-James Darius, Scorza Francesco Berlanda, Bloom David E, Burns Christopher J, et al.

## Abstract
Integrated strategies to combat antibiotic-resistant bacteria and fungi require innovative commercial models, new discovery platforms, standardized genomic surveillance, and streamlined clinical trials. Machine learning and other new technologies will accelerate the development of diagnostics and therapeutics (small molecules, antibodies, and vaccines) to combat antimicrobial resistance. Next-generation approaches should align societal impact with investment to ensure rapid global public health gains.
