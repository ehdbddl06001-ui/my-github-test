## Title
Biomarkers of subclinical cholestasis predict cancer immunotherapy outcomes

## Authors
Mallard de La Varende Anne-Laure, Fidelle Marine, Kroemer Guido, Zitvogel Laurence

## Journal / DOI
Oncoimmunology · DOI: 10.1080/2162402X.2026.2748140 · PMID: 42878219
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42878219/

## Abstract
In a recent paper published in Cancer Cell, we identified subclinical cholestasis as an accessible imprint of gut dysbiosis predicting resistance to immune checkpoint blockade. Our work connects γ-glutamyl transferase, bile-acid remodeling, and MAdCAM-1 loss to compromised antitumor immunity, opening avenues for novel biomarker-guided microbiota interventions.
