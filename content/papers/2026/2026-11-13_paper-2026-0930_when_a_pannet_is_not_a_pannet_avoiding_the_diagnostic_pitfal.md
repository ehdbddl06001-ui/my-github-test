## Title
When a PanNET Is Not a PanNET: Avoiding the Diagnostic Pitfalls of Peripancreatic Paraganglioma-A Case-Based Review

## Authors
Coffin Mackenzie L, McCrea Grace E, Songtanin Busara, Das Kanak, Ullah Asad, Mouw Tyler J

## Journal / DOI
The American surgeon · DOI: 10.1177/00031348261508229 · PMID: 43019451
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43019451/

## Abstract
Paragangliomas are rare extra-adrenal neuroendocrine tumors, with pancreas involvement being extremely uncommon. Their clinical and radiologic similarity with other more common pancreatic neoplasms can cause diagnostic uncertainty. We present a 70-year-old man who presented with acute cholangitis, and a 4.4 cm pancreatic tail mass was found incidentally. Biopsy suggested a well-differentiated neuroendocrine tumor. Despite imaging and biopsy, the diagnosis was made postoperatively with histopathology revealing a paraganglioma in the peripancreatic fat. This case and brief review highlights the limitations of current diagnostic modalities and the need for a comprehensive evaluation in distinguishing paragangliomas from pancreatic neuroendocrine tumors, which is essential for surgery, genetic counseling, and long-term management.
