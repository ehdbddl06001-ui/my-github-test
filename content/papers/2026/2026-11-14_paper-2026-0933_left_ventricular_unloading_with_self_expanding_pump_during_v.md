## Title
Left Ventricular Unloading With Self-Expanding Pump During Venoarterial Extracorporeal Membrane Oxygenation in Acute Myocardial Infarction

## Authors
Cai Weiting, Ma Juan, Bai Nan, Xi Qianlan, Wang I-Wen, Bai Ming, Lu Andong

## Journal / DOI
ASAIO journal (American Society for Artificial Internal Organs : 1992) · DOI: 10.1097/MAT.0000000000002920 · PMID: 43024931
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43024931/

## Abstract
Increased left ventricular (LV) afterload and distension during venoarterial extracorporeal membrane oxygenation (VA-ECMO) support may impede myocardial perfusion in acute myocardial infarction (AMI). Active LV unloading has been associated with improved outcomes. We report the first clinical use of a self-expanding percutaneous ventricular assist device (NyokAssist; magAssist, Suzhou, China) for LV unloading during VA-ECMO in a 56 year old man with AMI-related cardiogenic shock (CS). Successful coronary angiography and percutaneous coronary intervention (PCI) of the culprit left circumflex (LCx) lesion were performed with NyokAssist + VA-ECMO support. The patient was successfully weaned from VA-ECMO on post-procedure day 5 and remained on NyokAssist support alone for an additional 7 days before being transferred on VA-ECMO to a heart transplant center for consideration of advanced heart failure therapies due to limited myocardial recovery. No device-related complications were observed. This case demonstrates the technical feasibility of early implementation of a combined VA-ECMO and NyokAssist support strategy, enabling LV unloading, temporary hemodynamic stabilization, end-organ improvement, and VA-ECMO decannulation in a patient with AMI-related CS. As this report describes a single-patient experience, further clinical studies are warranted to define the safety, efficacy, and potential role of the use of NyokAssist in combination with VA-ECMO in the management of AMI-related CS.
