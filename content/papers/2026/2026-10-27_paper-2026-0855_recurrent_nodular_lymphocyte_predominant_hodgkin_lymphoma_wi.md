## Title
Recurrent Nodular Lymphocyte Predominant Hodgkin Lymphoma with Diagnostic Evolution Over Two Decades

## Authors
Atluri Swetha, Schiffman Fred

## Journal / DOI
Rhode Island medical journal (2013) · PMID: 42945516
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42945516/

## Abstract
Nodular lymphocyte predominant Hodgkin lymphoma (NLPHL) is a rare subtype of Hodgkin lymphoma with distinct pathologic and clinical characteristics. We present the case of a 51-year-old man with a nearly 20-year history of NLPHL characterized by multiple relapses, diagnostic reclassification, and treatment de-escalation. This case illustrates the diagnostic challenges in distinguishing NLPHL from lymphocyte-rich classical Hodg- kin lymphoma (LRCHL), the indolent natural history of NLPHL with prolonged remissions, and the role of active surveillance in managing asymptomatic relapsed disease.
