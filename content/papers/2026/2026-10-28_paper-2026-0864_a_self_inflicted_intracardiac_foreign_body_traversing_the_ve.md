## Title
A Self-Inflicted Intracardiac Foreign Body Traversing the Ventricular Septum

## Authors
Hajiali Arezoo, Hali Reza, Mohammadzadeh Saba

## Journal / DOI
CASE (Philadelphia, Pa.) · DOI: 10.1016/j.case.2026.05.008 · PMID: 42948799
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42948799/

## Abstract
• Multimodality imaging is vital for diagnosing complex intracardiac foreign bodies. • Chronic needle injuries may present as masses mimicking thrombus on imaging. • Histopathology is essential to differentiate fibroconnective tissue from thrombi. • Imaging facilitates precise surgical planning for objects traversing the septum. • Collateral history is key when clinical narratives are unreliable or withheld.
