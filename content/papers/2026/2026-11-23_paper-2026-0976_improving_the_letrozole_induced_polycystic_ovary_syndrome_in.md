## Title
Improving the Letrozole Induced Polycystic Ovary Syndrome in Rat Model: Effect of Vitamin D on Hyperandrogenism, Insulin Resistance, Inflammation and Adipokines

## Authors
Korkmaz Gizem Elif, Yaman Mine, Ak Tuba Parlak, Sağıroğlu Meltem, Yaman İhsan, Gençer Berrin Tarakçı

## Journal / DOI
Journal of biochemical and molecular toxicology · DOI: 10.1002/jbt.71206 · PMID: 43068454
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43068454/

## Abstract
Polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS) is a chronic systemic disease progressing. Vitamin D deficiency may contribute to the pathophysiology of PCOS by affecting multiple mechanisms. In this study, the effects of Vit D on altered biochemistry and ovarian histopathology and adipokine immunoreactivities in PCOS rats were investigated. Forty female Sprague Dawley rats were divided into 5 groups as control, Dimethyl sulfoxide (DMSO), Vit D, PCOS and PCOS+ Vit D experimental groups. DMSO group was applied 2% DMSO (1 mL/kg/day, by gavage). Vit D group was applied Vit D (200 IU/day, orally). PCOS group was applied letrozole dissolved in 2% DMSO (1 mg/kg/day, by gavage). PCOS+ Vit D group was applied letrozole and Vit D simultaneously. In the PCOS group, luteinizing hormone/follicle stimulating hormone (LH/FSH) ratio and LH, testosterone, interleukin-1 (IL-1), tumor necrosis factor alpha (TNF-α) and homeostatic model assessment for insulin resistance (HOMA-IR) values increased significantly, unlike the FSH value. In this group, the number of all follicles decreased significantly, while cystic follicles increased. Morever, the granulosa layer in the secondary and tertiary follicles decreased significantly, while the thecal thickness increased. Also it was observed disorganized zona pellucida, degenerated oocytes, thecal and hilar cell hyperplasia. All these changes were significantly improved in the PCOS+ Vit D group. Changes in omentin, visfatin and resistin immunoreactivity in PCOS were ameliorated by Vit D administration. This study determined the ameliorative effects of vitamin D supplementation on the biochemical and histopathological changes and regulation of adipokine distribution in PCOS.
