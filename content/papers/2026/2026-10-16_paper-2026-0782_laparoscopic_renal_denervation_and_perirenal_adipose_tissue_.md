## Title
Laparoscopic Renal Denervation and Perirenal Adipose Tissue Debulking for Refractory Hypertension in End-Stage Renal Disease

## Authors
Chu Jiapeng, Liu Xiaolong, Kuang Long, Gu Hongxing, Xie Wenjun, Ni Zhihang, Shao Chunlai

## Journal / DOI
JACC. Case reports · DOI: 10.1016/j.jaccas.2026.110641 · PMID: 42886134
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42886134/

## Abstract
**BACKGROUND:** Both renal sympathetic nerves and perirenal adipose tissue (PRAT) drive hypertension. However, evidence regarding renal denervation (RDN) in end-stage renal disease (ESRD) remains limited.

**FIRST-IN-HUMAN SUMMARY:** A 74-year-old man with diabetic chronic kidney disease stage 5 presented with refractory hypertension despite treatment with 4 antihypertensive agents and intravenous nitroprusside. He underwent bilateral laparoscopic-assisted RDN combined with PRAT debulking without complications. At 5-month follow-up, office and ambulatory blood pressures were substantially reduced, while renal function and medications remained stable.

**DISCUSSION:** Our case provides preliminary evidence supporting the feasibility of a combined laparoscopic RDN and PRAT debulking strategy in a patient with ESRD and refractory hypertension.

**NOVELTY:** To our knowledge, this is the first report of RDN combined with PRAT debulking using the laparoscopic system in ESRD.

**TAKE-HOME MESSAGE:** This combined intervention offers a promising new therapeutic option for refractory hypertension in patients with advanced kidney disease.
