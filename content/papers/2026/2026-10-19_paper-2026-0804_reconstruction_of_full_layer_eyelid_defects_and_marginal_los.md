## Title
Reconstruction of Full-Layer Eyelid Defects and Marginal Loss With "∏" Flaps Pedicled With Arterial Arch of Palpebral Margin

## Authors
Lu Jianghuiwen, Zhao Yuchen, Liu Xinyuan, Yu Wenyuan

## Journal / DOI
Annals of plastic surgery · DOI: 10.1097/SAP.0000000000004924 · PMID: 42896160
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42896160/

## Abstract
**BACKGROUND:** Surgical ablations of eyelid tumors, trauma, and burns, which encroach on the tarsal plate and eyelid margin, often create full-layer eyelid defects. However, the reconstruction of defects with marginal loss presents a challenge for plastic surgeons. In this report, we present a new modified surgical technique using "∏" flaps depending on the arterial arch of the palpebral margin to reconstruct eyelid defects with minimal loss.

**METHODS:** We reviewed 39 patients who underwent surgery using this technique from 2010 to 2021. "∏" Flaps, which include the tarsal plate, the conjunctiva, the orbicularis muscle, and the skin, were designed on 2 sides of the defect and then advanced horizontally to close the defect.

**RESULTS:** All the flaps survived with satisfactory functional and aesthetic outcomes. One minor complication occurred 1 month after the operation: dry eyes (n=1), which was improved 6 months later.

**CONCLUSION:** This technique can be readily used for moderate and large full-layer defects (1/6 to 1/2 involvement) with marginal loss of the upper/lower eyelid, not exceeding the upper edge of the tarsal plate. By using neighboring tissue, reconstruction can achieve pleasing aesthetic outcomes.

**LEVEL OF EVIDENCE:** Level IV-therapeutic study.
