## Title
Pathologic response after molecularly targeted therapy in thyroid carcinoma: a scoping review and reporting framework

## Authors
Okubo Yoichiro, Yuguchi Shu, Kanno Chiaki, Kobayashi Yuki, Wada Shuka, Kadoya Mei, Toda Soji, Ichikawa Yuichi, et al.

## Journal / DOI
European thyroid journal · DOI: 10.1530/ETJ-26-0329 · PMID: 43077489
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43077489/

## Abstract
**OBJECTIVE:** Molecularly targeted therapy is increasingly used to facilitate resection in advanced thyroid carcinoma, and post-treatment tissue is obtained in several clinical contexts, but pathologic response is described inconsistently and no thyroid-specific regression system is validated. We mapped reported tissue findings and proposed a reporting framework.

**METHODS:** Two executed PubMed searches and a candidate-report registry identified peer-reviewed English full-text reports of primary thyroid carcinoma with histologic sampling of a known carcinoma site or treated tumor bed after targeted therapy, with or without residual carcinoma, bounded at 3 July 2026. Eligibility, report role, source coverage, and qualifying post-treatment histopathologic or ancillary information were assessed separately. A paired comparator or demonstrated treatment-induced change was not required.

**RESULTS:** Of 9,887 unique PubMed records and 11 additional registry candidates, 150 reports were assessed and 124 were eligible: 107 principal and 17 linked reports. Qualifying pathology information was identified in 76 reports (61.3%); 38 (30.6%) had no qualifying information in the reviewed main article and 10 (8.1%) remained unresolved. Among the 76 reports, 19 demonstrated paired change, four reported unchanged comparisons only, and 53 provided post-treatment observations only. Findings were heterogeneous and site-specific.

**CONCLUSIONS:** A literature-informed, non-validated minimum dataset may help site-specific reporting. It should map sampled sites, separate residual tumor from necrosis and stromal remodeling, identify the comparator, state attribution limits, and explain when viability cannot be estimated. Prospective validation is required before use as a response category or surrogate endpoint.
