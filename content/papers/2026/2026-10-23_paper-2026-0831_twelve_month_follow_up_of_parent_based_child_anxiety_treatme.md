## Title
Twelve-month follow-up of parent-based child anxiety treatment

## Authors
Etkin Rebecca G, Shimshoni Yaara, Silverman Wendy K, Lebowitz Eli R

## Journal / DOI
Journal of consulting and clinical psychology · DOI: 10.1037/ccp0001033 · PMID: 42911791
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42911791/

## Abstract
**OBJECTIVE:** Supportive Parenting for Anxious Childhood Emotions (SPACE) is an efficacious parent-based treatment for child anxiety disorders. Little is known about the maintenance of treatment gains following SPACE, and no comparative follow-up studies have been conducted. We address both gaps by examining child anxiety severity up to 12 months following SPACE and cognitive-behavioral therapy (CBT). We also examine whether reductions in family accommodation of child anxiety are maintained through follow-up in both treatments.

**METHOD:** Participants were 125 mother-child dyads enrolled in a randomized controlled noninferiority trial comparing posttreatment outcomes of SPACE and CBT. Parents and children in both treatments completed measures of child anxiety severity and family accommodation at pre-, mid-, and posttreatment and at 3-, 6-, and 12-month follow-ups. Linear mixed models were used to examine effects of time, treatment, and their interaction across all assessment intervals, with particular interest in maintenance at 12-month follow-up.

**RESULTS:** SPACE and CBT both produced significant reductions in parent- and child-rated anxiety severity and family accommodation over time, indicating gains were maintained similarly for both treatments through 12-month follow-up. Parent (but not child) ratings of child anxiety severity were lower for SPACE than CBT at posttreatment and 12-month follow-up.

**CONCLUSIONS:** Results are the first to show maintenance of treatment gains up to 1 year following SPACE, comparable to CBT. These findings may inform clinical decision making and guide the selection of interventions that optimize both short- and longer term outcomes for anxious children and their families.
