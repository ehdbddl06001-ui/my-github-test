## Title
AI-based digital pathology defines spatial border ecosystems in triple-negative breast cancer

## Authors
Zhang Hang, Yang Fan, Tian Pu, Wang Han, Xu Ying, Zhu Xiao-Hui, Li Xue-Pei, Zhao Shen, et al.

## Journal / DOI
Science (New York, N.Y.) · DOI: 10.1126/science.aei6252 · PMID: 43097611
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43097611/

## Abstract
The invasive tumor border in triple-negative breast cancer (TNBC) is currently poorly understood. We integrated digital pathology and spatial multi-omics to define border ecosystems in TNBC. AI-based digital pathology identified five border ecosystems, three associated with prognosis. Spatial multi-omics analyses further resolved the spatial niche, molecular mechanisms and clinical relevance of these ecosystems. Immune-infiltrated borders contain a triadic immune niche associated with favorable prognosis and immunotherapy sensitivity. Tumor-stroma borders harbor a MIF+ fibroblast exclusion niche that blocks immune infiltration and suppresses T cells function, predicting poor prognosis. Tumor-adipose borders, featured an FLT1+ endothelial niche, promote hypoxia, vascular leakage, and tumor dissemination. Targeting this niche inhibits tumor progression. Collectively, these findings establish a spatial resource that might enable border-directed therapeutic strategies in TNBC.
