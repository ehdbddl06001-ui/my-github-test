## Title
Clinical and laboratory-based prognostic factors for pediatric postthrombotic syndrome: findings from the Kids-Duration of Therapy for Thrombosis in Children trial

## Authors
Betensky Marisol, Alleyne Maua M, Amankwah Ernest K, Bhat Rukhmi, Brandão Leonardo R, Bruzek Steven, Kucine Nicole E, Nakano Taizo, et al.

## Journal / DOI
Research and practice in thrombosis and haemostasis · DOI: 10.1016/j.rpth.2026.107035 · PMID: 43068242
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43068242/

## Abstract
**BACKGROUND:** Knowledge on risk factors for the development of pediatric postthrombotic syndrome (PTS) is scarce. We previously identified low plasma fibrinolytic capacity and persistent complete veno-occlusion as candidate risk factors for pediatric PTS. This study aims to substantiate and expand on these findings through a prespecified secondary analysis of the Kids-DOTT trial.

**OBJECTIVE:** To identify additional clinical and molecular prognostic factors for the development of PTS in children presenting with first time provoked deep venous thrombosis (DVT).

**METHODS:** We analyzed clinical data and plasma specimens from children with an extremity DVT in the Kids-DOTT trial. PTS was assessed at 1 year post DVT. Plasma fibrinolytic capacity and biomarkers of fibrinolysis were measured in plasma collected at 6 weeks post DVT. Risk factors, identified in our prior work or substantiated in the literature, were evaluated via univariable logistic regression, and variables with P < .05 were included in a multivariable model.

**RESULTS:** A total of 399 patients were included; 37% developed PTS. In the multivariable clinical model, adolescent age (13 to <21 years) and complete veno-occlusion at 6 weeks were associated with PTS (odds ratio [OR] = 1.69, 95% CI = 1.11-2.57, P = .015 and OR = 2.19, 95% CI = 1.2-3.99, P = .01, respectively). In a biomarker-enhanced model, complete veno-occlusion, reduced plasma fibrinolytic capacity, and increased levels of plasminogen activator inhibitor-1 antigen at 6 weeks were independent prognostic factors for PTS (OR = 2.76, 95% CI = 1.13-6.71, P = .025; OR = 1.83, 95% CI = 1.02-3.30, P = .044; and OR = 1.99, 95% CI = 1.1-3.59, P = .025, respectively).

**CONCLUSION:** We identified complete veno-occlusion, reduced plasma fibrinolytic capacity, and increased plasma levels of plasminogen activator inhibitor-1 at 6 weeks post DVT as independent prognostic factors for the development of PTS in children with provoked DVT.
